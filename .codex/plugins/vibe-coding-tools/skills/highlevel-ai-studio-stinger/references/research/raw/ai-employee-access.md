# **What is the AI Employee?**

The AI Employee includes HighLevel AI products that are managed and billed under the AI Employee offering. AI Employee brings multiple HighLevel AI products together, including Conversation AI, Voice AI, Reviews AI, Content AI, Ask AI, and AI Studio. To learn more about AI Employee products, check out our detailed guide on [**AI Tools in HighLevel**](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000002166).

Some AI features now have free access and no longer use AI credits. See the product-specific pricing article for the latest availability and billing details.

```
Note: For current pricing and scope, review AI Product Pricing
```

## **How to Enable AI Employees for Agency**

To enable "AI Employee" for your agency, check the AI employee toggle in the "Company" tab of your Agency Settings.

1. Click the **A** **gency S** **witcher T** **ab** at the top left corner of the sidebar.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/81tnaGjnwy9YJHOpagqI+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-18+at+18.38.30.png)

2. Click **Switch to Agency View** tab.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/d2p6ObrhmJGlxR9pNkxi+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-18+at+18.41.20.png)

3. Go to **Settings** tab at the bottom left corner of the sidebar.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/P2eLeCoZuyXnko9pXH75+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-18+at+18.44.38.png)

4. Click on the **Company** tab to get into the company menu. Once you are inside the company menu, scroll down until you see the **AI Employee** feature. Enable it using the toggle.

5. There you have enabled the AI Employee. Now you can customize the offering for individual sub accounts based on your clients usage and requirements.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/KVBqcnWNBE0iN2E5uMHA+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-18+at+18.46.24.png)

## **How to Grant or Revoke AI Employee Access for a Sub-Account**

Controlling AI Employee access at the sub-account level lets you decide which clients can use your agency's AI offerings. Agency-level controls make it easy to find a specific sub-account and grant or revoke access without switching into the client's account.

1. Log in to HighLevel in **Agency View**.

2. Click **AI Suite** in the left navigation menu.

3. Open **Plans & Limits**.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/KHsaqoKTZX4CJ28K3scK+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+16.47.56.png)

4. Scroll to the **list of sub-accounts**.

5. Locate the sub-account you want to manage. You can search using the **sub-account name or sub-account ID** and use the available filters to narrow the list.

6. Locate the **AI Agents** control for the sub-account.

7. Turn the control **on** to grant access or **off** to revoke access.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/9dWoXPvS4MWQVyQDBKXA+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+16.49.52.png)


### **Alternative method**

You can also manage access from the actions menu for the sub-account:

1. Click the **three-dot menu** on the right side of the sub-account.

2. Select **AI Agents**.

3. **Enable** or **disable** AI Employee access as needed.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/Pj0yJ1CLhJkvqsNiRVnv+/Screenshot+2026-08-20+at+16.52.20.png)

## **How to Configure AI Employee Rebilling for a Sub-Account**

Rebilling allows agencies to pass AI usage costs to eligible clients while applying a markup based on their pricing strategy. Configuring rebilling at the individual sub-account level gives you control over how that client's supported AI usage is billed.

```
Prerequisite: Make sure the applicable SaaS billing setup is active for the sub-account before configuring direct client billing. If the sub-account is not currently configured for SaaS billing, complete that setup before continuing.
```

1. From **Agency View**, go to **AI Suite > Plans & Limits**.



![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078894394/original/RyqvYJ02jFsE8d6NQhWItHFejrQ11nJKVA.jpeg?1787225181)

2. Find the sub-account for which you want to configure rebilling.

3. Click the **three-dot menu** beside the sub-account.

4. Select **Configure Rebilling**.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/rKdDadGB93hQjjZuxL4U+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+16.56.47.png)

5. In the **rebilling window**, review the **available AI Employee products**:




   - Conversation AI
   - Voice AI
   - Reviews AI
   - Content AI
   - Ask AI
   - AI Studio



     ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/5YmyoYRzlDZUEvQ1z95g+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+16.59.42.png)

6. Choose whether to use the **same markup for all supported products**.




   - Enable this option when you want to apply a consistent markup.

   - Disable it when you want to configure supported products individually.



     ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/g1G8KyA0ECYzHE4Q8v5H+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+17.01.07.png)
7. **Enable** **rebilling** for the **AI** **products** you want to charge the client for.

8. **Configure** the markup or billing option displayed for each product.

9. Review the configured rates.

10. Click **Save Markups** to save and activate the rebilling configuration.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/xhbW3WX2yToVEFqNTwRC+/GIF+Recording+2026-08-20+at+17.02.59.gif)


```
Rebilling vs. reselling: Rebilling is designed to charge clients based on applicable AI usage. If you want to offer AI Employee as a recurring client subscription instead, configure reselling.
```

## **How to Resell AI Employee Plans**

Reselling lets your agency package AI Employee as a client-facing subscription rather than relying exclusively on pay-per-use billing. The process has two parts: first configure your agency's customer-facing offer, then activate the appropriate offer for the individual sub-account.

```
Prerequisite: Confirm that the client's applicable SaaS billing setup is active before activating a paid AI Employee resale offer for the sub-account.
```

### **Configure Your AI Employee Reselling Price**

Your agency's reselling configuration determines the price clients will see when an AI Employee offer is made available to them. This allows you to account for your agency cost and define the margin you want to earn from the subscription.

```
Pricing: AI Employee pricing and included usage can change. Refer to the AI Product Pricing article and the pricing displayed in your HighLevel account for current plan costs and inclusions.
```

1. In **Agency View**, click **Reselling** in the left navigation menu.

2. Under **Subscriptions**, select **AI Employee**.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/SPiV1jAVmMuTlf8AJ4ga+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+20.20.01.png)

3. **Review** the available **AI Employee** **plans** and the **pricing**.

4. **Select** the **plan** you want **to configure**.

5. **Enter** the **price** you **want** **your** **customer** to **pay**.

6. **Configure** any **additional** **usage**- **based** **markup** options available for the selected plan.

7. Review the **displayed** **agency** **cost**, **customer** **price**, and resulting **profit**.

8. Click **Save Changes**.



![](https://jumpshare.com/share/SPiV1jAVmMuTlf8AJ4ga+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+20.20.01.png)


### **Activate the AI Employee Reselling Offer for a Sub-Account(s)**

Activating reselling for an individual sub-account determines which configured AI Employee offer that client can purchase. This step connects your agency-level pricing configuration to the selected client's account.

01. In **Agency View**, click **Sub-Accounts**.

02. Locate the sub-account where you want to activate the AI Employee offer.

03. Click the **three-dot menu** beside the sub-account.

04. Select **Manage Client**.



    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/h6f21P2MDvQCD8sxm3Bz+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+20.28.22.png)

05. Confirm that the appropriate SaaS billing setup is active for the client.

06. Open the **Reselling** tab.

07. Select **AI Employee** from the available reselling products.



    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/X4pwr1fU5AWnMIddaGSv+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-20+at+20.30.38.png)

08. **Choose** the **AI Employee offer**you want to make available to the client.

09. **Review** the **customer-facing price**.

10. Click **Save**.



    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/4juRLW8wLdq6insrEAfz+/GIF+Recording+2026-08-20+at+20.33.27.gif)

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: What is the AI Employee in HighLevel?**

The AI Employee is a collection of powerful AI tools designed to help businesses automate and streamline operations. It includes features like Voice AI, Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Funnel & Website AI and Content AI.

**Q: Does the AI Employee cover Phone/SMS charges?**

No, phone and SMS charges are separately billed through LC Phone or Twilio.

**Q: Can I control which sub-accounts have access to the AI Employee?**

Yes, you can enable or disable the AI Employee for each sub-account individually through your Agency Settings.

**Q: Is it possible to re-bill clients for AI Employee usage?**

Absolutely! You can set up custom rebilling rates for each sub-account, allowing you to monetize the AI tools as part of your service offering.

**Q: How much does the AI Employee cost?**

AI Employee pricing is available through Pay-Per-Use, AI Employee Growth, and AI Employee Unlimited options. Review the [AI Product Pricing](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006652) article for the latest plan details, included usage, pay-per-use rates, and fair-use terms.
