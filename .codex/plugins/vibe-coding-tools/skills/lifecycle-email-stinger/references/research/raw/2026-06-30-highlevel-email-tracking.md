## Captured evidence

HighLevel documents open and click tracking for LC Email dedicated sending domains. The page states that this tracking feature is not available for Mailgun, Gmail, or other providers through the same setting. Tracking is configured per dedicated domain.

The source presents opens and clicks as engagement diagnostics that can help compare content and prompt follow-up. It does not claim that every recorded open or click represents a human action.

## Transfer limits

- Platform availability depends on the sending provider and account configuration.
- Open and click data should remain secondary diagnostics. Replies and downstream outcomes are stronger lifecycle measurements.
- The source does not validate any specific automation cadence or performance benchmark.
