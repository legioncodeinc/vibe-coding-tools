# SR-052: A2P registration reuse across unrelated clients is requested

## Use when

Use this response when a customer asks to reuse one messaging brand, campaign, or number for separate client businesses.

## Required evidence

- Identity and account for each client business
- Messaging use case and consent path for each business
- Current brand, campaign, and number identifiers
- Current registration and traffic state for each client
- Reason the shared design is being requested
- Proposed relationship among the businesses, campaigns, and numbers

## Customer-facing email

**Subject:** {ticket_id}: Review required for cross-client messaging registration

Hi {customer_first_name},

I understand you are considering one messaging registration or number across multiple client businesses. We have not confirmed that the proposed design is permitted or that it preserves the required identity and consent boundaries.

Please leave each client's existing registration and traffic unchanged. In one reply, send:

1. The business identity and account for each client.
2. Each client's use case, consent path, brand and campaign identifiers, and assigned numbers.
3. The reason reuse is requested and the exact shared design you want reviewed.

Please redact unrelated customer information. Do not send passwords, verification codes, tokens, or unrestricted contact exports.

{agency} will have our messaging compliance authority review the design. We will not recommend a bypass or state that one registration can represent unrelated businesses while that decision is unresolved. I will update you after the compliance review is complete.

{agent_name}
{agency} Support

## Agent notes

- Evidence state: `UNRESOLVED`.
- Keep every client's current registration and traffic unchanged during review.
- Escalate the completed cross-client design to messaging compliance.
- Do not promise approval, advise registration reuse, or suggest number sharing as a cost workaround.
- Readiness is `DRAFT_WITH_GAPS` until each business identity, consent path, current registration, and approving authority are known.
