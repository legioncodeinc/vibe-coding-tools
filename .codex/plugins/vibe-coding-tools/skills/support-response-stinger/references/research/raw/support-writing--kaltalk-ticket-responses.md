## Captured findings

The article treats first reply, information request, status update, escalation, and closing as different jobs. It recommends templates as editable starting points, not unmodified final answers.

The component adopts that lifecycle distinction and adds stricter evidence rules: never claim a test, fix, escalation, refund, or ETA that the ticket record does not prove.
