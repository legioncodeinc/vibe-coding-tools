## Captured facts

Some scheduled Social Planner posts remained in the "In Progress" state and did not complete. The provider later reported restored operation.

## Safety caveat

Check the destination platform and the original post status before cloning, rescheduling, or publishing again. A delayed status can produce a duplicate post if the destination already accepted the original.
