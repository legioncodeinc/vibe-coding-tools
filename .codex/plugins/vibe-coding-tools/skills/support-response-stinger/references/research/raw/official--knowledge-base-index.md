## Captured evidence

The index organizes support content into broad operational surfaces. The largest or most support-relevant sections observed were phone and messaging, email, contacts, conversations, marketing, opportunities and pipelines, sites, workflows, calendars and appointments, payments, agency administration, and SaaS configuration.

Dedicated troubleshooting collections were visible for messaging, A2P registration, email, email delivery, funnels, workflows, and calendars. The index also exposes setup collections for forms, payment flows, contact imports, calendar integrations, domains, snapshots, and SaaS sub-accounts.

The collection sizes show breadth, not frequency. Examples captured on 2026-09-04 included 29 messaging articles, 14 A2P articles, 7 general email troubleshooting articles, 8 email delivery troubleshooting groupings, 11 calendar troubleshooting articles, 9 funnel troubleshooting articles, 43 form articles, 59 workflow builder articles, 29 conversations articles, and 114 funnel and website articles.

The catalog uses this index to prevent a Reddit-only ranking from omitting ordinary setup questions that rarely become public posts.
