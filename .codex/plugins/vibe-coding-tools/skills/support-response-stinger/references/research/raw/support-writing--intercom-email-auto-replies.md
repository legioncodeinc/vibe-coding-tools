## Captured findings

The source documents a customizable immediate acknowledgment for new inbound email conversations. Its operational purpose is to confirm receipt and set a response-time expectation.

The component keeps acknowledgment promises separate from resolution promises. `{next_update_at}` may be stated only when the team can meet it. If an SLA or working-hours rule is not supplied, the generated email says that the case is queued and avoids inventing a time.
