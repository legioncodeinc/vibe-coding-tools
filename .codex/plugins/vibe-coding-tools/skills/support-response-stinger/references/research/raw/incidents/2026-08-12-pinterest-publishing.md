# Social Planner - Pinterest posting is partially impacted

## Captured facts

An upstream Pinterest API issue caused some Pinterest posts to fail while other social platforms remained operational. The incident advised retrying failed Pinterest posts after recovery.

## Safety caveat

Confirm the original post status before retrying. Do not republish a post that may already be live, and do not reconnect unrelated social accounts when only Pinterest is affected.
