## Captured facts

A funnel that does not load requires checks for custom-domain verification, DNS propagation, and assignment to the intended funnel. A form that does not collect leads requires confirming that the form is present, running a controlled test, and reviewing any dependent workflow or automation.

## Safety caveat

Use a test submission that cannot send live campaigns, create charges, or notify real customers. Do not replace a funnel or form until its existing version, submissions, and automation dependencies are preserved.
