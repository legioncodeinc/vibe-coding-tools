## Captured facts

Opportunities may be created manually, imported, or created by workflows. For a missing or unmoved opportunity, check that the workflow is published, the contact met its trigger, the create or update action is present, the intended pipeline and stage are selected, and the setting that permits stage movement is correct.

## Safety caveat

Inspect the existing contact and opportunity before retrying. Creating a replacement or replaying a workflow can duplicate an opportunity or update the wrong deal when a contact has more than one.
