## Captured facts

Spam-like content, unsolicited recipients, poor list health, sender reputation, authentication, and sending patterns can cause filtering or blocks. The article recommends identifying affected content and recipients, reducing sends to engaged contacts, testing variations, validating authentication, monitoring reputation, and allowing a gradual recovery period.

## Safety caveat

Do not promise immediate inbox placement or a guaranteed recovery time. Do not weaken DMARC, replace a sending domain, or purchase a dedicated IP without ownership confirmation and deliverability review. Avoid resending the same blocked campaign to the same list.
