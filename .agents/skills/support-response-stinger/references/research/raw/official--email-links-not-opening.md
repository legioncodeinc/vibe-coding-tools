## Captured facts

Tracked links are rewritten through a tracking domain. A broken or unresolved tracking-domain DNS record, SSL problem, or subdomain mismatch can stop the redirect before the destination page loads.

The safe first response asks for one affected message, the visible destination, timestamp, browser result, and whether every recipient is affected. DNS changes require an authorized owner and the exact current record. Do not ask the customer to delete records speculatively.
