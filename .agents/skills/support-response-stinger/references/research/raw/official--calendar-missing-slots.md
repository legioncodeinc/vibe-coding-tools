## Captured facts

Documented causes include editing the wrong calendar, working hours and date overrides, no assigned team member, service duration and interval combinations, buffers, slot-capacity limits, busy events on connected calendars, timezone differences, and browser cache.

A support reply should ask for the public booking link, affected date and time, expected timezone, calendar name, assigned user, screenshot, and whether the slot is blocked in the built-in troubleshooting view. It should not promise that reconnecting the external calendar will help until integration status shows a failure.
