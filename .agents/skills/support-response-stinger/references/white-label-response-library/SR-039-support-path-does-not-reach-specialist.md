# SR-039: The support path does not reach a specialist

## Use when

Use when a customer has repeated the same steps, supplied evidence, or waited for a promised specialist without meaningful progress.

## Required evidence

- Ticket identifier, workspace, original symptom, business impact, timeline, evidence already supplied, repeated steps, any promised review or appointment, and the smallest accessible redacted sample.

## Customer-facing email

**Subject:** {ticket_id}: Consolidating your case for specialist review

Hi {customer_first_name},

You should not have to repeat the same troubleshooting or rebuild the case history. I am consolidating the verified details so {agency} can move the issue forward with one accountable owner.

Please reply with the original symptom, current business impact, first and most recent occurrence with timezone, evidence already supplied, steps you were asked to repeat, and any promised review or appointment. Include only the smallest accessible screenshot or redacted sample needed to show the issue. Do not send passwords, tokens, full datasets, or unrestricted recordings.

I will compare your reply with the existing ticket and prepare one internal escalation packet. I will confirm the assigned owner and next update only after that review is actually scheduled. I will not invent an escalation status, service commitment, or update time.

{agent_name}
{agency} Support

## Agent notes

Evidence class `F2`. Remediation class `UNRESOLVED`. Assign a named `{agency}` owner and consolidate existing evidence. Escalate only through verified internal routing. Never claim an external escalation, service commitment, or scheduled update without proof.
