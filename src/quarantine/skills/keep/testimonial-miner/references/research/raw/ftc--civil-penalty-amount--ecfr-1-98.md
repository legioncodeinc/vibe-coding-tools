**Source note on the current amounts:** 90 FR 5581, Jan. 17, 2025. The amounts apply to
violations assessed after January 17, 2025.

## Why this section is the right citation

Section 5(m)(1)(A) is the provision under which the FTC may seek civil penalties for a violation
of a rule respecting unfair or deceptive acts or practices. The Consumer Reviews and Testimonials
Rule at 16 CFR Part 465 is such a rule. The Endorsement Guides at Part 255 are not a rule and
carry no direct penalty of their own.

## Caveat recorded deliberately

This figure is inflation-adjusted and moves. As fetched on 2026-08-17 the eCFR still showed the
January 2025 amount of $53,088 as current. A search of Federal Register notices on the same day
surfaced both a "Civil Monetary Penalties, 2026 Adjustment" notice and a "No Adjustment to Civil
Monetary Penalty Amounts" notice from other agencies, neither confirmed as the FTC's own. **Any
skill quoting this number must state the figure as of a date and tell the user to re-check
16 CFR 1.98, rather than presenting it as permanently current.**

The penalty is stated per violation. Nothing in this source defines what counts as one violation
in a review or testimonial context, and this archive contains no source that does. That is a
named gap.
