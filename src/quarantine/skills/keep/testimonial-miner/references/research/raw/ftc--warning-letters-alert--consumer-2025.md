# FTC warns businesses about fake reviews (consumer alert)

- **Title:** FTC warns businesses about fake reviews (FTC Consumer Advice alert)
- **URL:** https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/12/ftc-warns-businesses-about-fake-reviews
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (FTC consumer alert)

## Facts

- **Date:** December 22, 2025
- **Action:** the FTC sent warning letters to **10 companies**
- **Conduct named:** "creating, buying, or posting fake reviews, or giving incentives for only
  positive reviews"
- **Consequence stated:** these practices "may trigger enforcement actions and fines"
- **Reporting channels the FTC pointed consumers to:** ReportFraud.ftc.gov, plus the platform
  where the review appears (Google, Amazon, Walmart and similar)

## Why it is archived

It is the FTC's own confirmation that the warning-letter campaign happened and how many letters
went out, which anchors the law-firm analysis in
`enforcement--reviews-rule-2026--dlapiper-2026.md` to a primary source. The alert itself does not
name the recipients or the industries. That detail comes from the law-firm source and is
attributed there.

Note the phrase "giving incentives for only positive reviews." Incentives alone are not the
violation. Conditioning on sentiment is.
