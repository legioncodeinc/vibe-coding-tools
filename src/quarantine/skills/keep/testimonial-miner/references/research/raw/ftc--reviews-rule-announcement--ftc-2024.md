# FTC Announces Final Rule Banning Fake Reviews and Testimonials

- **Title:** Federal Trade Commission Announces Final Rule Banning Fake Reviews and Testimonials
- **URL:** https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (FTC press release)

## Facts

- **Announcement date:** August 14, 2024
- **Vote:** 5 to 0, unanimous
- **Effective date as stated here:** 60 days after publication in the Federal Register. Combined
  with the 2024-08-22 publication date in the codified source note, that lands on the
  October 21, 2024 date the FTC states directly in its Q and A page.
- **Civil penalties:** the release says the rule "will enhance deterrence and strengthen FTC
  enforcement actions" by allowing the agency to "seek civil penalties against knowing
  violators." No dollar figure is given here.

## What the rule prohibits, as the FTC summarized it

- Fake or false consumer reviews, consumer testimonials, and celebrity testimonials, including
  AI-generated fake reviews
- Buying positive or negative reviews conditioned on a particular sentiment
- Undisclosed insider reviews and testimonials by company officers or managers
- Misrepresenting company-controlled websites as independent review sources
- Review suppression through threats, intimidation, or false accusations
- Selling or buying fake social media indicators such as followers and views when representing
  commercial influence

## Note on the AI item

The inclusion of AI-generated fake reviews in the prohibited list is directly relevant to a skill
that uses a language model to process praise. A model that rewrites, improves, smooths or
synthesizes a customer quote is generating text and attributing it to a named human. That lands
squarely in the first bullet.
