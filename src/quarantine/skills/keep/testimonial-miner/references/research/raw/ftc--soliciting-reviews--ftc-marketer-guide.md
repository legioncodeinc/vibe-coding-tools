# Soliciting and Paying for Online Reviews: A Guide for Marketers

- **Title:** Soliciting and Paying for Online Reviews: A Guide for Marketers (FTC business
  guidance)
- **URL:** https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/soliciting-paying-online-reviews-guide-marketers
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (FTC staff business guidance)

## Rules on who you ask

- "Don't ask for reviews from people who haven't used or experienced the product or service."
- "Don't ask your staff to write reviews of your business, at least not without ensuring that
  they disclose in their review that you employ them and asked them to write it."
- "Don't ask for reviews only from customers you think will leave positive ones."
- "Don't ask family and friends for reviews, at least not without ensuring that they disclose
  their personal connection in the reviews."

The third bullet is the review-gating prohibition and it is the one most likely to be broken by
accident. Asking for reviews only from happy customers is called out directly.

## Rules on incentives

- "If you offer an incentive for a review, don't condition it, explicitly or implicitly, on the
  review being positive."
- Where an incentive was given, "the review should disclose the incentive, because its offer may
  introduce bias or change the weight and credibility that readers give the review."

## Rules on third parties

- Avoid "pay-to-play operations, offering better ratings, reviews, and placement in exchange for
  a fee."
- A platform must "clearly disclose its commercial relationship with you."
- Do not misuse platform reporting mechanisms to "get rid of honest, negative reviews."
- On agencies and contractors: "you can be held responsible for what they do on your behalf."

## Relevance to this skill

This guide governs the permission-request drafts the skill produces. A permission request that
implies the business only wants to hear from customers who loved the work, or that dangles a
benefit contingent on the answer being flattering, converts a clean ask into the exact conduct
this guide prohibits. The skill's drafted requests must be sentiment-neutral and must not offer
a conditional benefit.
