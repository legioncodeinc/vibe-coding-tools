## Extracted content

**Method.** Start from a 60-minute webinar recording, extract key insights, quotes, and
timestamps, then generate multiple formats. The source claims roughly 28 pieces from one
webinar: 5 video clips, 3 carousels, 12 text posts, 8 image quotes.

**Moment types to extract.**

- Expert soundbites, 30 to 90 seconds
- Surprising statistics or data points
- Strong opinions or industry takes
- Memorable quotes from speakers
- Customer questions and discussion prompts

**Format guidance.**

- Document carousels (PDF): "stop the scroll", good for step-by-step guides or data
  breakdowns.
- Native video posted directly to LinkedIn rather than as a YouTube link, framed around
  trust-building rather than virality.
- Text posts: open with a compelling first line that earns the "...see more" click, then
  short paragraphs and lists.

**Recommended opening hooks.** Ask targeted questions, share opinions, tell specific
stories.
