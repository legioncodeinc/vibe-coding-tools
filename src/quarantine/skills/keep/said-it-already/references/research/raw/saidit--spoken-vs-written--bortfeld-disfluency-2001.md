- **Published in:** Language and Speech, 44(2), 2001, pages 123 to 147
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** academic

## Window note

From 2001, well outside the default window. Archived because it is the standard measured
baseline for how disfluent ordinary conversation is, and because no recent source found
in this sweep measures the same thing. Speech production rates are stable literature.

## Extracted content

**Headline rate.** "Speakers produced, on average, 5.97 disfluencies every 100 words."
The authors also note in a footnote that "about 6% of words in spontaneous speech are
disfluent."

**What was counted.** Three categories: "Repeats (just on the left left side), restarts
(e.g., imme- just below the left side), fillers (e.g., uh, ah, um, er)."

**By task difficulty.**

| Domain | Disfluencies per 100 words |
|---|---|
| Tangrams (abstract, hard to describe) | 6.37 |
| Pictures of children (familiar, easy) | 5.55 |

**By speaking role.**

| Role | Disfluencies per 100 words |
|---|---|
| Director (doing the explaining) | 7.00 |
| Matcher (receiving) | 4.93 |

**By speaker age.** Older speakers aged 63 to 72 ran 6.65, middle-aged 5.69, young 5.55.

**By gender.** Men 6.80, women 5.12.

## Claims this source supports

1. Roughly 6 disfluencies per 100 words is the normal texture of unrehearsed speech. A
   500-word transcript passage carries on the order of 30 of them. This is not a sign of
   a bad speaker, it is the baseline.
2. The person doing the explaining is measurably MORE disfluent than the person
   listening: 7.00 versus 4.93 per 100 words. The teaching explanation, which is one of
   this skill's seed types, is therefore the passage most likely to look rough on the
   page and most in need of a rebuild rather than a paste.
3. Harder and more abstract material produces more disfluency (6.37 versus 5.55). The
   most valuable seeds, where the user is working out something difficult out loud, are
   also the messiest verbatim.
4. Any workflow that pastes transcript into a post is publishing roughly 6% noise words
   plus restarts, before considering structure.
