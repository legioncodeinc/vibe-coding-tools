## Extracted content

**The five-step workflow.**

1. Transcription and asset mining. Generate transcripts, then timestamp standout moments.
2. Short-form video clipping.
3. SEO blog posts: restructure the transcript around buyer queries, add subheadings,
   remove conversational filler, add visuals and takeaways.
4. Email sequences: extract three to five standalone insights per episode.
5. Sales enablement: package frameworks, statistics, and quotes.

**What to look for in a transcript.** The source names five moment types:

- Strong claims or counterintuitive insights
- Data points and statistics
- Visible guest reactions
- Process explanations and frameworks
- Moments where listeners replay content, verified through platform retention analytics

**Working method.** "Do not re-listen to the full episode when producing derivative
content. The timestamped transcript is your map."

**Claimed asset volume per episode.**

| Team size | Assets per episode |
|---|---|
| Lean SMB, 1 to 2 people | roughly 15 |
| Mid-market, 3 to 5 people | 30 to 50 |
| Enterprise | 60 to 100 plus |

**Named failure modes.**

1. Transcript dumps. Verbatim transcription published as a blog post. The source insists
   output must be "editorial content that earns compounding search traffic."
2. One-size-fits-all distribution. "Distributing the same asset across every platform
   without adapting it is the primary reason repurposed content underperforms."
3. Skipping sales enablement, described as "the most frequently skipped asset type."
4. No human editorial review. AI tools "should not replace editorial judgment", and the
   source stresses verifying that "no AI-generated clip strips context that changes the
   meaning of a quote."
5. Missing platform optimization.

**Editing guidance for spoken to written.**

- Remove conversational filler before converting.
- Add subheadings.
- Close with three to five key takeaways.
- Use one strong transcript sentence as a headline.
- For clips: "Lead with the strongest or most provocative line. Never open with a
  context-setting intro."

**Externally attributed data cited by the source.** Edison Research 2025: 55% of
Americans listen to podcasts monthly. eMarketer: 26% of podcast listeners visit brand
sites after hearing ads. SEMrush: content repurposing ranks fifth among effective content
