## Source-quality note

This is the best-sourced vendor piece found in the sweep and is archived partly as a
contrast case: it shows what citation looks like in this domain when someone bothers. It
cites named academic papers with links and named platform documentation. It also clearly
labels its own framework as unvalidated. Separate the cited layer from the asserted layer,
which this file does below.

**Platform-sourced timing claims.** "State the proposition within three seconds and
prioritize the hook within six" (TikTok advertising guidance). TikTok reported showing
people in the first 2 seconds increased hooking power 1.5 times versus introducing them
later. TikTok Symphony Creative Studio: 90% of ad recall happens within six seconds. A
YouTube Shorts deep dive with creator Jenny Hoyos: "One second to hook someone, especially
on Shorts."

## The asserted layer (no source)

The article itself flags its central model as unvalidated: hook strength as "relevance x
clarity x tension x credibility" is "a planning model, not a validated scientific
equation." Also unsourced: the four-stage hook process (recognition, tension, proof,
reward), the claim "surprise can capture the eyes, but relevance keeps the viewer", the
12-video field test method, and all of the service-business example hooks.

## Claims this source supports

1. Surprise is a measured driver of visual attention (Itti and Baldi, 72% of gaze shifts).
   A hook built on a genuinely surprising line has an evidenced basis. A hook built on a
   template does not.
2. Curiosity works through an information GAP (Loewenstein). The mechanism behind a good
   hook is naming something the reader realizes they do not know, which is a different
   operation from withholding the answer as a tease.
3. Attention capacity is limited (Lang), which is the mechanistic argument for short,
   front-loaded openers rather than context-setting preambles.
4. Timing claims for video come from platform advertising guidance, which is
   self-interested and about ADS. Do not transfer them to text posts.
5. **Evidence pattern for the whole domain.** Even the best-cited vendor piece in this
   archive rests real psychology under an invented framework and says so. Hook formula
   lists are craft heuristics. The underlying attention and curiosity findings are the
   only part that is research.
