## Why it matters for this skill

The 73% irrelevant-outreach avoidance figure and the 71% frustrating-experience figure
are the commercial case for a brief that is specific rather than generic. The 83% and
81% figures say the buyer arrives at the call already informed, which means the seller's
only remaining edge is knowing THIS buyer and THIS history rather than knowing the
product.

## Limits of this source

Aggregator post. The underlying Gartner, Forrester, SBI and 6Sense reports are gated and
were not independently verified. Each stat is reported here with the attribution the
aggregator gave it. The page contains no statistics specifically on seller preparation
levels or objection handling, which is a gap.
