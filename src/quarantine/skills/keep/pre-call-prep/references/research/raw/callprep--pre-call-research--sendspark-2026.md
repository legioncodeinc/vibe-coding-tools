# Sales Call Preparation: B2B Pre-Call Playbook

- **URL:** https://blog.sendspark.com/sales-call-preparation
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (Sendspark)

## Four step pre-call research framework

1. **Company background.** Website, LinkedIn company page, press releases. For public
   companies, scan earnings call summaries.
2. **Stakeholder mapping.** Identify decision makers across the buying committee. The
   page states "B2B deals involve an average of 6-10 decision-makers, according to
   Garter [sic]", meaning Gartner. Check LinkedIn for reporting structure and CRM for
   other contacts.
3. **Trigger events.** Recent changes such as funding rounds, leadership shifts, product
   launches. Quoted: "Reps who reference a trigger event in their first call see 2.2x
   higher connection rates" (attributed to HubSpot).
4. **Prior engagement history.** Review CRM for website visits, email opens, video
   engagement. Quoted: "67% of lost deals have prior engagement history that was never
   reviewed" (attributed to Salesforce).

## Prep time

Stated optimum: 15 to 20 minutes.

> "Reps who prepare for 15 minutes or more before a call win 50% more deals" (attributed
> to Gong, 2024)

> "62% of B2B reps spend less than 10 minutes" preparing (attributed to Salesforce)

## Call stage differences

- Initial calls: discovery objectives, understanding situation and pain points.
- Qualification calls: validate fit against BANT or MEDDIC criteria.

## Pre-call checklist with time budget as published

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Review company website and LinkedIn | 5 min |
| Map stakeholders and reporting structure | 3 min |
| Check trigger events | 3 min |
| Review CRM history | 2 min |
| Write 3-5 objectives and 8-12 questions | 4 min |
| Send personalized video warm-up | 2 min |

## Why it matters for this skill

The "67% of lost deals have prior engagement history that was never reviewed" claim is
the single closest published statement of the problem this skill solves. The trigger
event step is the source for the "what changed on their side" section. The 15 to 20
minute budget is what the brief has to compress into a 90 second read.

## Limits of this source

Vendor blog with a product placement in the checklist (the final row sells the vendor's
own product). Statistics are attributed to Gong, Salesforce, HubSpot and Gartner but no
report titles or URLs are given, so none were independently verified. The page misspells
Gartner. Treat every number here as an unverified secondhand vendor claim.
