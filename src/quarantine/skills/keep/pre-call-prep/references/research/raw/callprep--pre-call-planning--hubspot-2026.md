# Pre-call planning: A comprehensive guide to effective sales calls

- **URL:** https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/pre-call-planning
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (HubSpot)

## The seven step pre-call planning framework, step names quoted

1. "Conduct in-depth research of your prospect's business landscape" (industry trends,
   competitive dynamics, financial performance, technology infrastructure)
2. "Know who you're going to be talking to" (role, tenure, career progression,
   professional interests)
3. "Explore mutual connections in your professional circles"
4. "Establish firm goals for the call" (specific, measurable outcomes)
5. "Ensure all necessary support materials are ready"
6. "Prepare insightful questions"
7. "Anticipate potential objections and queries"

## Research requirements as stated

**Company:** industry trends, competitive landscape, financial performance, technology
stack, recent strategic initiatives.

**Individual:** role, responsibilities, tenure, career progression, previous employers,
published content, social media activity.

## Prior conversation review

The article recommends reviewing "previous interaction key points and agreements",
analyzing which topics generated interest, noting repeated objections, and confirming
decision timeline status.

## Preparation timeline

Two phases: thorough research

> "24 to 48 hours before the call"

and a final review

> "15 minutes immediately before dialing."

## Why it matters for this skill

Two things map directly onto the brief design. First, the two phase timeline (deep prep
the day before, 15 minute refresh right before) is exactly the routine-plus-on-demand
split this skill implements. Second, step 7 plus the "noting repeated objections"
guidance is the source for the objections section of the brief.

## Limits of this source

Vendor blog. The three statistics are presented without attribution on the page and could
not be traced to a primary study. Treat them as unverified vendor claims, not facts. Do
not restate them as established numbers in a shipped brief.
