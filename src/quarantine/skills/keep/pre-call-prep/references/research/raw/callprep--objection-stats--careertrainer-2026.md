## What is missing

The page carries no statistics on objection types, on how often objections recur across
calls, or on preparing for known objections in advance. It is the second source checked
for objection recurrence data and the second to come up empty.

## Why it matters for this skill

Only one figure is usable and only weakly: "80% of sales require 5 follow-up calls after
the meeting" supports the premise that most commercial calls are continuing conversations
rather than first meetings, which is why the continuing-relationship brief shape is the
default rather than the exception.

## Limits of this source

Statistics aggregator with self referential attribution. Every figure is credited to the
site's own report rather than to a primary study, and several are widely circulated
industry folklore numbers of unclear origin. Low trust source. Nothing here should be
restated as a fact in a shipped brief. Archived to document the search for objection
recurrence data and its failure.
