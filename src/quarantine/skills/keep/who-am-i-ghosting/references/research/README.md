# Research archive for who-am-i-ghosting

Domain: responsiveness, how silence is read, communication triage, and reviving a stalled
professional thread. Swept 2026-08-17 with web search and direct page fetches.

## How to use this folder

Read `distilled-responsiveness-and-reengagement.md` first. It is the only file the skill's
guides cite directly, and every claim in it ends in a bracketed pointer to a file in
`raw/`. If a domain claim appears anywhere in this skill without a trail through the
distillation to a raw file, that is a defect.

## Source count

Thirteen raw sources. Nine academic, one practitioner press, one press column, and two
vendor or community pages retained as explicit negative findings. One of the nine academic
sources is archived for weak relevance, to document a gap rather than to support a claim.
The contract minimum is five and the assignment minimum is six.

## Named gaps

Three, all stated in the distillation rather than papered over:

1. **Does apologizing for the delay help or hurt.** No located study answers it for
   person-to-person correspondence. The skill's no-apology-opener rule is stated as a
   defensible default resting on practitioner guidance plus the urgency-bias finding, not
   as a proven optimum. Section 9.
2. **How long a professional relationship survives without contact.** No located study
   measures it for professional ties. The nearest evidence is a 25 person longitudinal
   panel of non-professional ties. Section 10.
3. **When to stop following up.** The entire recent-window literature is vendor content
   marketing citing other vendor content marketing, on the wrong population and in the
   wrong direction. No follow-up count threshold is quoted anywhere in this skill.
   Section 11.

## Window

Default window was the last six months. Ten of thirteen sources sit outside it and each
says so in its own header. The reason is uniform: the in-window material on this domain is
almost entirely marketing content with blog-to-blog citation chains, while the measured
work on reply behavior, silence interpretation, relationship decay, and reconnection was
done earlier and nothing located supersedes it. All three in-window sources archived are
low trust and all three are labelled as such.

## Two full texts that refused automated fetch

- Giurge and Bohns 2021, blocked at both ScienceDirect and the LSE eprint PDF. The archive
  holds the Cornell eCommons abstract-level record: eight pre-registered studies, N = 4,004,
  direction of effect, no magnitudes. Do not quote an effect size from it.
- Roberts and Dunbar 2015, blocked at the PMC mirror by a bot check. The archive holds the
  Springer article page, which carried the numbers used.
