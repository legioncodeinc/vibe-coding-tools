## Guidance extracted

**Subject line.** Use "Blast from the past" for an informal relationship such as a college
classmate, or "Reconnecting" for a formal one such as a former boss or client. The piece
claims this approach yields "over 90% response rate." No study is cited for that figure
and it should be treated as an unsupported practitioner claim.

**On apologizing.** Do not apologize extensively. Briefly acknowledge the time lapse early
in the body while updating the recipient on your professional progress. The framing should
normalize the silence rather than express regret about it.

**Tone.** Balance confidence with humility. Sound assured that they can help while
acknowledging they are busy. Avoid sounding "desperate or demanding."

**Make it easy to answer.** Offer to reduce the cost of replying: "Please let me know how
I can make it easier for you."

**The ask.**
- Give context for why you are reaching out now.
- Give them an exit: "I completely understand if this isn't a good time."
- Offer reciprocity by asking "how I can be helpful to you."
- Research in advance what specific help you could offer them.

**Do not.** Appear transactional, desperate, or demanding. Do not fail to acknowledge the
gap at all.

## Sourcing caveat

The author is an executive coach. No academic researchers are quoted and no data
underlies the recommendations, including the response-rate claim. Use this file for the
shape of the message and never for a number.
