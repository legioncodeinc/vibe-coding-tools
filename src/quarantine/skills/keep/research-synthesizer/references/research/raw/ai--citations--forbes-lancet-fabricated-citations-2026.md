# AI blamed for rise in fabricated citations found in recent research papers

- **URL:** https://www.forbes.com/sites/michaeltnietzel/2026/05/12/ai-blamed-for-rise-in-fabricated-citations-found-in-recent-research-papers/
- **Publisher:** Forbes, reporting a study published in The Lancet
- **Study lead author:** Maxim Topaz, Columbia University School of Nursing
- **Published:** week of 12 May 2026
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** journalism, reporting a peer reviewed study
- **Note on quotes:** dashes inside quoted passages normalized to spaced hyphens.

## What was measured

An audit of over 2.5 million papers published between January 2023 and February 2026 in the
PubMed Central Open Access dataset, containing more than 125 million bibliographic
references. Citations were verified against PubMed, Crossref, OpenAlex and Google Scholar to
identify fabricated ones.

## The numbers

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Fabricated citations found | Slightly over 4,000 |
| Papers containing them | 2,800 |
| Rate in 2023 | 1 in 2,828 papers |
| Rate in 2025 | 1 in 458 papers |
| Rate in the first 7 weeks of 2026 | 1 in 277 papers |
| Growth | Roughly 12-fold over three years |

## Mechanism

Large language model hallucinations generating non-existent citations. The rise begins after
widespread LLM adoption in late 2022 and 2023, appearing in the record after the expected
publication lag.

## The quote that names the harm

Topaz: "A medical professional or clinical guideline developer has no way of knowing that
the evidence they are relying on does not exist."

## Recommendations reported

- Automated verification systems before peer review
- Integrity metadata attached to article records
- Retroactive screening of existing publications for corrections and retractions
- Fabricated-reference tracking in research integrity databases

## What this source establishes for this skill

Two things. First, fabricated citation is a **measured, growing, real-world phenomenon**,
not a theoretical caution. Second, the harm is downstream and invisible: the person acting
on the synthesis cannot tell. That is the argument for every external claim carrying a URL
that the reader can open, rather than a citation-shaped string.

## Caveat on precision

The absolute rate is low: even at the 2026 figure, roughly 0.36 percent of papers. The
alarming property is the slope, not the level. Reporting the slope without the level would
overstate it.
