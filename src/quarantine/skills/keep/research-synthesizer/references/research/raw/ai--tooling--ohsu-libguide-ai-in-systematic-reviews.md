## Core principle

"AI tools should be used as a companion to human expertise, not a replacement."

Four evidence synthesis standards the guide holds AI to:

- **Rigor.** "Methods must remain systematic, validated, and defensible"
- **Transparency.** "AI use must be clearly documented and reported"
- **Reproducibility.** "Review processes must be understandable and repeatable"
- **Human oversight.** "Humans remain accountable for all decisions and outputs"

Ethical considerations named alongside: data privacy, copyright and licensing, bias,
authorship, and institutional policies.

## Tasks AI may support, with human oversight

- "Screening support after reviewer calibration"
- "De-duplicating citations"
- "Extracting structured data fields for human verification"
- "Drafting summaries that are reviewed and edited by subject experts"

Requirement attached: "All AI-generated outputs must be reviewed, corrected, and approved by
the review team."

## Tasks the guide says to avoid

Generative AI should be avoided for these core methodological steps:

- Literature searching
- Inclusion and exclusion decisions without human review
- Critical appraisal without human review
- Automated synthesis or meta-analysis
- Final manuscript production without verification

## The documented failure mode

"AI-generated searches miss a large proportion of relevant studies and lack transparency and
reproducibility."

This is the most operationally important line in the source for any tool that runs its own
searches. **A search an AI composed is a search with unknown recall.** The stated
consequence is not that the results are wrong but that the coverage is unknown and the
search is not reproducible.

## Tension worth noting against the joint position statement

This guide places literature searching on the avoid list. The joint Cochrane, Campbell, JBI
and CEE position statement
(`ai--evidence-synthesis--cochrane-campbell-jbi-cee-position-2025.md`) does not prohibit any
specific task and instead conditions all use on demonstrated soundness, oversight and
disclosure. The guide is stricter than the position statement. Both are recorded.
