# Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-Analysis of Effects and Processes

- **URL:** https://cancercontrol.cancer.gov/sites/default/files/2020-06/goal_intent_attain.pdf
- **Also indexed at:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/bookseries/abs/pii/S0065260106380021
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** academic (Gollwitzer and Sheeran, Advances in Experimental Social
  Psychology, 2006)

## What an implementation intention is

A structured if-then plan: "If situation Y is encountered, then I will initiate behavior
Z". It links a specific situational cue to a specific response. It is distinct from a goal
intention, which names only the outcome.

## Meta-analytic results

94 studies.

| Outcome | Effect size |
|---|---|
| Goal attainment overall | d = .65 |
| Initiating action | d = .61 |
| Preventing derailment | d = .77 |

## Mechanisms

1. **Cue accessibility.** "the mental representation of this situation becomes highly
   activated, and hence more accessible".
2. **Strategic automaticity.** The response is automated so "people do not have to
   deliberate anymore about when and how they should act".

## Boundary conditions, which matter more than the headline number

**A self-regulatory problem must actually exist.** "When there are few barriers to goal
achievement, then favorable goal intentions and self-efficacy can suffice ...
implementation intention formation might be superfluous."

**The underlying goal intention must be strong.** "strong effects of implementation
intentions were obtained predominantly when the underlying goal intention was strong and
activated."

## Reading for brief design

The if-then form is only worth its words on an item the reader is likely to avoid or
forget, and only where the reader already wants the outcome. Wrapping every routine item
in an if-then format spends words for nothing, by the source's own boundary condition.

## Caveat

2006 meta-analysis, pre-registration era, and the literature contains later work
questioning the durability of implementation-intention effects outside the lab. This
archive did not sweep that later literature. Named as a gap.
