# Declining open rates: A diagnostic guide

- **URL:** https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/38340775067415-declining-open-rates-a-diagnostic-guide
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-docs (beehiiv, newsletter platform support documentation)

## Engagement decay over subscriber lifetime

"Subscribers are most engaged in the early weeks after they join. Over time, that
changes." The guide frames disengagement as the normal path, not the exception, and
recommends cohort review to find "when engagement drops off (e.g., month 1 vs month 3 vs
month 6)". No decay percentages are given.

## Frequency

"Daily sending is where this shows up most clearly." The guide states that a daily cadence
"raises the bar significantly" for content quality. No comparative open-rate figures are
supplied for daily versus weekly.

## Operational thresholds published

- Bounce rate above roughly 2%: address list quality before the next send.
- Complaint rate approaching or exceeding roughly 0.1%: same.
- Unsubscribe rate above 0.3%: same.

## Measurement caveat named by the source

Apple Mail Privacy Protection changes since mid-2024 produced "a decline in open rates",
described as "partially unwinding" the inflation introduced by the 2021 implementation.
Magnitude not specified. The practical consequence: open rate is a degraded metric and
cannot be used alone to decide whether a digest is working.

## What this source does not establish

No quantified relationship between cadence and abandonment. No content-level analysis of
which digests survive. The single load-bearing claim taken from this source is the
qualitative one: daily cadence raises the quality bar, and early-tenure engagement is the
high-water mark rather than the baseline.
