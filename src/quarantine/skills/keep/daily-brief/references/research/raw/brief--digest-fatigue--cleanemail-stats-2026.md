# Email Subscription Fatigue Statistics for 2026 and 2030's Predictions

- **URL:** https://clean.email/blog/insights/email-subscription-fatigue-statistics
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (Clean Email, statistics aggregator)

## Figures as published

**Unsubscribe behavior**

- 70% of consumers unsubscribed from at least three brands in the past three months
  because of excessive messaging. 36% unsubscribed from six or more. Original source not
  named on the page.
- MailerLite's reported unsubscribe rate rose from 0.08% in 2024 to 0.22% in 2025,
  attributed to Gmail's Subscription Management Center.
- Gmail's Subscription Center launch in July 2025 is said to have triggered unsubscribe
  rate spikes of 50% to 300% across senders within six months. Original source not named.

**Stated reasons for unsubscribing**

| Reason | Share |
|---|---|
| Too many emails | 20% |
| Lost interest | 18% |
| Irrelevant content | 17% |

Original source not named on the page.

**Disengagement**

- 45% of recipients with no engagement over six months will never re-engage without
  intervention. Attributed on the page to Return Path.
- Email list decay averages 22.5% annually, roughly one in four subscribers disengaging
  per year. Original source not named.
- 41% of consumers report experiencing subscription fatigue. Original source not named.
- 50% of respondents had cancelled or intended to cancel at least one subscription by
  2025. Original source not named.

## Quality caveat, recorded deliberately

This is an aggregator page. It supplies no survey sample sizes and names an original
source for only two of its figures. Treat every number here as directional, not as a
citable statistic. It is archived because the *ranking* of unsubscribe reasons is
corroborated by the beehiiv source, not because the percentages are trustworthy.
