# CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business

- **Title:** CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business
- **URL:** https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (US Federal Trade Commission)

## Extracted content

**The seven requirements, verbatim headings**

1. "Don't use false or misleading header information"
2. "Don't use deceptive subject lines"
3. "Identify the message as an ad"
4. "Tell recipients where you're located"
5. "Tell recipients how to opt out of receiving future marketing email"
6. "Remember that subscribers and members can opt out of marketing emails"
7. "Honor opt-out requests promptly"

**Penalty**

Up to $53,088 for each separate email that violates the law.

**Message classification**

- Commercial message: an electronic mail message whose primary purpose is advertising or
  promoting a commercial product, service, or website content operated for commercial
  purposes.
- Transactional or relationship message: email that facilitates an agreed-upon
  transaction, provides product safety or warranty information, notifies about account
  changes, conveys employment information, or delivers previously ordered goods or
  services.

## Claims this source supports

1. If the campaign follow-up moves to EMAIL, CAN-SPAM applies with force: physical
   address disclosure, ad identification, working opt-out, and prompt honoring of it.
2. The penalty is per message, not per campaign. A 200-person list sent one
   non-compliant email is 200 violations.
3. A hand-raiser who commented a keyword asking for a resource has not, by that act,
   given an email address or consented to email. Moving the channel from DM to email
   requires collecting the address and the consent separately.
