# 2026 Speed to Lead Benchmark, B2B Response Time Data

- **Source type:** vendor-blog (Artemis GTM, presented as research)

## Source-quality caveat

The publisher labels its own numbers as "illustrative and directional, drawn from client
engagements and published industry benchmarks, not a guarantee." Treat every figure below
as directional.

## Extracted content

**Median response time by industry**

| Industry | Median response |
|---|---|
| RevOps tools | 22 hours |
| Sales enablement | 28 hours |
| Marketing automation | 35 hours |
| FinTech B2B | 48 hours |
| Overall average | 42 hours |

**Conversion by response window**

| Response window | Conversion |
|---|---|
| 0 to 5 minutes | 21% |
| 5 to 30 minutes | 13% |
| 30 to 60 minutes | 8% |
| 1 to 24 hours | 5% |
| 24 hours or more | 2.3% |

> "Fast (sub-5-minute) responders convert at several times the rate of those who wait a
> day or more."

**Response distribution**

Roughly 7% of leads receive a sub-5-minute reply. 35% wait 24 hours or more.

**Methodology**

Drawn from "B2B SaaS GTM engagements ($1M-$50M ARR)" combined with published benchmarks,
presented as "directional medians and ranges."

**Gap in this source**

> The document contains no data specific to inbound social leads. All benchmarks
> aggregate across form submissions and inbound channels without isolating social.

## Claims this source supports

1. The conversion decay curve is steep and front-loaded. The largest single drop is
   between the 0 to 5 minute window and the 5 to 30 minute window.
2. Most teams are nowhere near the fast end. A 42-hour average against a 21% sub-5-minute
   conversion rate is the size of the prize.
3. Named gap: no source found in this archive isolates conversion data for inbound SOCIAL
   leads. Applying form-fill benchmarks to a Facebook commenter is an assumption, and the
   skill states it as one.
