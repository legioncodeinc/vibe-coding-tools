# X Automation Rules

- **Title:** Automation rules
- **URL:** https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (X Corp)

## Why this source matters here

X is the one major platform with an explicit, published, plain-language rule on automated
DMs triggered by keyword. It says no. This source is the strongest single piece of
evidence for the design decision that `lead-harvester` drafts and never sends.

## Extracted content

**Automated Direct Messages**

Prohibited:

> "You may not send unsolicited Direct Messages in a bulk or automated manner."

Permitted only with prior opt-in, where recipients "have requested or have clearly
indicated an intent on X to be contacted by you via Direct Message." Users must be given
clear opt-out mechanisms.

**Automated replies and mentions**

Prohibited: sending unsolicited automated replies based solely on keyword searches.
Spammy or duplicative use results in enforcement.

Permitted only where users have explicitly opted in through an action such as replying to
your posts or mentioning your account. The rules then require:

- "one automated reply or mention per user interaction"
- clear opt-out mechanisms
- filtering for sensitive language and media
- prior X approval for advertisers and brands

**Automated follows and unfollows**

> "You may not follow or unfollow X accounts in a bulk, aggressive, or indiscriminate
> manner."

Applications claiming to increase followers are banned.

**Automated likes and reposts**

> "You may not like posts or hide replies in an automated manner."

Reposts are permitted for "entertainment, informational, or novelty purposes" if
otherwise compliant. Bulk aggressive reposting violates the rules.

**Spam and platform manipulation**

Prohibited automated activity includes:

- "automatically post about trending topics"
- duplicate or near-duplicate posts across accounts
- misleading links in automated posts or DMs
- automation of abusive, harassing, or hateful content

## Claims this source supports

1. Unsolicited bulk or automated DM on X is explicitly prohibited, in those words.
2. Keyword-triggered automated replies are explicitly prohibited when unsolicited.
3. A user who replied to your post HAS created an opt-in of limited scope: one automated
   reply per interaction. That is the ceiling, not a licence to sequence.
4. Automated liking is prohibited outright, which rules out automated reciprocal
   engagement as a warming tactic.
