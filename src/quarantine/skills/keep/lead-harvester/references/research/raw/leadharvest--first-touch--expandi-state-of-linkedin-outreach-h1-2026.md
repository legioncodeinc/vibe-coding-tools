## Source-quality caveat

Expandi sells LinkedIn automation, so its interest is in showing outreach works. The
underlying sample is large and platform-measured, which is the reason to keep it. Note
that Expandi is itself named as a flagged tool in
`leadharvest--platform-rules--linkedin-crackdown-anybiz-2026.md`. Both readings are kept.

## Extracted content

**Methodology**

"70,130+ real campaigns" analyzed from Expandi's platform, plus a survey of "2200+
sellers." Data spans January to June 2026.

**Connection request acceptance**

| Campaign type | Acceptance |
|---|---|
| Connector | 29.61% |
| Mobile connector | 22.32% |
| Builder (warm-up approach) | 22% |
| January baseline (seasonal low) | 21.45% |

**Reply rates**

| Channel or campaign type | Reply rate |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn overall | 10.3% |
| Cold email (comparison) | 5.1% |
| Messenger campaigns | 16.86% |
| Event campaigns | 14.21% |
| Inbound visitor campaigns | 13.4% |
| Template-based campaigns | 8.62% |
| January baseline (seasonal low) | 6.47% |

**Message style**

> "short, casual LinkedIn messages have the highest response rate at 16.86%"

Specific character or word counts are not given.

**Personalization adoption**

- 53.71% personalize manually
- 29.19% use AI
- 17.10% do not personalize

**Follow-up sequencing**

| Touch | Effect on responses |
|---|---|
| First follow-up | 0.6% fewer responses than the initial message |
| Second follow-up | 4.05% more responses |
| Third and beyond | diminishing, roughly 1% |

**Volume**

71% of surveyed sellers send 50 or fewer connection requests weekly. 54% send fewer than
25 weekly, well under the 80 to 100 weekly ceiling.

## Claims this source supports

1. Short and casual outperforms long and formal in this dataset, at 16.86% versus 8.62%
   for template-based campaigns. Nearly a 2x gap between casual and templated.
2. Templated messaging is the worst-performing named category in the report.
3. Follow-up structure has a specific shape: the first follow-up does slightly worse than
   the opener, the SECOND follow-up is where the lift is, and the third onward is
   marginal. Two follow-ups, not five.
4. Warm inbound outperforms cold: inbound visitor campaigns at 13.4% against 10.3%
   overall. A hand-raiser from a keyword campaign is warmer still than a profile visitor.
5. Most operators send far below the ceiling, so volume is rarely the binding constraint.
   Response quality is.
