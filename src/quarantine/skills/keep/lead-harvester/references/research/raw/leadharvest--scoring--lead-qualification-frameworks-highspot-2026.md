**Named qualification frameworks**

| Framework | Expansion |
|---|---|
| BANT | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline |
| CHAMP | Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization |
| MEDDIC | Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion, Competition |
| GPCTBA/C&I | Goals, Plans, Challenges, Timeline, Budget, Authority / Consequences and Implications |
| SPICED | Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, Decision |

On BANT:

> "BANT focuses on four simple but vital pieces of sales intelligence: how much a buyer
> can spend, who makes the call, what problem needs solving, and how soon they're looking
> to move."

CHAMP inverts BANT's sequence, putting the buyer's challenges first, then authority,
funding, and initiative priority.

MEDDIC is designed for complex, multi-stakeholder deals with longer cycles.

**MQL versus SQL**

- Marketing-Qualified Lead: prospects showing engagement interest (downloading assets,
  attending webinars, returning site visits) without direct sales involvement.
- Sales-Qualified Lead: fully vetted prospects meeting opportunity criteria, with
  confirmed need, timing alignment, and verified decision-making authority.

**Engagement scoring**

The article names "Engagement recency and frequency scoring" as a scoring dimension:
interaction timeliness and repetition, "where recent repeated actions score higher than
isolated historical touches."

**Gap in this source**

No inbound qualification rate figures are given. No social-channel-specific data.

## Claims this source supports

1. A hand-raiser from a keyword-comment campaign is an MQL by the standard definition:
   engagement interest with no sales involvement and no verified budget, authority, or
   timing. Calling one an SQL without a conversation is a category error.
2. Recency and frequency are established, named scoring dimensions in mainstream lead
   qualification practice, not an invention of this skill. Repeated recent actions score
   higher than isolated older ones. This is the published basis for stacking multiple
   signals from the same person into a higher rank.
3. The frameworks above (BANT and its relatives) qualify on facts a comment cannot carry.
   Signal-strength scoring for social hand-raisers is a PRIORITIZATION model, not a
   qualification model, and the two should not be conflated.
