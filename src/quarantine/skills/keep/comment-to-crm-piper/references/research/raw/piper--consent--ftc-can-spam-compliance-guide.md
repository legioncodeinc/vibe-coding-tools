# CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business (FTC)

- **Title:** CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business
- **URL:** https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (US Federal Trade Commission)

## Extracted content

**The seven requirements**

1. Accurate header information. "Your 'From,' 'To,' 'Reply-To,' and routing
   information...must be accurate".
2. Truthful subject lines. "The subject line must accurately reflect the content".
3. Ad disclosure. "You must disclose clearly and conspicuously that your message is an
   advertisement".
4. A valid physical postal address.
5. A clear and conspicuous explanation of how to opt out.
6. Subscribers retain opt-out rights for marketing email.
7. Prompt compliance. "You must honor a recipient's opt-out request within 10 business
   days".

**Message type definitions**

| Type | Definition |
|---|---|
| Commercial | Primarily "advertises or promotes a commercial product or service" |
| Transactional or relationship | Facilitates an existing transaction, provides account information, or covers warranties, without promotional intent |

**Opt-out window.** Recipients have 30 days after receipt to opt out. Senders must comply
within 10 business days.

**Penalties.** "Each separate email in violation of the law is subject to penalties of up
to $53,088".

**Prior consent.** No prior consent is required to send a commercial email under CAN-SPAM.
The recipient's remedy is the opt-out.

## Claims this source supports

1. The US regime and the UK or EU regime differ at the root. CAN-SPAM is opt-out and needs
   no prior consent; the ICO and PECR position for individual subscribers is consent or
   soft opt-in. A skill serving both audiences cannot state one rule.
2. The moment a hand-raiser is moved from a platform DM to EMAIL, CAN-SPAM attaches in the
   US: physical address, ad disclosure, working opt-out honored within 10 business days.
3. A first message that only delivers the specific thing the person asked for leans toward
   transactional or relationship. A first message that pitches is commercial. The drafting
   choice has a legal consequence, so the drafts should be classifiable.
4. Penalty exposure is per email, which is why an automated blast off a partially verified
   roster is disproportionately risky compared to the same messages sent by hand.
