# Sending direct marketing: choosing your lawful basis (ICO)

- **Title:** Sending direct marketing: Choosing your lawful basis
- **URL:** https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/sending-direct-marketing-choosing-your-lawful-basis/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (Information Commissioner's Office, UK)

## Extracted content

**The base obligation**

> "You must have a valid data protection reason if you want to use people's information to
> send your direct marketing messages."

Two bases are in play, consent and legitimate interests, and which one is available depends
on the CHANNEL and on whether the recipient is an individual subscriber or a corporate
subscriber.

**Consent is mandatory for**

- Automated and recorded marketing calls.
- Live calls to numbers registered on a preference service.
- Claims management services calls.
- Electronic mail to individual subscribers, meaning people, sole traders, and
  partnerships.

**Legitimate interests may apply for**

- Live marketing calls to unregistered numbers where no objection has been made.
- Postal marketing.
- Email to corporate subscribers, meaning limited companies and similar entities.

**Soft opt-in.** Listed as an alternative to consent for electronic mail to individual
subscribers. This page does not spell out its conditions.

**How PECR interacts with the lawful basis**

> "PECR can affect your choice of data protection lawful basis. This is because sometimes
> PECR says you must have consent to send direct marketing."

**Third party and public sources.** Not discussed on this page.

## Claims this source supports

1. Lawful basis is channel-dependent. There is no single answer that covers a direct
   message, an email, and a phone call to the same person.
2. In the UK, marketing email to a sole trader or an individual needs consent or the soft
   opt-in. That is exactly the population a social hand-raiser campaign attracts.
3. Marketing email to a limited company address sits in a different, more permissive
   bucket, so the corporate-versus-individual distinction is worth capturing on the record
   at intake.
4. A skill cannot resolve lawful basis for the user. It can surface which channel and which
   subscriber type the record implies, and tell the user that determines the answer.
