# SR-058: Class booking lacks required waitlists or membership credits

## Use when

Use this response when a customer needs class waitlists, membership credits, cancellation credit handling, or a complete member-booking experience that has not been confirmed as supported.

## Required evidence

- Account and current class configuration
- Capacity model and required waitlist order
- Membership and credit-pack rules
- Cancellation, expiration, and credit-return policy
- Required member portal experience
- Payment implications and current billing configuration

## Customer-facing email

**Subject:** {ticket_id}: Review of class waitlist and membership credit requirements

Hi {customer_first_name},

I understand your class-booking flow needs waitlists, membership credits, cancellation handling, and a member-facing booking experience. We have not confirmed that the complete requested flow is supported in the current configuration.

To avoid creating an unsafe billing workaround, please send the requirements as five separate parts:

1. Class capacity and overbooking rules.
2. Waitlist order and promotion rules.
3. Membership and credit-pack earning, use, and expiration rules.
4. Cancellation timing and whether a credit should be returned.
5. Required portal experience, payment impact, and the current configuration.

Redact private member information. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, tokens, or unrestricted customer exports.

{agency} will have a product or implementation specialist review each requirement and identify the supported path and any gaps. We will not promise native support or invent a billing workaround while that review is open. I will update you after the specialist review.

{agent_name}
{agency} Support

## Agent notes

- Evidence state: `UNRESOLVED`.
- Separate capacity, waitlist, credits, cancellation, portal, and payment requirements before evaluating a solution.
- Escalate to a product or implementation specialist after the evidence set is complete.
- Do not promise native support, create duplicate entitlements, or improvise billing logic.
