# SR-035: An international number cannot support the requested channel

## Use when

Use when an international number cannot be used for the requested voice, SMS, or WhatsApp path.

## Required evidence

- Country, business and number type, current carrier, requested channels, existing number, direction, registration state, workflow goal, and expected and actual billing behavior.

## Customer-facing email

**Subject:** {ticket_id}: Reviewing international number and channel support

Hi {customer_first_name},

I understand that the existing international number does not support the voice, SMS, or WhatsApp path you expected. Regional support, registration, portability, and billing can differ, so we need the exact country, number, channel, and use case before making a change.

Please send the country, business type, number type, current carrier, requested channels, existing number, inbound or outbound direction, registration state, workflow goal, and the expected and actual billing behavior. Do not send carrier or account credentials.

Please do not port, replace, or reconfigure the number. After I receive these details, I will route the case to our telephony and compliance specialist. We cannot promise number portability, channel support, cost, or a compliant workaround before that review.

{agent_name}
{agency} Support

## Agent notes

Evidence class `F2`. Remediation class `UNRESOLVED`. Stop after minimal regional evidence collection and escalate. Do not promise portability, capability, price, or compliance, and do not modify the number.
