# SR-011: Social planner opens as a blank page

## Use when

Use when the social planning area loads as a blank page for a customer.

## Required evidence

- Workspace, exact planner page, post or draft identifier, browser, timestamp and timezone, screenshot, and one clean-browser comparison.

## Customer-facing email

**Subject:** {ticket_id}: Checking the blank social planner page

Hi {customer_first_name},

I understand that the social planner is opening as a blank page. Please preserve any draft content outside the editor before testing so your work remains protected.

Please send the workspace, exact planner page, affected post or draft identifier, browser, and time of the latest occurrence with your timezone. Include a screenshot that excludes private account details. Then open the same page once in a clean browser session and tell me whether it remains blank.

Please do not delete drafts, posts, or connected accounts. If the same workspace remains blank in the clean session, I will consolidate the evidence for our technical team. We have not confirmed a current service-wide issue or a cause, and I do not have a confirmed update time yet.

{agent_name}
{agency} Support

## Agent notes

Evidence class `F1`. Remediation class `R1`. Apply `HR-13`. Preserve content and compare one clean session. Deletion or recreation is prohibited without an object-level diagnosis and dependency review.
