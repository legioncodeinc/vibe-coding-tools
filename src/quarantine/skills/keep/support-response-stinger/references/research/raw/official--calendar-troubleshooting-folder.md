## Captured evidence

The folder identifies distinct support paths for:

- Google Calendar writer-permission errors
- Reconnecting a user's Google Calendar
- Missing appointment slots
- Appointment merge values not rendering
- Contact reassignment after group-calendar booking
- Double bookings
- A connected Google account not appearing in integration settings
- Google Calendar integration breakage
- Blank rescheduling links
- The built-in calendar troubleshooting tool
- Counting only available days inside a booking window

Calendar replies should first identify the affected calendar, user, date, timezone, integration, and expected slot. Missing availability, sync failure, a conflict, capacity, notice, buffer, and permissions are different causes.
