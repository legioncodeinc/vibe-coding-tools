## Captured facts

A missing course or offer can result from the wrong sub-account, draft status, an unattached course, or unsaved configuration. Purchase failures require checking the supported payment connection, pricing, and offer publication. Access failures require confirming enrollment, offer-to-course association, and any access-grant workflow. Media failures require verifying upload completion and supported content.

## Safety caveat

Do not re-enroll a learner, recreate a purchase, or republish an offer until payment and access history are checked. Those actions can produce duplicate access or billing.
