## Captured facts

Payment-method availability depends on the connected processor, whether the account is in test or live mode, the method enabled in settings, and the product surface using it. A method available for invoices, funnels, stores, or payment links may not be supported for SaaS subscription onboarding or recurring payments.

## Safety caveat

Confirm product surface, processor account, currency, country, test or live mode, and one-time or recurring use before changing settings. Never ask for a full card number, security code, bank credential, or processor password in a ticket email.
