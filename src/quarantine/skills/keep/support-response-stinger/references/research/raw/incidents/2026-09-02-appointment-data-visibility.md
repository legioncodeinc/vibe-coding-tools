## Captured facts

Appointment data was missing from certain views and reports for a defined period. The service was restored and the provider reported that affected data was being backfilled.

## Safety caveat

Do not tell a customer to recreate appointments while backfill or visibility recovery is still being validated. Collect the affected account, calendar, view or report, appointment identifiers, and timestamps, then confirm the records before taking corrective action.
