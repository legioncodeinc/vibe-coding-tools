# Course Page Latency and Loading Timeouts

- URL: https://status.gohighlevel.com/incident/975423
- Fetched: 2026-09-04
- Source type: official-status-incident
- Incident observed: 2026-07-23
- Trend window: 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive
- Window role: direct recent incident evidence

## Captured facts

Course pages experienced increased latency and loading timeouts. The provider later confirmed that course pages were loading normally.

## Safety caveat

Do not republish the course, recreate the offer, or re-enroll the learner solely because a page times out. Collect the course, offer, learner, sub-account, page URL, timestamp, and whether another learner or network reproduces the problem.
