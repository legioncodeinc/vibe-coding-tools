# Intermittent 520 Errors Affecting Certain Pages

- URL: https://status.gohighlevel.com/incident/926759
- Fetched: 2026-09-04
- Source type: official-status-incident
- Incident observed: 2026-06-17
- Trend window: 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive
- Window role: direct recent incident evidence

## Captured facts

A subset of pages intermittently failed with HTTP 520 errors. The affected deployment was rolled back. The incident recommended clearing browser cookies or site data only if failures continued after the rollback.

## Safety caveat

Clearing site data can sign the user out and remove local preferences. Explain that impact before asking the customer to do it, and first confirm the exact page, timestamp, browser, account, and whether an incognito session reproduces the error.
