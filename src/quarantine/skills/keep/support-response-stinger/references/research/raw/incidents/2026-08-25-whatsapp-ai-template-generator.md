## Captured facts

AI-assisted WhatsApp template generation was unavailable while underlying AI infrastructure was updated. The provider tested and rolled out a fix, then reported normal operation.

## Safety caveat

Do not advise deleting existing templates, reconnecting WhatsApp, or resubmitting approved templates solely because the generator is unavailable. Confirm whether manual template creation still works and preserve all approved content and identifiers.
