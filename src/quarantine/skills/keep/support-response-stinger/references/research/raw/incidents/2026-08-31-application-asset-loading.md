## Captured facts

Application assets failed to load in production. The provider attributed the recovery to a production configuration update and reported that expected behavior was restored.

## Safety caveat

Do not ask the customer to rebuild pages or delete content because scripts, styles, or images fail to appear. First capture the page, browser, timestamp, screenshot, console or network error when available, and whether another browser or account reproduces the failure.
