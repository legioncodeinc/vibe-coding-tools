## Captured facts

The article separates calls that connect with poor audio from calls that never connect. For choppy audio, one-way audio, echo, delay, or dropped calls, it recommends scoping the affected users and networks, comparing Wi-Fi and wired service, closing bandwidth-heavy applications, checking the selected microphone and headset, updating or restarting the app, and reviewing latency, jitter, packet loss, and quality warnings.

## Safety caveat

Do not blame the customer's network without comparative evidence. Escalate with the affected account, user, contact or number, exact timestamp, call direction, app surface, network type, warnings, and reproducibility. Do not request call credentials or unredacted private content.
