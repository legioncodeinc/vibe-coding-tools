## Captured facts

The FAQ covers removal of the last card, card changes across accounts sharing one processor customer ID, missing payment details, Indian billing-address requirements, 3DS-only auto-recharge failures, welcome-email fields and templates, duplicate or missing sub-account creation, subscription moves and upgrades, unsupported billing-interval changes, account transfers, invalid currency, blocked processor disconnects, V1 versus V2 plan visibility, mandatory email verification, rebilling, manual verification, account pauses, and dashboard visibility.

## Safety caveat

Disabling SaaS cancels the subscription and permanently deletes the wallet, and refunds may require manual handling. Do not recommend disablement, processor disconnection, account transfer, currency changes, or subscription moves without billing authority, impact review, explicit confirmation, and a recovery plan.
