## Captured findings

The article separates acknowledgment, reproduction evidence, confirmed defect and escalation, investigation without an ETA, workaround, scheduled fix, shipped fix, and closure. This supports stage-specific templates instead of using one generic apology for every ticket.

The useful pattern is to state what is known, what evidence is still needed, who owns the next action, and when the next update will arrive. A support agent should not convert an update commitment into an unsupported resolution promise.
