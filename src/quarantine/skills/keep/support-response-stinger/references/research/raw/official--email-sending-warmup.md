## Captured facts

The guide recommends an authenticated dedicated sending domain, gradual volume increases, opted-in and engaged recipients, controlled frequency, healthy lists, concise content, and no public URL shorteners. It separates native email service support from custom SMTP responsibility.

Troubleshooting starts with domain age and warm-up state, DNS and authentication, current reputation signals, list source and engagement, and content or link risk. Domain reputation data can lag, so a reply should avoid promising instant recovery.

Customer-facing copy may summarize these checks under `{agency}` but must not name the upstream mail product or link to the source.
