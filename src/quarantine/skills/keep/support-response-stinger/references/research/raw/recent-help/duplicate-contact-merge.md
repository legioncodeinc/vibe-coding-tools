## Captured facts

Possible duplicates can be reviewed manually or through a duplicate-management tool. The retained master record controls conflicting field values, while appointments, payments, orders, invoices, conversations, tasks, tags, and active workflow behavior can be affected. Admin permission and selection limits also affect whether the merge action is available.

## Safety caveat

Contact merges are permanent. A response must require explicit confirmation of the master record and a review of conflicting fields, financial records, appointments, and active workflows before the merge is performed.
