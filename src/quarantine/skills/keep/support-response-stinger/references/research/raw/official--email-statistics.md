## Captured facts

Missing open, click, delivery, or bounce statistics can depend on how the message was sent, which sending provider is configured, a tracking-domain CNAME, or provider webhooks. Statistics may live in different views for bulk actions, smart lists, workflows, campaigns, or templates.

A response should ask for the contact, approximate send time, sending method, message or workflow name, and which specific metric is missing. Provider credentials or webhooks should not be reset until configuration ownership and impact are confirmed.
