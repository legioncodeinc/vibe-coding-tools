## Captured facts

Portal setup includes a custom domain, branding, language, invitations, application permissions, and optional Google sign-in. Stale branding can require a private-browser or cache check. Affiliate statistics depend on login and permissions. DNS may require propagation time. The recommended favicon is square, uses a supported image format, and is no larger than 16 by 16 pixels.

## Safety caveat

The customer-facing response must use `{agency}` as the provider and must not expose the upstream help site, product name, default portal domain, or vendor identity. Do not weaken authentication or broaden application permissions merely to bypass a login problem.
