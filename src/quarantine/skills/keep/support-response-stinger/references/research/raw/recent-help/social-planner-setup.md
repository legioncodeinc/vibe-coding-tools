## Captured facts

Publishing requires an active social connection, access to the destination page or account, supported media, a selected channel, and the intended location timezone. Failed posts appear in the planner. Authentication or permission alerts can require reconnecting the affected account, and platform-specific capabilities vary.

## Safety caveat

Check whether the post already reached the destination before retrying. Reconnect only the affected social account and confirm the authorizing user's permissions. Do not duplicate a scheduled or partially published post.
