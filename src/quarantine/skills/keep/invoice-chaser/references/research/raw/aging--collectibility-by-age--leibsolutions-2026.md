# A/R Invoice Collectibility By Age

- URL: https://www.leibsolutions.com/a-r-collectibility-by-age/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor (receivables consultancy)
- Publication date: not stated on page

## What it says

Presents collectibility bands by days past due:

| Days past due | Collectibility as printed |
|---|---|
| 0-30 | 95% to 97% |
| 31-60 | 80% to 90% |
| 61-90 | 60% to 75% |
| 91-120 | 30% to 40% |
| 121-180 | 40% to 50% |
| 181 plus | Less than 50% |
| 360 plus | Less than 70% |

Verbatim: "The time for outside, 'third-party' collection agency action is when the debt
may still be collectible, best from 90-120 days past due. Waiting too long is to invite a
total write-off."

## Limitations noted at fetch time

- No external data source is cited. The page calls the numbers a "general trend" and a
  "typical breakdown."
- The table is internally inconsistent: the 121-180 band (40% to 50%) is printed higher
  than the 91-120 band (30% to 40%), and the 360-plus band ("Less than 70%") is printed
  as a weaker constraint than the 181-plus band ("Less than 50%"). Collectibility cannot
  rise with age. Treat the specific percentages as unreliable and use this source only
  for the qualitative claim that the sharpest drop sits around the 90-day mark.
