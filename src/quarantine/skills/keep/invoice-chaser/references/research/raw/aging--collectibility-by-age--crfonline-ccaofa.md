# Benchmarking the Collectability of Your Accounts Receivable

- URL: https://www.crfonline.org/education/benchmark-collectability-ar/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: industry-association (Credit Research Foundation, reproducing Commercial Collection Agencies of America data)
- Publication date: not stated on page

## What it says

Reproduces a collectability table attributed to Commercial Collection Agencies of America
(CCA of A), measured as delinquency period from the invoice due date against collection
potential.

Verbatim table as extracted:

| Delinquency period from invoice due date | Collection potential |
|---|---|
| 1 month | 88.7% |
| 3 months | 68.9% |
| 6 months | 51.3% |
| 9 months | 37.5% |
| 12 months | 21.4% |
| 18 months | 15.2% |
| 24 months | 8.9% |

Verbatim fragment: "Delinquency Period Collection from Invoice Due Date Potential 1 month
88.7% 3 months 68.9%..."

The page's own subject is a "Collectability Scoring" methodology that weights multiple
parameters. It does not carry DSO benchmarks, best-possible-DSO, or Collection
Effectiveness Index figures.

## Limitations noted at fetch time

- The page does not state when CCA of A collected the underlying data or the sample size.
- The figures are collection potential, not realized collection, and no confidence
  interval is given.
