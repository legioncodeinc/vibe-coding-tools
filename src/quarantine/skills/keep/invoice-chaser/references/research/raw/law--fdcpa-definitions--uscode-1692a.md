# 15 U.S.C. 1692a, Definitions (Fair Debt Collection Practices Act)

- URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A15+section%3A1692a+edition%3Aprelim%29
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: official-docs (United States Code, Office of the Law Revision Counsel)

## What it says

Definition of "debt collector", 1692a(6), verbatim in relevant part:

"any person who uses any instrumentality of interstate commerce or the mails in any
business the principal purpose of which is the collection of any debts"

Exclusion, verbatim in relevant part:

"any officer or employee of a creditor while, in the name of the creditor, collecting
debts for such creditor" is not a debt collector under this definition.

Definition of "debt", 1692a(5), verbatim:

"any obligation or alleged obligation of a consumer to pay money arising out of a
transaction in which the money, property, insurance, or services which are the subject of
the transaction are primarily for personal, family, or household purposes."

## Why it matters here

Two independent limits appear in the statutory text itself. The "debt" definition is
restricted to transactions primarily for personal, family, or household purposes, which
places ordinary business-to-business invoices outside the Act. The "debt collector"
definition excludes a creditor's own officers and employees collecting in the creditor's
name, which places a business collecting its own accounts outside the Act separately.
