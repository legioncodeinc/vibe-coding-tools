# The Rosenthal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (RFDCPA): key differences from the FDCPA

- URL: https://www.kandhlawgroup.com/blog/the-rosenthal-fair-debt-collection-practices-act-rfdcpa-key-differences-from-the-fdcpa/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: legal-explainer (law firm blog, Khosroabadi and Hill APC)
- Publication date: not stated on page

## What it says

Verbatim: "The RFDCPA protects debtors from first-party creditors. It prohibits harassment
from both third-party debt collectors and original creditors."

Worked example given on the page: if a person owes a bank directly, the FDCPA protects
them only against collection agencies acting on the bank's behalf, while the RFDCPA
extends protection against the bank itself.

The page mentions that violations can expose a collector to "actual, statutory, and legal
damages."

## Limitations noted at fetch time

- The page does not quote the RFDCPA statutory definition of "debt collector" and gives no
  California Civil Code section numbers.
- It does not resolve whether the RFDCPA reaches commercial or business-to-business debt,
  and its discussion of commercial debt is limited to venue and judgment topics.
- It is a law firm marketing blog, not a primary source. Treat the direction of the claim
  (at least one state reaches original creditors, unlike the FDCPA) as supported, and
  treat any specific application as needing a lawyer.
