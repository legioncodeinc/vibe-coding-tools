# The Fair Debt Collection Practices Act for Small Businesses

- URL: https://www.findlaw.com/smallbusiness/business-finances/the-fair-debt-collection-practices-act.html
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: legal-explainer (FindLaw, Thomson Reuters)
- Updated: July 12, 2024

## What it says

Verbatim: "A person or entity that collects its own debts or does so only in isolated
instances is not considered a debt collector."

Verbatim: "The FDCPA does not cover commercial debt collection" and it "does not apply to
the company owed the debt."

Applies only to consumer debts, described as debts for "primarily personal, family, or
household purposes," including credit cards, auto loans, and medical bills.

Prohibited practices summarized on the page, applying to covered debt collectors:

- Harassment: no threats, no obscene language, no excessive calls, stated as a maximum of
  seven calls per seven days.
- False statements: cannot impersonate attorneys, cannot claim the debtor committed a
  crime, cannot misrepresent the amount of the debt.
- Unfair practices: no premature check deposits, no illegal property threats, no unlawful
  fees.
- Third-party contact: limited, and cannot reveal the debt-collection purpose on a
  postcard.

Verbatim on state law: "State laws can also address what creditors may do."
