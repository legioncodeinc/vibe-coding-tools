# Payment reminder email: 8 templates and a sequence guide

- URL: https://www.chaserhq.com/blog/email-templates-friendly-late-payment-reminders-for-your-customers
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor (credit control software blog)

## What it says

Recommended sequence and tone, as printed:

| Stage | Timing | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-due | 7 days before due | Warm and helpful |
| Due date | Due date | Friendly and clear |
| First overdue | 1 to 3 days late | Polite, no blame |
| Second reminder | 7 days late | Firm but respectful |
| Payment plan offer | 14 days late | Empathetic and clear |
| First formal notice | 30 days late | Formal and direct |
| Second formal notice | 60 days late | Serious and factual |
| Final notice | 90 days late | Formal and final |

Verbatim on the overall shape: "Most businesses send a pre-due reminder, a due-date
prompt, and two or three overdue reminders before moving to a formal notice at around 30
days."

Verbatim on switching channel: "When email goes quiet, a friendly call often resolves
things faster than another message."

Verbatim on the pre-due reminder: "A pre-due reminder is the most underused message in
credit control, and it prevents more late payments than any other."

Collections escalation is placed after the final notice at 90 days overdue.

## Limitations noted at fetch time

No quantified response-rate data is offered anywhere on the page, including for the
pre-due claim, which is stated as a strong assertion with no supporting figure. This is a
vendor recommending a cadence its own product automates. Treat the eight-stage ladder as
industry convention, not as a tested schedule.
