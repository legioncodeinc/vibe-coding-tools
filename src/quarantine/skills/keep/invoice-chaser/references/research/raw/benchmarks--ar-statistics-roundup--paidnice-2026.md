# 25 Accounts Receivable Statistics shaping AR in 2026

- URL: https://www.paidnice.com/blog/accounts-receivable-statistics
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor (AR automation software blog, aggregating third-party figures)

## What it says, with the underlying source each figure is attributed to

Late payment rates:

- Atradius (2023): "Over 50% of all B2B invoiced sales in the USA, UK, and Asia are
  overdue."
- Federation of Small Businesses (2023): "52% of small businesses experienced late
  payments from their customers at some point in the previous 90 days."
- FSB (2023): "Half of all payments made to SMEs are paid late, with 10% paid more than 30
  days late and another 12% paid 60 days late."

Financial impact:

- Intuit and UK Department for Business and Trade (2023): UK small businesses owed
  "27,000 pounds in late payments", a 27.4% increase since 2021.
- Xero (2022): annual late payment costs of "AU$1.1 billion" (Australia), "NZ$456 million"
  (New Zealand), "684 million pounds" (UK).

Operational impact:

- Xero (2024): "Small-business owners spend approximately 10% of their workday (5 hours a
  week) following up on unpaid invoices."

Days sales outstanding:

- Sage (2022): "Between 2010 and 2021, UK small businesses halved their days sales
  outstanding (DSO), from around 45 days in the 2010 to 2013 period to about 22 days by
  2020 to 2021."
- Atradius (2024): "Average payment terms in Europe increased 26% in 2024 to an average of
  52 days, up from 41 days in 2023."

Automation impact:

- Paidnice own data: "DSO comes in by about 50% within 30 days on average" when reminders
  and statements automate.
- PYMNTS (2021): "62% of firms implementing AR automation saw measurable reductions in
  their DSO."
- Intuit QuickBooks (2023): "Businesses using financial management software experience a
  25% reduction in overdue payments and a 21% improvement in cash flow."
- BlackLine (2024): "91% of mid-sized firms with fully automated AR systems report
  increased savings, less cash-flow stress, and faster growth."
- MineralTree (2023): "85% of finance teams using automation report increased efficiency in
  payment processing."

Industry variation:

- Smart Data Foundry and University of Edinburgh (2022): highest late-payment rates in
  Education (69%), Construction (63%), Manufacturing (63%); lowest in Financial Services
  (18%) and Accommodation and Food (21%).

Adoption:

- PYMNTS and American Express (2021): 44% of US firms automate only a few AR tasks; 33%
  remain manual.

## Limitations noted at fetch time

This is a secondary aggregation. Figures were not verified against the primary sources at
fetch time, and several are three or four years old. The self-serving figures (Paidnice's
own 50% DSO reduction claim) carry the strongest commercial interest and the weakest
methodology disclosure.
