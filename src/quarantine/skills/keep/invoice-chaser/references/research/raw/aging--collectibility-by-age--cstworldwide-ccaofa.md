# Collection Statistics (CST WorldWide resources page)

- URL: https://www.cstworldwide.com/resources-collection-statistics.html
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor (commercial collection agency)
- Publication date: not stated on page

## What it says

Cites a survey by Commercial Collection Agencies of America for collectability by age of
debt:

| Age past due | Collectability |
|---|---|
| 3 months | 68.9% |
| 6 months | 51.3% |
| 1 year | 8.9% |

Verbatim: "the probability of full collection on a delinquent account drops drastically
according to the length of the delinquency."

Verbatim: "In as little as three months, the probability of collecting a delinquent
account drops to 68.9%."

Attribution given for CCA of A: member agencies "in aggregate handle approximately 60% of
claims placed with professional commercial collection agencies in the United States of
America."

## Conflict flagged at fetch time

This page's 1-year figure (8.9%) does not match the CRF Online reproduction of the same
CCA of A table, where 8.9% is the 24-month value and 12 months is 21.4%. Two vendors
citing the same source disagree on which row is the one-year row. See
`aging--collectibility-by-age--crfonline-ccaofa.md`.
