# Small businesses getting paid quicker, but still late (Xero Small Business Insights)

- URL: https://blog.xero.com/data-insights/small-business-insights-data-late-payment-results/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor-research (Xero Small Business Insights, derived from anonymized
  accounting platform data)
- Published: March 2, 2026

## What it says

Average days late, December quarter, by country:

| Country | Average days late |
|---|---|
| New Zealand | 4.5 (record low) |
| Australia | 6.6 (second shortest quarterly result) |
| United States | 7.8 (shortest in four years) |
| United Kingdom | 8.0 (second shortest quarterly result) |
| Canada | 9.7 (shortest since June quarter 2024) |

The page describes a "clear trend of getting paid 'less late'" across all five countries,
while stating that "small businesses are still being paid late" and that this continues to
hurt cash flow.

Four practices are named as correlating with faster payment: online payment options,
automated reminders, pay-now buttons, and multiple payment methods.

## Limitations noted at fetch time

The page does not disclose sample size or method beyond noting that Australian records
begin in January 2017. "Days late" here is days beyond the agreed due date, which is a
different measure from days sales outstanding and from days past due on a single invoice.
The four named practices are presented as correlations, with no causal test.
