# 2026 Small Business Late Payments Report

- URL: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/small-business-late-payments-report-2026/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor-research (Intuit QuickBooks commissioned survey)

## What it says

Verbatim: "Nearly 3 in 5 businesses (59%) say at least some of their invoices are overdue
by 30 days or more, up from 47% last year."

Verbatim: "The money waiting to come in averages $17.7K per business, roughly consistent
with last year's $17.5K."

Verbatim: "For 1 in 5 businesses (22%), it runs deeper than a few stragglers: at least 20%
of their invoices are sitting unpaid past that 30-day mark."

Verbatim: "Nearly half of owners (49%) say standard payment processing times create
critical or moderate cash-flow gaps."

Verbatim: "Nearly 2 in 5 owners (39%) say one late payment made it hard to cover payroll
or bills in the past year."

Verbatim: "More than 1 in 4 owners (27%) say a missed payment under $5,000 made it harder
to cover payroll or bills."

Verbatim: "Nearly 3 in 5 (59%) reported paying for instant transfer or fast deposit in
2025."

## Methodology as stated

Verbatim: "An online survey of 1,305 US business owners commissioned by Intuit QuickBooks
in December 2025." Part of "An ongoing quarterly survey of small business owners and
decision-makers commissioned by Intuit QuickBooks. Total quarterly sample of approximately
5,000 respondents."

## Limitations noted at fetch time

Self-reported survey data commissioned by a company that sells invoicing and instant
payout products. The 47% to 59% year-over-year jump is large enough that a change in
question wording or sample composition cannot be ruled out from the page alone.
