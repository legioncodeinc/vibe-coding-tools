# What laws limit what debt collectors can say or do?

- URL: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-laws-limit-what-debt-collectors-can-say-or-do-en-329/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: official-docs (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau)

## What it says

Verbatim: "Under the FDCPA, debt collectors can include collection agencies, debt buyers,
and lawyers."

Verbatim: the FDCPA "doesn't generally cover collection by the original creditor or
business you owed money to."

Verbatim: "Debt collectors may not harass you or anyone else over the phone or through
any other form of contact, including text or email."

On timing: collectors cannot contact a person "at an unusual time or place, or at a time
or place they know or should know is inconvenient to you."

Verbatim on state law: "Most states have laws about debt collection practices, many of
which are similar to the FDCPA... States also have unfair and deceptive acts and
practices laws that may apply to debt collection."
