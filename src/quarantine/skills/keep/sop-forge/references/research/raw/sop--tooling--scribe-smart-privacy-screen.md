# Smart Privacy Screen (Scribe Support Portal)

- **URL:** https://support.scribehow.com/hc/en-us/articles/24626046961437-Smart-Privacy-Screen
- **Related:** https://support.scribehow.com/hc/en-us/articles/7006819610909-Screenshots-Redact-Sensitive-Information
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (vendor product documentation)
- **Why archived:** Establishes that the leading capture-based SOP tool treats
  auto-redaction as a paid enterprise control rather than a default, and that its own
  documentation does not promise completeness or instruct manual verification. That is the
  evidence base for making redaction an explicit, mandatory, human-confirmed step rather
  than an assumed background feature.

## What it does

Quoted: "Smart Privacy Screen is a powerful screenshot redaction feature included in
Enterprise plans." It allows admins to "set account-level redaction for specific terms and
categories of sensitive information."

## Categories covered

The system can redact "common types of sensitive information by selecting from a list" and
provides "lists of types of private information by country." Admins can add custom terms
and custom data types.

## Timing

The documentation does not state whether redaction is applied at capture time or after
capture. It describes configuring "automatic redaction" without specifying the processing
point.

## Limitations

No formal limitations section is published. The article notes that "team-level redaction
criteria" is "included in select Enterprise plans," so availability varies by tier.

## Manual verification

The article does not instruct users to manually verify redactions after they are applied.
This is a documented absence, not a claim that verification is unnecessary.

## Plan gating

Smart Privacy Screen is Enterprise-only. Team-level redaction requires specific Enterprise
tiers.
