## Definition

Cognitive task analysis is "a collection of interview-based, qualitative methods ... used
to identify the cognitive processes and skills required to perform complex tasks" in
demanding work contexts.

## Named methods

**Critical Decision Method (CDM).** Retrospective interview technique. The expert reflects
on a challenging incident. Structured as four sequential "sweeps":

1. Incident identification
2. Timeline verification
3. Deepening probes
4. Hypothetical "what if?" questions

**Applied Cognitive Task Analysis (ACTA).** Three phases: task diagram development,
knowledge audit interviews, and data analysis and representation.

Additional techniques named: knowledge audits, cognitive demands tables, cognitive mapping.

## The tacit knowledge problem

The paper does not carry a specific numeric figure for omitted steps. It states that
"experts often find it difficult to reflect upon their own cognitive processes" and that
existing elicitation techniques inadequately capture unarticulated knowledge. CTA was
designed specifically to address that gap.

## Validation

Validation runs through CDM's sweep two: the interviewer narrates the account back to the
participant for correction. Analysis approaches named include thematic analysis, content
analysis, and grounded theory, cross-referencing insights across multiple interviews.
