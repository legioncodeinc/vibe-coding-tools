# Amazon KDP AI content guidelines

- **Title:** AI content guidelines (Kindle Direct Publishing help topic)
- **Publisher:** Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing
- **URL:** https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390
- **Published:** no date given on page
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (first-party platform policy)

## Why this source matters here

It is the clearest platform-level definition located of the line between AI-GENERATED and
AI-ASSISTED content, and the line is drawn exactly where this skill operates. It settles,
for at least one major platform, the question a user will ask: if an AI wrote it and I
fixed the tone, what is it?

## Findings

### The two definitions, verbatim

**AI-generated:** "AI-generated content as text, images, or translations created by an
AI-based tool. If you used an AI-based tool to create the actual content (whether text,
images, or translations), it is considered 'AI-generated,' even if you applied
substantial edits afterwards."

**AI-assisted:** "If you created the content yourself, and used AI-based tools to edit,
refine, error-check, or otherwise improve that content (whether text or images), then it
is considered 'AI-assisted' and not 'AI-generated.'"

### The disclosure rule

- **AI-generated content must be disclosed** when publishing new books or editing
  existing titles.
- **AI-assisted content must not be disclosed.** The guidelines state: "It is not
  necessary to inform us of the use of such tools or processes."

### Responsibility

Publishers must verify that all AI-generated and AI-assisted content complies with
content guidelines and intellectual property rights.

## Claims this source supports

1. At least one major publishing platform defines the categories by **who created the
   first draft**, not by how the finished text reads.
2. **Editing does not reclassify AI-generated content.** The policy says so explicitly:
   it remains AI-generated "even if you applied substantial edits afterwards."
3. A human-written draft that an AI tool edits, refines or improves is **AI-assisted**,
   and carries no disclosure obligation on this platform.
4. Therefore the two jobs this skill performs sit on OPPOSITE sides of this line. Tone
   correcting the user's own draft produces AI-assisted content. Tone correcting an
   AI-produced draft leaves it AI-generated, no matter how good the rewrite is.

## Limits of this source for our purpose

- One platform, book publishing. Does not govern social platforms, email, or client work.
- Undated page. Platform policy changes without announcement and must be re-checked.
- The policy governs disclosure to Amazon, not disclosure to readers, clients, or
  employers, each of whom may have their own expectation.
