# Amabile and Kramer's Progress Theory (study scale)

- **URL:** https://www.mindtools.com/arzm8fy/amabile-and-kramers-progress-theory/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** community and training vendor summary. Archived only for the study scale
  figures, which the HBR article text did not carry. Lowest rank in this archive.

## Study scale as reported here

- 238 participants
- 26 project teams
- 7 organizations
- over 12,000 anonymous daily diary entries

## Definition as reported here

> "when people consistently take steps forward, even small steps, on meaningful projects,
> they are more creative, productive, and engaged, and they have better relationships"

## The asymmetry

Also **not** quantified here. Two independent sources describing this work decline to put a
number on the setback-versus-progress asymmetry, so the distillation treats the asymmetry
as directional only.

## Reading for skill design

Use these numbers only to state the scale of the underlying study when the progress
principle is cited. Do not use this source for any claim about mechanism or effect size,
because it is a secondary summary with no statistics.
