# Format specifications per surface

Every figure in this guide is second-hand and dated. **No first-party platform documentation
was obtained in the research sweep for any surface**
(`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 7). Instagram's own help
page returned metadata with no body content. X's ranking repository was not independently
retrieved.

**So: treat every number here as a starting assumption to verify, not as a fact.** Where a
draft is about to be sized against a limit that matters, re-check it. Platform limits are
exactly the class of fact that changes without an announcement.

---

## The rule that governs every surface

**Each surface has a ceiling and a fold, and the fold governs the draft.**

The ceiling is where the platform rejects the post. The fold is where the reader decides
whether to keep reading. On LinkedIn the ceiling is 3,000 characters and the mobile fold is
around 140 (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2). The
ceiling is 21 times the fold. Writing to the ceiling and ignoring the fold is the standard
way a repurposed post fails.

A derivative is drafted to its fold first and its ceiling second.

---

## Ceilings

Character limits as reported by a scheduling product that enforces them at its API boundary
(`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2). The page carries no
date, which is a real weakness for this class of fact.

| Surface | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| X, free account | 280 |
| X, paid account | see the conflict note below |
| LinkedIn post | 3,000 |
| LinkedIn first comment | 1,250 |
| Facebook post and reel caption | 5,000 |
| Facebook first comment | 5,000 |
| Instagram post and reel caption | 2,200 |
| Instagram first comment | 2,200 |
| Instagram story caption | 120 |
| Threads post | 500 |
| Bluesky | 300 |
| TikTok | 2,200 |
| YouTube Shorts description | 5,000 |
| Pinterest | 500 |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 |

**Conflict on X paid tiers, unresolved.** One source states 25,000 for Basic, Premium and
Premium+ alike. Another states 4,000 for Premium and 25,000 for Premium+
(`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 6). Both are
second-hand and tiering changes with pricing. **Verify against the user's own account before
sizing an X draft.** Where verification is not possible, draft to 280 and let the user
expand, because a 280-character piece is publishable on every tier and a 4,000-character
piece is not.

**Media consumes character budget on X and LinkedIn** (same source, section 2). A draft
sized against text alone can still fail at post time with an image attached. Leave headroom
on any piece that ships with media.

## Folds

| Surface | Visible before truncation |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn feed, mobile | approximately 140 characters |
| LinkedIn feed, desktop | approximately 210 characters |
| LinkedIn About section | approximately 300 characters |
| LinkedIn headline in search results | approximately 60 to 70 characters |
| Email preview text, practical target | 40 to 90 characters, key message in the first 40 |

Source: `research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2.

**Named gap: no fold figures were located for Facebook, Instagram, or X.** Do not invent
them. Where a surface has no known fold, apply the LinkedIn mobile figure of roughly 140
characters as a working assumption, state that it is an assumption, and put the strongest
line first regardless, which is correct on every surface anyway.

### What this means for drafting

Draft the first 140 characters of every feed piece as if it were the entire piece. If those
140 characters do not stand alone as a complete thought worth having, the piece has spent
its only guaranteed surface on setup.

This is the same instruction that every craft source in the archive gives from a different
direction: each format needs its own hook, and copy-paste is the named failure
(`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 3). The opening-craft
taxonomy itself lives in the sibling skill's archive and is not restated here. See
`../../said-it-already/references/research/distilled-content-mining-and-repurposing.md`,
section 3.

## Per-surface notes

### LinkedIn

- Ceiling 3,000, mobile fold approximately 140, desktop fold approximately 210
  (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2).
- Line breaks render, and the first line before the fold is the entire decision surface.
- One vendor dataset of 372,126 posts from September 2025 to February 2026 reports posts in
  the 1,301 to 2,500 character range engaging 27% better than posts under 400 characters
  (same source, section 0 and section 2). **Do not use this as a reason to write long.** The
  analysis states no controls for account size, follower count, posting frequency, or topic,
  and the publisher sells a LinkedIn long-post tool. It is in this guide so the skill can
  say why it is not acting on it.
- Document posts, meaning carousels, are covered below.

### X

- Ceiling 280 free, paid tier disputed. See the conflict note above.
- **Link handling is a genuine differential constraint.** One reading of the published
  ranking code reports a 30% to 50% reach reduction for external links, and a separate
  analysis is cited for the claim that since March 2025 link posts from free accounts have
  had zero median engagement (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`,
  section 2). Both are second-hand. The common practical remedy is keeping the link out of
  the primary post.
- **No equivalent link evidence exists for any other platform in this archive.** Do not
  assert that LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram penalize links. That is a widely repeated
  claim with nothing behind it in this research.
- Ranking weights as read from the published code: a reply engaged by the author +75, a
  reply +13.5, a bookmark +10.0, dwell time of 2 minutes or more +10.0, a retweet +1.0, a
  like +0.5 (same source, section 2). If accurate, a reply is worth roughly 27 likes. That
  is the mechanical argument for a thread built to earn a genuine reply rather than a like.
  Attribute it as one publisher's reading of a repository, never as "the algorithm rewards".
- The same source's format claims, that text beats video by 30% and that long-form is now
  favored over threads, carry no attribution at all (same source). Not used.

### Facebook

- Ceiling 5,000 for posts and comments
  (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2).
- **No fold figure was located.** Named gap.
- The 5,000 ceiling is more than double Instagram's 2,200, which is the concrete reason a
  Facebook source post frequently cannot survive a straight paste to Instagram. That length
  gap is a format constraint that forces a rewrite, and a rewrite is the moment to change
  the angle rather than just cut words.

### Instagram

- Caption ceiling 2,200 for posts and reels, 120 for story captions
  (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2).
- **No fold figure was located.** Named gap.
- **No first-party Meta source is in this archive at all.** Every Instagram figure here is
  third-hand (same source, section 7).

### Email

The strangest surface, and the most constrained where it counts.

- **There is no fixed limit. There is a reader-configurable one.** What displays "ranges
  from zero to about five lines of text, roughly 278 characters at the top end, depending on
  the client and how the reader has configured their inbox"
  (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2).
- Per-client behavior (same source):

| Client | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Gmail | Subject line and preview text share ONE line of inbox space |
| Apple Mail on iPhone | Preview length is a reader setting, up to five lines |
| Outlook desktop | One to three lines |
| Outlook on the web | Three lines |

Two consequences that change what the skill produces.

1. **A long Gmail subject line eats the preview text entirely.** Subject and preview are not
   independent budgets there.
2. **Preview text is a separately authored field, not a truncation of the body.** An email
   derivative that does not specify its preview text has left its most constrained surface
   unwritten. **Every email in a content pack ships with an explicit subject line AND an
   explicit preview text line.** No exceptions.

Target 40 to 90 characters of preview text with the key message in the first 40 (same
source).

**Named gap: no subject-line length data with a stated method was located.** Many articles
claim to be data-backed and name no study. None were archived, and this skill states no
optimal subject length.

### Carousels and slide outlines

**LinkedIn document posts** (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`,
section 2):

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Formats accepted | PDF, PPTX, DOC, DOCX. PDF recommended for consistent rendering |
| Maximum file size | 100 MB |
| Maximum pages | 300 |
| Recommended slides | 5 to 15 |
| Portrait | 1080 x 1350 px, 4:5 |
| Square | 1080 x 1080 px, 1:1 |
| Landscape | 1920 x 1080 px, 16:9 |
| Minimum resolution | 300 DPI |
| Safe margin | 50 px from all edges |

Copy constraints from the same source: maximum 60 words per slide, minimum 24 pt body font,
no more than 6 to 8 lines per slide, one key point per slide. Cover slide carries a bold
claim or a data point rather than a title. CTA slide restates the takeaway with one call to
action.

**The planning budget that follows: 60 words across 5 to 15 slides makes a carousel a 300 to
900 word artifact.** A how angle that does not fit inside that is two carousels or an email.

Note the ceiling-versus-effective-limit pattern again: 300 pages allowed, 5 to 15 usable.

**Instagram carousels.** Reportedly expanded from 10 slides to 20, with individual per-slide
captions, rolled out 2026-06-19 (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`,
section 2). **This is third-hand reporting citing tech press, with no Meta source and no
first-party confirmation** (same source, section 6).

**Working rule: build to 10 slides unless the user verifies 20 in their own app.** Ten
renders correctly under both the old and the reported new behavior. If per-slide captions
are confirmed, an Instagram carousel becomes a sequence of copy units rather than one caption
plus images, which is structurally closer to a LinkedIn document post and should be outlined
the same way.

### Threads on X

- Practice guidance suggests 4 to 8 posts per thread
  (`research/distilled-repurposing-and-format-adaptation.md`, section 2). **Stated without
  any source attribution** in the original, and recorded as convention rather than finding.
- Each post in the thread is bounded by the account's per-post ceiling.
- The first post carries the whole decision. It has to stand alone.
- Given the reply and bookmark weighting above, a thread that ends on a genuine question the
  user will actually answer is structurally different from one that ends on a call to like.

### Short-video script or hook list, optional

No specifications were researched for video in this sweep, because the hook-craft literature
found was uniformly vendor content with no method. **Named gap.**

Where the user wants this piece, produce a script or hook list built on the ANGLE, keep it
under the character budget of whichever surface it ships on, and say plainly that the skill

## The per-piece format record

Every piece in a content pack carries this, so the user can check it before publishing:

```
Surface:        LinkedIn
Angle:          The counter-example
Character count: 612 of 3,000 ceiling
First 140 chars: [the exact text that appears above the fold]
Link handling:  no link in body, link in first comment
Media:          none
Verified:       ceiling and fold not re-verified this run, taken from research archive
                dated 2026-08-17
```

The `Verified` line is not optional. It is what stops a stale number from being presented as
a current one.
