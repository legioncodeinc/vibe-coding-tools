## Why this source is archived

Not for its claims. It is archived as the **exhibit** for the archive's central warning
about this domain: content repurposing statistics circulate as round, memorable,
unattributed numbers, and a skill that repeats them inherits their unreliability. This file
exists so the distillation can point at a concrete example rather than gesturing at "vendor
marketing" in the abstract.

## Claims this source supports

1. **Quarantine claim.** The headline performance statistics in this domain do not survive
   a provenance check. They are unfalsifiable as stated: there is no document to go read.
2. The specific figures 60%, 80%, and 92% are in wide circulation and should be treated as
   folklore until a primary source turns up. This skill does not repeat them and does not
   let them into a deliverable.
3. The gap this exposes is the important one: **no source found in this sweep measures
   whether a repurposed derivative outperforms, matches, or underperforms an original
   piece.** The question is treated as settled by everyone selling the service and has not
   been answered by anyone.
