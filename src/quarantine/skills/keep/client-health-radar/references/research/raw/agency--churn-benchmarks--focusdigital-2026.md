# Average Marketing Agency Churn: 2026 Report (Focus Digital)

- **Title:** Average Marketing Agency Churn: 2026 Report, Focus Digital
- **URL:** https://focus-digital.co/average-marketing-agency-churn/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog. **LOW TRUST. Archived as a negative finding about the state of
  the evidence, not as a source of usable benchmarks.**

## Why this source matters for client-health-radar

This is the most cited-looking agency churn benchmark set in the sweep, it is the source that
other agency-retention articles quote, and it discloses no methodology whatsoever. It is
archived to justify the skill's refusal to compare a user's clients against any published
agency churn benchmark.

## Extracted claims

**Numbers presented, by engagement model:**

| Model | Monthly churn | Annual churn | Average client lifespan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retainer | 1.6% | 18% | 56 months |
| Project-based | 4.2% | 42% | 24 months |
| Hybrid | 2.5% | 28% | 36 months |
| Performance-based | 3.1% | 33% | 30 months |

**By agency headcount:** 1 to 10 employees 32% annual churn; 11 to 25, 24%; 26 to 50, 19%;
51 and above, 15%.

**By specialization:** full-service 25%; SEO 38%; PPC 49%; social media 46%.

**Methodology disclosure, in full:** the page states "From January through April 2026, our
research team compiled an analysis". That is the entirety of it. Sample size, data source,
participant selection, survey instrument, and geographic scope are all absent. No external
citation supports any figure on the page.

## Direct implication for the skill

These numbers are downstream-cited by other agency retention content as though they were
survey results. They are not checkable. The skill therefore never tells a user that their
churn is above or below an industry average, and never uses a published benchmark to set a
threshold. Every threshold in this skill is derived from the user's own history with that
specific client, which is the only baseline with receipts.
