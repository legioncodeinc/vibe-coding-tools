## Scope and applicability

"The Code is not a corporate policy; it contains guidelines by which companies and
practitioners can set their own standards along the ethical spectrum." SCIP expects
members, partners, staff, and the entire community to adhere to these principles.

## The seven provisions, verbatim

1. **Elevate the Profession.** "To continually strive to increase the recognition and
   respect of the profession"
2. **Always in Compliance.** "To comply with all applicable laws, domestic and
   international"
3. **Transparent.** "To accurately disclose all relevant information, including one's
   identity and organization, prior to all interviews"
4. **Conflict-Free.** "To avoid conflicts of interest in fulfilling one's duties"
5. **Honest.** "To provide honest and realistic recommendations and conclusions in the
   execution of one's duties"
6. **Act as an Ambassador.** "To promote this Code of Ethics within one's company, with
   third-party contractors and within the entire profession"
7. **Strategically Aligned.** "To faithfully adhere to and abide by one's company
   policies, objectives and guidelines"

## Enforcement

SCIP reserves the authority to "revoke and terminate any membership, sponsorship or other
similar agreement at any time if we determine that one's activities are in direct
violation of the Code of Ethics."

## What this source does NOT say

The code contains no provision covering information that arrives unsolicited, information
observed incidentally in the ordinary course of business, or material seen during a
partner or client screen share. Provision 3 governs active elicitation (interviews).
Provision 2 governs legality. Provision 7 pushes the residual judgment back onto company
policy. The code is a floor, not a complete decision procedure for passive observation.
