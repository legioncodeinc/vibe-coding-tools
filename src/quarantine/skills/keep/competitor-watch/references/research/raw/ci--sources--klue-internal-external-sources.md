# 10 Best Sources of Competitive Intelligence (Internal and External)

- **URL:** https://klue.com/blog/sources-of-competitive-intelligence
- **Publisher:** Klue (competitive enablement vendor)
- **Author:** Adam McQueen
- **Published:** 2022-08-25, modified 2025-09-04
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog
- **Note on quotes:** dashes inside quoted passages normalized to spaced hyphens.

## Internal sources

| # | Source | What it reveals | Caveat given |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stakeholder interviews | Competitive priorities, knowledge gaps, existing talk tracks, positioning approaches | Tracy Berry recommends "30-in-30 interviews", thirty stakeholders for thirty minutes each |
| 2 | Internal messaging platforms | Real-time customer-facing interactions, pricing strategies, common objections, buyer feedback | "You cannot just take your stakeholder's word as gospel. Make sure you verify what gets shared in the channel." |
| 3 | Win-loss interviews | Why prospects chose you or a competitor, third-party objective buyer perspective | "Win-loss interviews offer an unparalleled opportunity to get honest, straightforward feedback from people who have been immersed in studying similarities between you and competitors." |
| 4 | CRM data and deal notes | Competitor presence in deals, win rates by competitor, quantified revenue impact per competitor | Enables threat analysis and the competitive revenue gap calculation |
| 5 | Call recordings | How messaging resonates, real talk tracks, what works in customer conversations | Conversational intelligence software can alert when competitors are mentioned |

## External sources

| # | Source | What it reveals | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | PR and news | Product releases, partnerships, M&A, messaging trends, strategic direction | Timing patterns, sponsored events, awards, customer focus shifts in funding announcements |
| 7 | Web content and social media | Blog frequency, messaging ownership, content strategy, gaps, brand positioning intent | Dave Barker: this "really tells you what they think and what they want the market to think of them" |
| 8 | Product, packaging, pricing | Feature comparisons, pricing changes, UX issues, roadmap hints | Analyst reports (Forrester, Gartner, IDC), advanced search, FAQs, support threads |
| 9 | Personnel and hiring | Growth indicators, strategic direction, organizational needs, C-suite shift implications | Job location, posting count, role types, posting duration, executive background changes |
| 10 | Customers and review websites | Competitor weaknesses, common pain points, credibility, UX issues | Includes Glassdoor and product review sites |

## Closing caveat

"Even the best intel in the world won't help you win more deals" unless it is converted
into actionable insight and activated through competitive enablement.
