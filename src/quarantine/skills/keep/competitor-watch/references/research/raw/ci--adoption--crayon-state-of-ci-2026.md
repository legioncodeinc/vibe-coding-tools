# The 2026 State of Competitive Intelligence

- **URL:** https://www.crayon.co/state-of-competitive-intelligence-2026
- **Publisher:** Crayon (competitive intelligence vendor)
- **Published:** 2026
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (vendor-run practitioner survey)
- **Note on quotes:** dashes inside quoted passages normalized to spaced hyphens.
- **Methodology caveat:** the page does not state sample size or survey method. Treat all
  figures as vendor-reported and directionally useful rather than as precise measures.

## Competitor tracking

- 70 percent of teams actively track 30 or fewer competitors.
- The largest single group tracks 11 to 30 competitors.

## Market competition trends

- 57.5 percent report more competitive deals than a year ago.
- Only 16 percent say competition eased.
- 49.6 percent saw competitive win rate increase over the past 12 months.
- 6.3 percent experienced declining win rates.
- 70 percent of teams say at least half their sales pipeline is competitive.
- Average rep readiness score: 6.3 out of 10.

## Sharing frequency, the cadence finding

- 56 percent share intelligence weekly, daily, or in real time.
- 79 percent revenue impact for weekly-or-faster sharing versus 41 percent for
  monthly-or-slower.
- 83 percent achieve revenue impact when updating deliverables weekly or faster.

## Tool adoption

- 66 percent run dedicated CI platforms, up from about 33 percent in 2022.
- 60.5 percent track specific KPIs, up from 30 percent in 2022.
- 80 percent use AI to generate compete content, up from 25 percent in 2024.
- 50 percent run AI agents in production or pilot.

## Revenue impact

- More than 56 percent of teams report positive revenue impact, more than double 2022.
- 82 percent revenue impact for teams using AI agents versus 42 percent without.

## Executive sponsorship

- 56.7 percent have a sales executive sponsor, flat for years.
- 77 percent revenue impact with a sponsor versus 44 percent without.
