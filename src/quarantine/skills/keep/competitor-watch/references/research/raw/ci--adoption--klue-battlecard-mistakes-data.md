## The four mistakes

1. **Lack of action-oriented content.** Only 43 percent of audited cards included talk
   tracks and only 19 percent provided supporting evidence. By contrast, 100 percent of
   the highest-retention battlecards contained both, demonstrating the "Know, Say, Show"
   method: context, talking points, proof.
2. **Missing revenue team input.** Organizations with immature competitive programs were
   three times less likely to collect feedback from sales teams in the field. Result:
   marketing-heavy language ("robust," "seamless") disconnected from how sellers actually
   speak.
3. **Absent prescriptive guidance.** Cards were 1.5 times less likely to include guidance
   on when and how to deploy competitive insight. Sellers need deal-stage and
   buyer-familiarity context before deploying competitive positioning.
4. **Insufficient proof points.** Only 35 percent of audited battlecards included
   customer-facing proof points.

## Baseline statistic

33 percent of deals are lost directly to competitors, and nearly 50 percent of those were
winnable.
