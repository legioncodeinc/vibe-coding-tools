## The four blind spots that cause failure

1. **Cookie-cutter approach.** Ignoring individual stakeholder needs across teams and
   individuals.
2. **Fragmented intelligence.** Failing to integrate disparate data sources and insights
   across the organization.
3. **Poor competitor identification.** Lacking the infrastructure to accurately track who
   competitors are in CRM.
4. **Over-reliance on tools.** Depending on CI software without human analysis.

## Statistics cited

- 42 percent of companies planned to grow CI headcount in 2022 (Crayon 2022 State of
  Competitive Intelligence Report).
- 35 percent average growth rate across sources, with Klue estimating 30 percent.
- 77 percent of companies reported CI as important or absolutely critical.
- 23 percent increase in companies calling CI "absolutely critical" over two years.
- 94 percent of companies claimed their market became more competitive.
- 89 percent of respondents experienced increased competitive landscape pressure (Product
  Marketing Alliance / Klue State of Competitive Enablement Report 2022).
- 83 percent reported markets becoming more crowded.

## On collection without analysis

Quoting Alok Vasudeva: "Collection without analysis reverts the analyst to the role of a
librarian." Raw data without human interpretation becomes noise.

## On ownership

Quoting Peter Mertens: "If competitive data is everyone's job, it's no one's job."

## Recommended fixes

Plan-Do-Check-Act applied to stakeholder alignment:

- **Plan:** understand business objectives, segment stakeholders.
- **Do:** one-on-one meetings on needs, objectives, pain points, competitor focus, and
  content preferences.
- **Check:** confirm current intel meets team needs, assess technical depth.
- **Act:** implement improvements iteratively.

For the cookie-cutter problem: tailor content by individual preference, geography, tenure,
and personal goals; different teams need different depth, technical detail, and action
items.

For fragmented intelligence: position the CI function as a "central hub" collecting intel
from all departments.

For poor competitor tracking, a six-step CRM fix: sales leadership buy-in, an internal
CRM champion, sales cycle pattern analysis, validation with sales leaders, comprehensive
multi-select competitor picklists, automated weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting.

For tool limitations: pair CI software with dedicated analysts and combine tooling with
human strategy to distinguish noise from actionable insight.
