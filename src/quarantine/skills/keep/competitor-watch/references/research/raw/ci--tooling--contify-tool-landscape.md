## The structural observation

Every tool in this list monitors a supplied set of external sources: competitor domains,
news feeds, search engines, review sites, or integrations into systems the company
already owns. The unit of configuration is a competitor name or a URL that the user
already knows to enter. Wide Narrow and Valona are the only two whose descriptions
include internal or field intelligence, and both require the organization to populate
that data deliberately.

## Alert fatigue and noise, named explicitly

- Google Alerts: "Hundreds of alerts", "unrelated information"
- WatchMyCompetitor: "Inaccurate alerts", customization difficulty
- Multiple platforms described as having overwhelming feature sets that limit usability
