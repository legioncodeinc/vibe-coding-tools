# Digital reminder nudges and psychological safety in global virtual teams

- **Title:** Fostering Psychological Safety in Global Virtual Teams: The Role of
  Team-Based Interventions and Digital Reminder Nudges
- **Journal:** Group Decision and Negotiation (Springer)
- **URL:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10726-024-09899-5
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** academic (peer-reviewed)

## Design and sample

- Survey-based design, not a controlled experiment.
- **235 participants** across global virtual teams.
- Builds on earlier work presented at HICSS-56 (Hawaii International Conference on System
  Sciences, 2023).

## Findings

- Headline result: "our data show that TBI and DRN foster psychological safety in GVT."
  Both team-based interventions (TBI) and digital reminder nudges (DRN) are associated
  with higher psychological safety.
- Important caveat stated by the authors: "only the effect of TBI on psychological safety
  can be explained with a higher-quality coordination process. It remains unclear what
  causal mechanism explains the effect of DRN."

## Gaps in this source

The accessible article content does not report nudge frequency, repetition strategy,
message framing variants, or evidence about diminishing returns from repeated nudges. The
specific question "how often can you nudge before it costs you" is **not answered by this
source or by any source located in this sweep**. Recorded as a named gap.

## Direct implication for the skill

- A reminder mechanism is not inherently corrosive to a working relationship. The
  association here runs the other way. That is a defensible basis for a skill that
  produces nudges at all.
- The authors' own admission that the causal mechanism for reminder nudges is unknown is a
  reason to keep the human in the loop rather than automating sends. The evidence supports
  nudging existing; it does not support any particular unattended nudging policy.
- The unanswered frequency question is why the skill escalates by changing channel and
  framing rather than by increasing frequency. Repetition is the variable with no
  supporting evidence, so the design avoids relying on it.
