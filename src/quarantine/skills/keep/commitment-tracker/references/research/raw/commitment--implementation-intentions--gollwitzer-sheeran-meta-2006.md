# Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-Analysis of Effects and Processes

- **Title:** Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement: A Meta-Analysis of Effects and Processes (Gollwitzer and Sheeran, 2006, Advances in Experimental Social Psychology vol. 38)
- **URL:** https://cancercontrol.cancer.gov/sites/default/files/2020-06/goal_intent_attain.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** academic (peer-reviewed book-series chapter, hosted by the US National Cancer Institute)

## Why this source matters for commitment-tracker

This is the foundational quantitative evidence that an intention alone does not produce
action, and that binding an intention to a specific cue does. A commitment ledger is a
mechanical version of the same idea: it converts "I said I would do that" into a named
item with a named owner, a named meeting, and a date.

## Extracted claims

- The meta-analysis covers **94 studies**. Implementation intentions produced "a
  medium-to-large effect on rates of goal attainment across the 94 studies included in
  the review (d = .65)."
- Broken down by problem type:
  - Getting started problems: **d = .61**
  - Getting derailed problems: **d = .77**
- The if-then structure is the active ingredient. The format is "If situation Y occurs,
  then I will initiate goal-directed response Z!" The paper reports that "the conditional
  structure of the implementation intention had a dramatic impact" on effectiveness.
- Two mechanisms are named:
  1. **Cue accessibility.** Forming the if-then plan means "the mental representation of
     this situation becomes highly activated, and hence more accessible", which lets
     people "identify and take notice of the critical cue."
  2. **Strategic automaticity.** Once the plan is set, "people do not have to deliberate
     anymore about when and how they should act."
- On goal intentions alone: goal intentions explained only "28% of the variance in
  behavior" across 422 studies. Experimentally changing goal intentions alone produced
  only "small-to-medium (R2 = .03)" behavioral effects.

## Direct implication for the skill

A meeting commitment captured only as spoken words is a bare goal intention: weak
predictor, roughly 28 percent of variance. A commitment written down with an owner, a
date, and a verification cue is closer to an implementation intention. The ledger's value
is not nagging. It is supplying the missing cue.
