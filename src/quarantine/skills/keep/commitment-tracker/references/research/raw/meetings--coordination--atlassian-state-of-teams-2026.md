# Atlassian State of Teams 2026

- **Title:** The State of Teams 2026
- **URL:** https://www.atlassian.com/blog/state-of-teams-2026
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor research report (large-sample survey, methodology disclosed,
  published by a collaboration-software vendor with a commercial interest in the finding)

## Methodology as stated

- **12,035 global knowledge workers** surveyed
- **173 Fortune 1000 executives** surveyed
- Fieldwork January to February 2026
- Plus qualitative interviews with 25 Fortune 500 executives

## Extracted statistics

### Coordination

- **87 percent of knowledge workers** report that when colleagues focus heavily on
  execution, they lack adequate capacity to coordinate effectively.
- **24 percent of leaders** prioritize using AI to improve teamwork, which implies 76
  percent are pointing AI at something other than coordination.

### AI adoption

- **85 percent of knowledge workers use AI at work**, but only **29 percent** have
  embedded it into regular workflows.
- **89 percent of executives** say AI increases speed, while just **6 percent** are
  certain they can demonstrate organization-wide return on investment.
- **55 percent of executives** believe AI is widening performance gaps between teams.

### Top-performing teams

High-performing teams, described as 14 percent of organizations, are:

- **5.6x** more likely to report AI supports improved planning and prioritization
- **9.4x** more likely to report AI strengthens collaboration
- **2.3x** more likely to trust AI for surfacing relevant, contextualized information

## Bias note

The publisher sells collaboration software. Findings that coordination is broken and that
AI should be pointed at teamwork are commercially convenient for the publisher. The sample
size and disclosed fieldwork window make it usable; the direction of the conclusions
should be read with the publisher's interest in mind.

## Direct implication for the skill

The 87 percent coordination-capacity finding, and the gap between 85 percent AI usage and
29 percent workflow embedding, are the market case for a skill that runs unattended on a
schedule rather than requiring the user to remember to ask. The 2.3x trust figure on
"surfacing relevant, contextualized information" is the argument for receipts: the teams
that get value are the ones that trust the surfacing, and trust comes from being able to
check the line.
