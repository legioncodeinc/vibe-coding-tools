# What is GTD: the five steps

- **Title:** What is GTD?
- **URL:** https://gettingthingsdone.com/what-is-gtd/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (the David Allen Company, the method's owner)

## Extracted content

The five steps of the Getting Things Done method, in the publisher's own framing:

1. **Capture.** "Collect what has your attention." Write, record, or gather everything
   into a collection tool.
2. **Clarify.** "Process what it means." Determine whether items are actionable, identify
   next actions and projects, or file as trash, reference, or someday/maybe.
3. **Organize.** "Put it where it belongs." Place reminders in the appropriate locations
   in the system.
4. **Reflect.** "Review frequently." Update and examine system contents to regain control.
5. **Engage.** "Simply do." Use the trusted system to make confident action decisions.

## Not on this page

This page does not define "next action" formally, does not state the two-minute rule, and
does not describe the Waiting For list or the Weekly Review in detail. Those are covered
by the Weekly Review checklist archived separately.

## Direct implication for the skill

commitment-tracker is a Clarify plus Reflect tool, not a Capture tool. Littlebird already did
Capture, and the meeting summary already did most of Clarify by emitting Action Items with
owners. The skill's job starts at Organize and Reflect: put the harvested items where they
belong across two columns, then review them on a cadence.
