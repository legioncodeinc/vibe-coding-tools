## Extracted content

**On what a stage is, quoted:**

Each stage "represents a step your deals go through before they're won or lost", helping
teams "track progress, forecast revenue and identify bottlenecks."

**On naming stages after observable actions, quoted:**

"Align stages with real actions, such as 'Meeting scheduled' or 'Proposal sent,' instead
of broad labels like 'In progress'."

**On pipeline design, quoted:**

"your pipeline should reflect your real sales process"

**On stage count, quoted:**

Create "fewer stages to keep your pipeline focused and easy to manage", because "too many
stages make the pipeline harder to scan and reduce adoption."

**Default pipeline:** Pipedrive ships a default five-stage pipeline that users customise.
This article does not enumerate the five default names.

## Claims this source supports

1. A vendor with a large SMB base explicitly recommends naming stages after OBSERVABLE
   ACTIONS ("Meeting scheduled", "Proposal sent") rather than internal judgments. This is
   the single most important design licence for a skill that infers stage from captured
   activity, because those are exactly the artifacts capture can see.
2. Stage count should be small, and vendor guidance ties high stage counts to reduced
   adoption.
3. A stage is defined as a step toward won or lost, not as a confidence level.
