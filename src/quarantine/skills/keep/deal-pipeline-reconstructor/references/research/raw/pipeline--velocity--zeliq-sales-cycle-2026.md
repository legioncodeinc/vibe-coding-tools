## Source-quality caveat

Vendor aggregation. Figures are attributed to RAIN Group and Forrester but the primary
reports were not fetched and the attributions are secondhand. Treat as directional claims.

## Extracted content

### Cycle length, attributed to a RAIN Group 2025 B2B Sales Cycle Benchmark

"the median B2B mid-market sales cycle in 2026 is 92 days, versus 68 days in 2019, a 35%
increase"

### By segment, attributed to RAIN Group and Forrester

| Segment | Stated duration |
|---|---|
| SMB | "30-45 days" |
| Mid-market | "60-92 days" |
| Enterprise | "6-9 months" |
| Strategic (above 500k ARR) | "9-18 months" |

### Typical duration per stage, as stated on the page

| Stage | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| Discovery | 7 to 14 days to confirm |
| Demo | 7 to 21 days after discovery |
| Technical evaluation | 7 to 14 days after demo |
| Proposal | 7 to 14 days for a reply |
| Negotiation | 14 to 30 days |
| Closing | 7 to 21 days |

### Silence decay, attributed to RAIN Group

"Beyond 14 days without interaction, close rate drops 38%"

No explicit threshold is given for when a deal becomes unlikely to close purely by age.

## Claims this source supports

1. Typical time-in-stage differs by stage, and the two shortest expected windows are
   discovery response and proposal response. A silence of the same length therefore means
   different things at different stages.
2. A 14-day silence is the most commonly cited inflection point in this archive, and it is
   cited in the direction of materially reduced close rate.
3. The SMB segment cycle is short, roughly 30 to 45 days, which means going-cold
   thresholds calibrated on enterprise cycles are far too slow for the solo operator this
   skill serves.
4. Cycle lengths have LENGTHENED over time, so older benchmarks understate current
   durations.
