## Source-quality caveat

A CRM vendor aggregating statistics about CRM. Unusually for the category it names an
originating source and year for most figures, which makes each claim traceable to a named
third party even though those primary reports were not fetched. One figure is
self-published by SuperOffice itself and is flagged below. Every number here is a CLAIM
attributed to a named party.

## Explicit absence

The page contains no statistic specific to small-business or solo-operator CRM usage. The
adoption figure it does give is scoped to "companies with more than 11 employees", which
EXCLUDES the majority of this skill's target users. That exclusion is itself informative:
the published adoption data does not describe the population this skill serves.

## Claims this source supports

1. The premise of this skill is corroborated at the mechanism level: manual data entry is
   claimed to consume a large fraction of a seller's week, and only about a third of a
   rep's time is claimed to go to selling. A solo operator resolves that tension by
   abandoning the CRM, not by working more hours.
2. CRM data quality is a widely reported problem even inside organisations that HAVE a
   CRM and staff to maintain it. The empty-CRM operator is the extreme case of a general
   pattern, not an outlier.
3. Published CRM adoption statistics are scoped to firms above 10 employees, so the
   sub-10-person segment is a documented data gap, not merely an under-served one.
