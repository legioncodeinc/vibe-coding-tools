## Source-quality caveat

Vendor-published research over the vendor's own customer base. Sample sizes are stated for
two of the five insights and omitted for three. The sample is self-selected: it consists of
companies that bought a revenue-intelligence platform, which skews toward larger, more
process-mature sales organisations than the solo operator this skill serves. Treat as
directional claims about mid-market and enterprise B2B, and do not transfer the magnitudes
to a one-person business.

## Claims this source supports

1. **The largest-sample finding in the archive is that won deals involve roughly twice as
   many buyer-side contacts as lost deals**, over 1.8 million opportunities. Contact COUNT
   on the buyer side is therefore a deal-health signal independent of stage, and it is one
   a capture record can partially observe: distinct named people appearing in the same
   company's threads and calls.
2. This gives a board a health column that does not depend on any inferred stage at all.
   Single-threaded is a reportable, evidence-backed risk flag.
3. The correlational limits must be stated. Bigger, later-stage, higher-value deals
   naturally involve more people, so contact count partly PROXIES for deal size and
   maturity rather than causing the win. Gong does not publish a causal design here. Report
   thread count as an observation, flag single-threading as a risk, and do not promise that
   adding a contact raises the win rate.
