## The framing that is worth more than the numbers

"The real deadline isn't the renewal date." The article's argument is that the notice
deadline, typically 30 to 90 days before renewal, is the operative date, and that a reminder
set two weeks before renewal is already too late to negotiate. Its recommendation is to
begin assessment at the notice deadline and add a 30-day buffer before that for internal
approvals.

## Reliability assessment

Weak. Two of seven figures carry a named source and neither was checked against the original
report. The 48% and 15% figures carry no attribution at all. The dollar range is an
enterprise figure and does not describe a solo operator or small agency.

Use this source for the **concept** (notice deadline, not renewal date) and not for the
numbers. The concept is independently corroborated by the Common Paper benchmark and the
CloudNuro timeline. The numbers are not.

## Notes for the sentinel

The lead-time argument is the entire thesis of this skill and this is the source that states
it plainly. Cite it for that. Do not quote its percentages in a user-facing deliverable
without the attribution caveat attached.
