## What the vacated 2024 rule required

Four obligations, as summarized by the source:

1. "separate, express consent to the negative option feature"
2. Prohibitions on misrepresentations about material aspects of the transaction
3. Restrictions on retention attempts made during a cancellation flow
4. A cancellation mechanism "at least as easy as the method used to enroll"

## The vacatur

- Vacated July 2025 by the Eighth Circuit.
- Reasoning as stated: "the FTC failed to conduct a preliminary regulatory analysis that was
  required because the Rule's economic impact exceeded the statutory threshold for major
  rules under Section 18 of the FTC Act."
- The court held the FTC's later analysis could not cure the procedural defect.

## The restarted rulemaking

- ANPRM submitted to OIRA on 2026-01-30.
- Announced 2026-03-11.
- Comment deadline 2026-04-13.
- Scope of the ANPRM: whether to amend the existing Negative Option Rule, regulatory
  alternatives, and certain provisions of the vacated Click-to-Cancel Rule.

## Enforcement authority that survives

The FTC continues to enforce under:

- ROSCA, the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act.
- Section 5 of the FTC Act.

Both are described as "fully operative".

## State law

Multiple states have strengthened disclosure, reminder, and cancellation requirements, and
some exceed the federal ROSCA baseline.

## Notes for the sentinel

The critical accuracy point for this skill: as of the fetch date there is **no federal
click-to-cancel rule in force.** The 2024 rule was vacated in full, a replacement is at the
advance-notice stage only, and the operative federal constraints are ROSCA and FTC Act
Section 5 plus whatever state law applies. A skill that tells a user "federal law requires
your vendor to make cancellation as easy as signup" would be wrong.
