## Case identification

- Case name: *Custom Communications, Inc. v. Federal Trade Commission*.
- Decision date: 2025-07-08.
- The source does not give a reporter citation, so this archive records the case name and
  date only. Do not invent a citation.

## Holding

The Eighth Circuit held that Section 18 of the FTC Act "mandates a separate preliminary
analysis in *any* case where the FTC issues an NPRM that surpasses the $100 million
threshold." The Commission had not done one before finalizing, despite record evidence that
compliance costs would exceed $100 million. The rule was vacated on that procedural ground.

Note what this is not: the court did not rule that the substance of the click-to-cancel
requirements was unlawful. It vacated for failure to follow the required rulemaking
procedure.

## What remains on the books

"State laws, as well as the original FTC rule and related regulations ... remain on the
books" and continue in effect. The 1973 Negative Option Rule was never vacated.

## Remaining enforcement

The FTC "has continued to file complaints and announce settlements related to businesses'
subscription ... practices, primarily under ROSCA and Section 5."

## Notes for the sentinel

Timing detail worth carrying: the vacatur landed days before the rule's 2025-07-14
compliance date. Vendors that had already rebuilt their cancellation flows for compliance
mostly kept them, but that is a business choice and not a legal obligation, so a user
cannot rely on it.
