# CloudNuro: SaaS Renewal Process, Contract Negotiation and Timeline Guide

- **Title:** SaaS Renewal Process: Contract Negotiation & Timeline Guide
- **URL:** https://www.cloudnuro.ai/blog/saas-renewal-process
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (SaaS management platform vendor; commercially interested in renewal management being hard)
- **Publication date:** 2026-02-27 (last updated)

## The recommended renewal timeline

| Stage | Window before renewal | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory | 120 days and earlier | "Identify all contracts expiring in the next 120-180 days" |
| Analysis | 90 to 120 days | Usage reports, vendor research |
| Engagement | 60 to 90 days | Formal vendor contact, quote requests |
| Negotiation | 30 to 60 days | Active rounds; "Most enterprise negotiations require 2-4 rounds" |
| Execution | 0 to 30 days | Final approvals and signature |

## Auto-renewal notice windows

"Auto-renewal clauses automatically extend your contract unless you provide written notice
30-90 days before expiration."

## Price movement

- "SaaS vendors raised prices an average of 12-18% in 2024".
- A worked example in the article uses a 22% increase on a Salesforce renewal.

## Savings and utilization

- Organizations following a structured renewal process achieve "15-30% on renewal costs".
- "Studies show 30-40% of SaaS licenses go unused or underutilized". The underlying studies
  are not named.

## What the source does not give

Statistics on how often companies miss deadlines or auto-renew unintentionally.

## Notes for the sentinel

This source is the reason the horizon is 90 days rather than 30. At 30 days out, the
negotiation stage has already started or passed and the notice window on a 30-day contract
is closing that day. At 90 days out, every stage is still available.

Treat the 12% to 18% price figure as a vendor estimate about an enterprise cohort. It is not
a solo-operator number and the article does not name a sample.
