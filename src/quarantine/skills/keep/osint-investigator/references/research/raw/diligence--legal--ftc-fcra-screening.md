# What Employment Background Screening Companies Need to Know About the Fair Credit Reporting Act

- **URL:** https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/what-employment-background-screening-companies-need-know-about-fair-credit-reporting-act
- **Publisher:** US Federal Trade Commission
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** official-docs (US federal regulator)

## What a "consumer report" is

A background report qualifies as a consumer report under FCRA when it:

- serves as a factor in determining eligibility for "employment, credit, insurance, housing, or other purposes", AND
- includes information "bearing on a consumer's credit worthiness, credit standing, credit capacity, character, general reputation, personal characteristics, or mode of living".

## What a "consumer reporting agency" is

Companies that sell or provide such reports constitute consumer reporting agencies under FCRA **regardless of how they self-identify**. The FTC guidance stresses that a screening firm meets the definition even if it does not consider itself a traditional credit bureau.

## What triggers coverage

Coverage attaches by the act of providing information about individuals to employers for hiring or other employment decisions.

## Obligations once covered

- Maintain "reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy".
- Obtain client certifications confirming proper use.
- Provide FCRA responsibility notices and consumer rights summaries.
- Honor subject rights: file access, dispute investigation, timely response.
- Follow special procedures for public record information.

## Relevance to a person dossier

Two load-bearing points. First, the definitional trigger is about **furnishing reports to third parties for eligibility decisions**, not about the act of researching. A user assembling a brief for their own negotiation is not thereby a consumer reporting agency. Second, the "character, general reputation, personal characteristics, or mode of living" phrase is a strong warning label about what a dossier starts to look like once it drifts past business facts, and it is the phrase to keep in mind when deciding what to exclude by construction.
