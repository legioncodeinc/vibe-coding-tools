## Ten types of due diligence

Financial, Legal, Operational, Enhanced (EDD), Customer (CDD/KYC), Vendor (VDD), Third-party, **Management (IDD, investigation of key individuals and directors)**, ESG, Investment.

## Triggers for enhanced due diligence

- Subject is a politically exposed person.
- Counterparty is in a high-risk jurisdiction.
- Transactions lack clear economic rationale.
- Beneficial ownership is hard to verify.

The Money Laundering Regulations 2017 mandates EDD for these categories in the UK.

## What EDD on an individual covers

- Source of wealth and funds investigation.
- Adverse media screening, including non-English content, across a claimed 40,000 outlets.
- **Network analysis**: "Network analysis is particularly useful here: it maps connections between individuals and entities" that single-source checks miss.
- Senior management approval before onboarding.
- Enhanced ongoing monitoring.
- Corporate structure and beneficial ownership analysis.

## Checklist framework

Identity verification with reliable independent sources; beneficial ownership; source of wealth; purpose and nature of the relationship; sanctions and PEP screening; adverse media including archived and non-English; regulatory compliance; ongoing transaction monitoring; documentation with audit trail.

## 2026 regulatory drivers named

- EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive, phased from July 2027.
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 raising supply chain risk to board level.
- FCA Financial Crime Guide update, April 2025, on dynamic customer risk profiling and third-party vendor management.
- "New global sanctions designations increased by 50% between 2022 and 2024. Screening that was adequate three years ago may not be adequate today."

## Relevance to a person dossier

Establishes **Management / Individual Due Diligence (IDD)** as a named, standard category, which is the professional bucket this skill's output sits in. Two named EDD components are deliberately out of scope for this skill: source of wealth is financial detail, and PEP and sanctions screening is a regulated function requiring licensed data. The skill names both as boundaries and tells the user where a real EDD provider takes over. **Network analysis** IS adopted, in the narrow form the evidence standards permit: the Littlebird capture shows who appeared around the subject, which is a genuine relationship map, and the third-party rule keeps it limited to what the stated purpose needs.
