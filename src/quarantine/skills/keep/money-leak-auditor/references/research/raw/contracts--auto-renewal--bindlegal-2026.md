# Bind Legal: Auto-Renewal Clauses, What Is Standard (2026)

- **Title:** Auto-Renewal Clauses: What's Standard (2026)
- **URL:** https://bindlegal.com/resources/guides/auto-renewal-clause/
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (contract tooling vendor, citing the Common Paper benchmark)
- **Publication date:** 2026-06-16

## Extracted figures

Notice periods:

- 30 days: most common in standardized SaaS agreements, around 84% per the Common Paper
  benchmark.
- 60 days: "a common, more buyer-protective target in negotiated enterprise deals".
- 90 days: considered aggressive, because it "forces a decision before you can evaluate
  the term".

Renewal structure:

- Fixed renewal terms equal to the initial term provide reassessment points.
- Evergreen clauses roll indefinitely until cancellation. The guide recommends avoiding
  them and preferring "fixed annual renewals over evergreen or multi-year re-locks".

Price increase caps:

- Buyer-protective standard: the lesser of 5% or CPI, applied once, with a ceiling on
  the CPI figure.
- Average annual increase vendors seek: approximately 12%.

US state auto-renewal statutes referenced:

- New York General Obligations Law 5-903
- Wisconsin Section 134.49
- Colorado, extended to B2B effective 2026-02-16

The guide notes most US auto-renewal laws are consumer-only, so B2B protection comes
mainly from negotiated contract terms.

## Negotiation advice, as stated

1. Raise renewal-pricing protection before discount discussions.
2. Trade longer commitments for lower price caps.
3. Begin renewal conversations 90 to 120 days before expiration.

## Notes for the auditor

The 12% typical vendor-sought increase versus the 5%-or-CPI buyer-protective cap gives a
concrete threshold for the price-increase sentinel: a captured renewal notice showing an
increase above roughly 5% is worth surfacing, and one above 12% is above even the
vendor-side norm.

The 30-day standard notice period sets the urgency rule for the action pack: a vendor
whose renewal is under 30 days out may already be past the cancellation window.
