# BetterCloud: The big list of 2026 SaaS statistics

- **Title:** The big list of 2026 SaaS statistics that you should know
- **URL:** https://www.bettercloud.com/monitor/saas-statistics/
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (aggregator, restating Gartner and its own State of SaaSOps data)
- **Publication date:** 2026-02-24, updated 2026-04-14

## Extracted figures

Apps per organization:

- 106 average SaaS apps per company in 2024, down from 112 in 2023.
- Consolidation rate slowed to 5% year over year, down from 14%.

Shadow IT:

- 75% of employees expected to acquire, modify, or create technology outside IT
  oversight by 2027.
- 55% of employees adopting SaaS applications without security involvement.
- 30% to 40% of IT spending in large organizations classified as shadow IT.

Spend and growth:

- Global SaaS market projected at $300 billion in 2025.
- 19.1% YoY growth in global SaaS end-user spending.
- 18.7% CAGR in global SaaS revenue through 2030.

Unused and redundant:

- 63% of organizations report too many unused or underutilized SaaS apps, which is
  driving consolidation.
- 25% overspending expected by 2027 from unused entitlements and overlapping tools.
- 33% of organizations consolidated redundant apps in 2025.
- 65% of total SaaS apps are IT-managed and supported.
- 70% or more of organizations will centralize SaaS management on a platform by 2028,
  up from under 30% in 2025.

## Notes for the auditor

The 30% to 40% shadow-IT share of spend is the headline number justifying a
capture-based audit: that share is by definition not in the procurement system. The
forward-looking figures (2027, 2028) are analyst projections, not measurements, and
should be cited as projections if used at all.
