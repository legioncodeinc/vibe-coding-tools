# Vertice: Unused SaaS Applications

- **Title:** Unused SaaS applications
- **URL:** https://www.vertice.one/insights/unused-saas-applications
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (SaaS purchasing platform, reporting from its own processed spend)
- **Publication date:** 2026-07

## Methodology as stated

Derived from over $75 billion in globally processed spend managed by Vertice during 2026.

## Extracted figures

- 65% of all SaaS licenses are either entirely unused or underutilized as of Q2 2026,
  up from 62% the previous year.
- Shelfware, meaning completely unused: 14% of licenses, down from 15% year over year.
- Underutilized, defined as under 50% of purchased seats in use: 51% of licenses, up
  from 47%.
- Combined waste peaked at 69% in December 2025 before declining to 65% by June 2026.

## Key argument

Underutilization, not abandonment, is the growing driver. Vertice's framing:
"right-sizing underutilized tools is one of the single largest savings opportunities
available without cutting a single tool entirely."

## Notes for the auditor

Vertice's split of shelfware (14%) from underutilized (51%) is the most useful
decomposition found in this archive, because it maps directly onto two different
actions: cancel versus downgrade. A skill that only produces a cancel list is addressing
the smaller of the two buckets.

Caution: the "under 50% of purchased seats" definition of underutilized is seat-based
and does not transfer cleanly to a single-seat solo operator, where the equivalent
question is tier utilization rather than seat utilization.
