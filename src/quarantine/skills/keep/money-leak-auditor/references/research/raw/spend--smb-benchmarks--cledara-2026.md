# Cledara: Average SaaS Spend Per Employee in 2026

- **Title:** Average SaaS Spend Per Employee in 2026: The Definitive Benchmark
- **URL:** https://www.cledara.com/blog/average-saas-spend-per-employee-2026
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (SMB-focused SaaS management and card platform, reporting its own transaction data)
- **Publication date:** 2026-03-25

## Extracted figures

Per-company annual spend by region:

- US: $349,000 annually for the reference company size
- UK: $209,000 annually
- Europe: $167,000 annually

Per-employee monthly spend by company size:

- Startup around 10 people: roughly $150 per employee per month
- 50 to 500 people: $250 to $350 per employee per month
- Enterprise: rises again because of premium tiers and specialized tools

Active subscriptions:

- Median company: 25 active subscriptions
- Top 10% of companies: 49 or more subscriptions
- New subscriptions added per month, April 2025: 53
- New subscriptions added per month, March 2026: 401, a 7x increase

Spend by category:

| Category | Share |
|---|---|
| Collaboration and productivity | 34.3% |
| Engineering tools | 16% |
| Marketing technology | 13.3% |
| Sales, finance, HR, other combined | 36.4% |

AI adoption:

- April 2025: AI was 8.8% of purchases
- March 2026: AI was 26.4% of purchases
- Cledara's recommended allocation: 15% to 25% of new SaaS spend toward AI tools

## Notes for the auditor

This is the closest thing in the archive to a small-operator benchmark. The median of
25 active subscriptions is the right order of magnitude to expect from a solo operator
or small agency ledger, and a reconstructed ledger far below that count is a signal the
capture sweep was incomplete rather than a signal the user is lean.

The 8.8% to 26.4% jump in AI share of purchases within eleven months is the single most
relevant trend line for a 2026 audit: AI tooling is where new, unbudgeted, duplicated
spend is concentrating.

Caution: the regional per-company figures and the per-employee figures are not
reconcilable from the text alone. Cite them separately, do not multiply them together.
