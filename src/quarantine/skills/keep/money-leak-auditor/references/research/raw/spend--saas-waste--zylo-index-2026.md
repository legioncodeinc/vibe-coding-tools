# Zylo 2026 SaaS Management Index (press release)

- **Title:** Zylo's 2026 SaaS Management Index Finds AI-Native App Adoption Is Surging, with ChatGPT Now the Most Expensed App
- **URL:** https://zylo.com/news/2026-saas-management-index
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (SaaS management platform vendor, primary data holder)
- **Publication date:** 2026-01-29

## Methodology as stated

Analysis of 40 million SaaS licenses and $75 billion in SaaS spend, plus a survey of
218 IT leaders. Beyond the sample size, survey methodology is not disclosed in the
release.

## Extracted figures

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| SaaS spend per employee | $9,455 median |
| License waste / underutilization | 36% average unused licenses |
| AI-native app spend growth | 393% YoY at enterprises over 10,000 employees; 108% overall |
| Monthly app turnover | 21 applications added monthly at large enterprises |
| Spend control | 81% controlled by business units, 15% by IT |
| Expense-based SaaS growth | 267% YoY increase |
| Unexpected cost incidents | 78% of IT leaders reported unplanned charges |
| Budget disruption | 61% forced to cut projects because of cost increases |
| Most expensed app | ChatGPT |

## Notes for the auditor

- "Expense-based SaaS" means software bought on a personal or corporate card and
  expensed rather than procured. Its 267% YoY growth is the structural reason a
  screen-capture audit finds spend that no finance system holds.
- The 81% business-unit / 15% IT split is the enterprise analogue of a solo operator
  buying everything on one card with no procurement step at all.
- 78% reporting unplanned charges supports treating surprise charges as a normal
  condition, not an anomaly.
