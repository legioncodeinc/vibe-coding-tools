# Duplicate SaaS Subscriptions: The 2026 Benchmark

- **Title:** Duplicate SaaS Subscriptions: The 2026 Benchmark
- **URL:** https://coommit.com/blog/duplicate-saas-subscriptions-2026-benchmark
- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17
- **Source type:** vendor-blog (secondary aggregation, lower rank than a primary data holder)
- **Publication date:** 2026-04-25

## Extracted figures

- Average apps per enterprise: 305 applications.
- Overlapping apps: 30% to 40% of the total stack, meaning 90 to 120 redundant
  applications per enterprise.
- Unused licenses: 51% of SaaS licenses remain unused.
- Duplicate share of spend: "15% of that spend is sitting on duplicate SaaS
  subscriptions", around $8.4M annually for a median enterprise at $55.7M total spend.
- Total recoverable waste: 15% to 30% of total SaaS budget through duplicates plus
  unused licenses.
- Consolidation savings potential: $120K to $225K annually for 200-person scale-ups;
  $4.5M to $5.6M for larger enterprises when AI premium elimination is included.

## Most commonly duplicated categories, as listed

1. Communication: Slack, Teams, Zoom, Meet, Loom, Discord
2. AI assistants: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity
3. Project management: Asana, Notion, Jira, Linear, ClickUp, Monday
4. Note-taking and meeting AI: Otter, Fathom, Fireflies, Granola, native tools
5. Whiteboards and canvas: Miro, FigJam, Mural, Lucid, Excalidraw

## Conflict to record

This source states 51% of licenses are "unused". Vertice
(raw/spend--shelfware--vertice-unused-2026.md) uses 51% for "underutilized" and 14% for
entirely unused. The number appears to have been copied across a definitional boundary.
Prefer the Vertice split, which defines its terms, over this restatement.

## Notes for the auditor

The five duplicate categories are the most directly usable artifact in this file. They
transfer to a solo operator almost unchanged, because the categories are functional, not
headcount-dependent. Use them as the seed cluster list for duplicate detection.
