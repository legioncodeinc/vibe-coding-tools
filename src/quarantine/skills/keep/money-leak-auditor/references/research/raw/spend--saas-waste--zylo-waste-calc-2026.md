- **Title:** How Much Is Wasted on SaaS Spend? Learn How to Identify and Stop Waste

- **Fetch date:** 2026-08-17

## Extracted figures

Annual waste by organization size:

| Employees | Wasted annually |
|---|---|
| 1 to 500 | $3.8M |
| 501 to 2,500 | $9.5M |
| 2,501 to 10,000 | $29.8M |
| 10,001 or more | $80.6M |
| Average organization | $19.8M |

License utilization:

- "the average organization only uses 54% of its SaaS licenses", leaving 46% unused.
- High performers reach a utilization rate of at least 90%.

Waste definition used: licenses not driving value for the organization, which "often
fly under the radar as shadow IT."

## Conflict to record

This page says 46% of licenses are unused. The same vendor's own index press release
(raw/spend--saas-waste--zylo-index-2026.md) says 36%. Same vendor, same index, same
year, two different denominators. Treat both as vendor estimates of an unstandardized
quantity, not as a measured constant.

## Notes for the auditor

The 1-to-500-employee band still shows $3.8M of annual waste, which is a band average
dominated by the top of the range. Do not apply enterprise dollar averages to a solo
operator or a five-person agency. Use the percentage findings, not the dollar findings,
when reasoning about small operators.
