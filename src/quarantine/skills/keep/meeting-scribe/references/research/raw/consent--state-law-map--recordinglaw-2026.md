## Federal baseline

Quoted: "The Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA), codified at 18 U.S.C. Section
2511, prohibits intentionally intercepting wire, oral, or electronic communications."

Stated federal exposure: up to 5 years imprisonment, $250,000 fines, civil damages of
$10,000 per violation.

## The capability test

The guide reports that courts are applying a standard from *Ambriz v. Google* under which
vendors with the capability to use intercepted data for independent purposes, such as
model training, may be treated as unauthorized third-party interceptors regardless of
one-party consent rules.
