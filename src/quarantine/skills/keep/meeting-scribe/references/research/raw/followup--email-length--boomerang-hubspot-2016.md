# What is the ideal length of a sales email? Insights based on 40 million emails

- **Title:** What is the ideal length of a sales email? Insights based on 40 million emails
- **Publisher:** HubSpot Sales Blog, reporting a Boomerang study
- **URL:** https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/ideal-length-sales-email
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Published:** February 2016, updated 2025-07-28
- **Source type:** vendor-blog reporting a vendor dataset. The underlying study is
  Boomerang's, not HubSpot's. Sample is large and the numbers are reported, which puts it
  above ordinary content marketing, but the genre is sales email and not meeting recap.

## Note on window

The underlying data is from 2016, well outside the six month default window. It is
retained because it is the only large-sample word count dataset the sweep found, and
because every 2026 result for the same question was cold-outreach content marketing
recycling this same study without attribution. Age is flagged, not hidden.

## The study

- Sample: 40 million emails
- Reported optimal range: 50 to 125 words

## Response rate by word count

| Words | Response rate |
|---|---|
| 10 | 36% |
| 25 | 44% |
| 50 | 50% |
| 75 | 51% |
| 100 | 51% |
| 125 | 50% (top of the stated optimal band) |

The curve is flat between roughly 50 and 125 words. The penalty is at the very short end.

## Reading level

Quoted: "third-grade level emails performed 36% better in terms of open rate than those
written at a college reading level and boasted a 17% higher response rate than emails
composed at a high school reading level."

## The genre limitation, stated plainly

The article does not specify whether the studied emails were cold outreach or
existing-relationship communications. Given the publication context, sales outreach is
the likely majority.

A post-meeting recap is a different genre. The recipient just spent 30 minutes talking to
the sender, already knows who they are, and is not deciding whether to reply to a
stranger. Response rate is not even the right success metric for a recap, whose job is
shared record and follow-through.

## Direct implication for the skill

The 50 to 125 word band is the best available anchor and it corroborates the Granola
"under 150 words" claim to within one band width. Use the range as a soft ceiling for
prose, and hold action items in a bulleted block that does not count against it. Do not
present either number to the user as an established fact about meeting recaps, because
neither source measured meeting recaps.
