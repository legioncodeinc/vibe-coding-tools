# AI and you: confidentiality risks of using transcription and note-taking software

- **Title:** AI and You: Confidentiality Risks of Using Transcription and Note-Taking
  Software During Meetings
- **Author:** Gabriel Buehler
- **Publisher:** American Bar Association, GPSolo eReport
- **URL:** https://www.americanbar.org/groups/gpsolo/resources/ereport/2025-september/ai-you-confidentiality-risks-meeting-transcription-note-taking-software/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Published:** September 2025
- **Source type:** professional association publication. Editorially reviewed, legal
  profession framing, advisory rather than empirical.

## Three confidentiality threats named

1. **Third-party access.** Quoted: "AI note-taking tools typically process audio in the
   cloud, meaning a third party gains access to otherwise confidential attorney-client
   communications."
2. **Permanent discoverable records.** AI notetakers "could expose sensitive conversations
   to legal discovery and dramatically expand the scope of discoverable material."
3. **Training data leakage.** Quoted: "Most commercial AI systems train on user-provided
   inputs. This introduces the possibility that privileged content could be reproduced or
   leaked."

## Recommendations

- Firm-wide policies and training that "prohibit automatic AI use in privileged meetings"
- "Transparently disclose AI use during privileged communications, explaining potential
  risks, and obtain explicit informed consent"
- Prefer on-premise systems, or vendors providing "end-to-end encryption" and "immediate
  deletion after use"

## What this source does NOT address

Asked directly, the piece says nothing about accidental disclosure to the wrong recipient
and nothing about automatic sharing of summaries with all participants. Its threat model
is vendor-side, not recipient-side. The recipient-side risk is covered in this archive
only by the MLT Aikins piece.

## Named incidents

None. The piece references no specific breach.

## Direct implication for the skill

The "permanent discoverable record" framing is the one that generalizes past law practice.
A recap that quotes a call verbatim converts a conversation into a durable written artifact
held by everyone who receives it. That is the point of decision durability, and it is also
the reason the durability has to be a deliberate choice rather than a side effect. The
skill states what it is creating before it creates it.
