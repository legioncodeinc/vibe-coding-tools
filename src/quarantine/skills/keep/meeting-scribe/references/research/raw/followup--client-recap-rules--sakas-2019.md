# 3 rules for better client meetings: agenda, recap, and follow-through

- **Title:** 3 rules for better client meetings: Agenda, recap, and follow-through
- **Author:** Karl Sakas, Sakas and Company (agency operations consultant)
- **URL:** https://sakasandcompany.com/better-client-meetings/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Published:** 2019-01-23, updated 2022-05-28
- **Source type:** practitioner consulting blog. Advice from a specialist in agency
  operations, not a study.

## Note on window

Outside the six month window. Retained because it is the clearest practitioner statement
of the client-facing recap norm found in the sweep, and because the 2026 results for the
same query were notetaker vendor content marketing.

## The rules

Quoted: "Always have an **agenda**, even if it's an informal agenda" and "Always send a
prompt post-meeting **recap**, including a summary of decisions and each person's
actionables."

## What the recap contains

Quoted: "a summary of **decisions** and **actionables**, including an 'owner' and deadline
for each actionable."

## The per-person split

Where multiple parties have tasks, Sakas recommends splitting actionables "into
sub-sections, so that each person has their 'own' actionables list."

This is a split BY OWNER inside one document sent to everyone. It is not the same as
sending different documents to different parties, which the piece does not address.

## Timing

Quoted: "Soon! I email a copy of the actionables after each call (immediately, or within
one business day)."

And: "The longer you wait, the less relevant it becomes."

## Who sends it

The account or project manager who owned the meeting, by email, with the content also
recorded in a project management system.

## What the piece does NOT cover

It gives no guidance on what to exclude from a client-facing recap. Asked directly, the
text is silent on the internal-versus-shareable filter. That is a named gap and the skill
must source that rule elsewhere.

## Direct implication for the skill

Two structural claims are usable: decisions plus owned actionables is the minimum content
of a recap, and the owner split is per person. The exclusion rule has to come from the
confidentiality sources, not from here.
