# Noteworthy concerns: discussing the risks of AI note-taking apps

- **Title:** Noteworthy concerns: Discussing the risks of AI note-taking apps
- **Publisher:** MLT Aikins LLP (Canadian law firm insight piece)
- **URL:** https://www.mltaikins.com/insights/noteworthy-concerns-discussing-the-risks-of-ai-note-taking-apps/
- **Fetched:** 2026-08-17
- **Published:** 2025-05-13
- **Source type:** law firm client advisory. Editorially reviewed by lawyers, written to
  be cautious, Canadian legal framing. Not a study, and not US law.

## Privilege and confidentiality

Quoted: "It is unclear whether enlisting the help of an AI note-taking app to record and
transcribe a privileged conversation is the same as disclosing the information to a third
party."

The honest answer here is unsettled, and the piece says so rather than asserting.

## Inadvertent transmission

Quoted: "Data collected by an AI note-taking app can be inadvertently sent to certain
parties" through technical error or misconfiguration, potentially exposing confidential
information to unauthorized recipients.

## Automatic distribution to attendees

The piece states that summaries and transcripts are "circulated to attendees by the AI
note-taking app," which can share privileged material with every call participant.

This is the mechanism the skill exists to interrupt. The default behavior of the tooling
category is to push the internal artifact outward, unedited, to everyone in the room.

## Three categories of inaccuracy named

1. Apps record immaterial detail alongside material detail, so the record does not
   distinguish importance.
2. Apps "fill in the blank" with an incorrect guess rather than marking a passage
   inaudible. The failure is silent, which is what makes it dangerous.
3. Apps fabricate. The example given is an app inventing a meeting with the Prime Minister
   out of unrelated small talk.

## Recommended safeguards

- Consider handwritten or typed notes instead
- Read the app's terms of service and privacy policy before use
- Obtain explicit approval from all meeting participants before enabling the app

## Direct implication for the skill

Two rules follow directly. First, no summary content goes outward without a human reading
it, because the summary may contain a silent invention and the invention will read as
fluent and confident. Second, the outbound artifact is a separate document authored for a
specific audience, not the internal record forwarded. The category's default is the
forward, and the default is the defect.
