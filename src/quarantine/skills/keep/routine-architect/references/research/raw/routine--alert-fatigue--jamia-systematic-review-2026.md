## Methods

A systematic review of systematic reviews. Searched Cochrane Library, Embase, and PubMed
from database inception through 2024. 22 systematic reviews met inclusion criteria. Two
independent reviewers screened and extracted with standardized tools. Quality assessed with
Revised Assessment of Multiple Systematic Reviews (R-AMSTAR).

## Definition of alert fatigue

Only one included study gave an explicit operational definition. That definition: alert
fatigue occurs when "excessive and/or irrelevant alerts lead recipients to ignore,
overlook, or override alerts" without appropriate engagement with the context.

The review team proposed a measurable definition: "a statistically significant, sustained
decrease in appropriate response rates over time relative to a previously established
baseline."

That is a definition of fatigue as a **trend**, not a level. The signal is engagement
falling over time, not any single ignored alert.

## What the literature actually measures

| Metric | Share of the 22 reviews reporting it |
|---|---|
| Alert quantity | 54.5% (12) |
| Override rate | 45.5% (10) |
| Acceptance rate | 45.5% (10) |
| Appropriateness / positive predictive value | 31.8% (7) |

Only 3 of 22 reviews (13.6%) reported rates of appropriate versus inappropriate overrides,
which is the distinction that separates a bad alert from a bad responder.

One study example noted: drug-drug interaction alerts had "the highest inappropriate
override rates."

## Evidence on interventions

Intervention evidence is limited. The review states that interventions "directed toward
proxies of alert fatigue may be ineffective if alert fatigue is not actually present." In
other words, tuning an alert stream because override rates look high can miss, because a
high override rate can mean the alerts are wrong rather than that the human is fatigued.

## Quality caveat

Study quality was predominantly medium (81.8%). Only one review achieved high quality.
Reporting of primary study characteristics was insufficient in 86.4% of reviews.
