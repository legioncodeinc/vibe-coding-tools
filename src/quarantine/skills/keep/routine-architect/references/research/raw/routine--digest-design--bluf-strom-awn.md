## What BLUF is

A writing tactic that places the main point or conclusion at the beginning of a document,
email, presentation, or memo, before the supporting detail.

## Origin

Army Regulation 25-50 requires that "Army writing will be concise, organized, and to the
point" with "the main point at the beginning of the correspondence (bottom line up front)."
Air Force Handbook 33-337 similarly advises communicators to "state your bottom line early
in the message" using a direct, deductive approach.

## Structure

- **First:** state the position, purpose, or main point in unambiguous language.
- **After:** provide the supporting argument and context.

## Bottom line versus summary

The key distinction in the piece. A summary recaps the entire content. A bottom line
captures "the decisive moment of your argument" in one or two sentences. The test is
whether the reader can respond immediately, not whether they have received all the
information.

## Practical caveats

Brevity takes more effort on word choice, not less. Context matters: a skeptical audience
may dismiss the content that follows a blunt opening. The technique works best in
asynchronous communication and in high-information environments, which is exactly the
setting of an unattended recurring report.
