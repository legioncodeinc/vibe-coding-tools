## Current-generation lens lineup (iPhone 17 Pro / Pro Max)
- **0.5x Ultra-Wide — 13 mm equivalent**, 48 MP sensor (outputs 24 MP in standard HEIF/JPG). Same sensor and built-in lens as iPhone 16 Pro / Pro Max.
- **1x Main Wide — 24 mm equivalent**, 24 MP output. "Hasn't changed physically at all" from the prior generation.
- **4x Telephoto — 100 mm equivalent**, 48 MP (drops to 12 MP at minimum focus distance). **Sensor size increased 56%** this year. Replaced the iPhone 16's 5x lens; better dynamic range and "significantly more data."
- **2x — digital zoom crop** of the main sensor, not a physical lens (~48 mm equiv. look, but a crop).
- **8x — 200 mm equivalent, digital zoom** off the 4x module.

## Front / selfie camera
- Sensor is **almost 2x larger** than iPhone 16 Pro.
- **Square sensor** — enables automatic landscape/portrait orientation switching without rotating the phone.
- Supports ProRes Log video.

## Notes on what this means for the "phone look"
- The lineup has drifted: main went from **26 mm -> 24 mm** equivalent (wider, more perspective stretch on close subjects); telephoto from 77 mm -> 120 mm -> 100 mm equivalent.
- The "2x portrait" most people shoot is a **sensor crop of a 24 mm lens**, so it keeps the 24 mm *perspective* geometry (nose enlargement at close range) even though the framing looks like a 48 mm.
- Aperture f-numbers and exact sensor dimensions are not published in this article.
