# Fake Smile or Genuine Smile? The Duchenne Marker (Paul Ekman Group)

## Muscle anatomy
- **Genuine / enjoyment (Duchenne) smile** = *zygomatic major* (pulls lip corners up and back) **+ orbicularis oculi** (the muscle orbiting the eye).
- **Social / fake smile** = *zygomatic major only*. The eye ring stays inactive.
- FACS coding: the orbicularis oculi pars lateralis is **AU 6** ("cheek raiser"); the lip-corner pull is AU 12. Duchenne smile = **AU6 + AU12**.

## Visible signature of AU6 (what to describe in a prompt)
Quoted list of the genuine markers:
- cheeks are pulled **up**
- the skin **below the eye may bag or bulge**
- the **lower eyelid moves up** (narrowing the eye aperture)
- **crow's feet wrinkles** appear at the outer corner of the eye socket
- the skin **above the eye is pulled slightly down and inwards**
- the **eyebrows move down very slightly**

## Fake-smile tell
- Primary marker: "the absence of movement in the outer part of the muscle that orbits the eye" — wide mouth, unchanged eye aperture, no lower-lid rise.
- For broad fake smiles (where cheek raise happens passively from the mouth), the reliable subtle clue is "a very slight lowering of the eyebrows and the skin between the eyebrow and the upper eyelid, which is called the **eye cover fold**."

## Reliability caveat
A "deliberately made broad smile will produce all these signs, making it more difficult to spot fabrication"; the difference "is difficult to recognize, and most of the time we are easily fooled by broad smiles that are fabrication."

## Prompt-usable takeaways
- To describe an authentic candid: narrowed eye aperture, lower lid pushed up, crow's feet, cheek bulge under the eye, brow slightly lowered — **eyes changed shape**, not just a wide mouth.
- The AI/stock-photo "social smile" tell: full teeth, wide mouth, eyes wide open and unwrinkled, brows unmoved.
