# Build with Nano Banana Pro, our Gemini 3 Pro Image model (developer announcement)

- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor-blog

## Positioning
- "studio-quality image generation"
- "High-fidelity images with higher accuracy in text rendering and robust world knowledge"
- Character consistency and photo restoration; local image editing; Google Search grounding for retrieving data based on prompts.

## Reference-image limits (verbatim)
> "Achieve consistent resemblance for up to five individuals, integrate six high-fidelity shots, or blend as many as fourteen standard inputs into a single, polished ad."

Interpretation of the three numbers (corroborated by ai.google.dev docs):
- up to **5 character references** (people whose likeness is preserved)
- up to **6 high-fidelity object/subject references**
- up to **14 total standard input images**

## Resolution
- "2K and 4k resolution" options.

## Text rendering
- "state-of-the-art text rendering, producing clear, accurate text integrated in your images"; handles semantic context for localization tasks.

## SynthID (verbatim)
> "SynthID digital watermarks" are "integrated into every image created or edited with Gemini 3 Pro Image"

## Cost / latency
- Notes "higher cost and latency" versus Gemini 2.5 Flash Image.
