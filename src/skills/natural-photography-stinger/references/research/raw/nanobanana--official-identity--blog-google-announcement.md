# Nano Banana Pro: Gemini 3 Pro Image model from Google DeepMind (blog.google announcement)
- URL: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/nano-banana-pro/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: vendor-blog

## Identity
- "Nano Banana Pro" is the nickname/marketing name. The official model is **Gemini 3 Pro Image**, built on Gemini 3 Pro.
- Announced 2025-11-20.

## Capabilities (as stated)
- Image generation producing "accurate, context-rich visuals based on enhanced reasoning."
- Both a **generation and editing** model. Editing capabilities called out: localized editing, camera angle adjustment, color grading, scene lighting transformation.
- Real-time information integration via **Google Search grounding**.
- Text rendering: described as "the best model for creating images with correctly rendered and legible text directly in the image," supporting "multiple languages" and "a wider variety of textures, fonts and calligraphy."

## Resolution
- **2K and 4K resolution** available (1K is the base tier per API docs).
- Multiple aspect ratios supported (not enumerated in this announcement).

## Reference-image conditioning / consistency
- Accepts **up to 14 images** as inputs for composition work.
- Maintains consistency of **up to 5 people** across complex compositions.

## SynthID / provenance
- All Google-generated media include an "imperceptible SynthID digital watermark."
- A **visible** Gemini sparkle watermark is applied to free-tier and Pro-tier outputs.
- **No visible watermark** for Google AI Ultra subscribers and Google AI Studio developer-tool users. (The invisible SynthID watermark remains regardless.)
- Users can upload images into the Gemini app to ask whether it was generated by Google AI — verification performed via SynthID.

## Availability at launch
- Consumers: Gemini app (select the "Thinking" model). Free users get limited quota, then fall back to original Nano Banana.
- Subscribers: Google AI Plus / Pro / Ultra higher quotas; Pro/Ultra in Search (US only); NotebookLM subscribers globally.
- Professional: Google Ads (global), Workspace (Slides, Vids).
- Developers: Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Vertex AI; coming to Gemini Enterprise.
- Creatives: Google AI Ultra in Flow.
