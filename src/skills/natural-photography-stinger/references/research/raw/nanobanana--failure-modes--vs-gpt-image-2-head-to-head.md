# Nano Banana Pro vs GPT Image 2.0 — 10-prompt head-to-head
- URL: https://aivideobootcamp.com/blog/gpt-image-2-vs-nano-banana-pro-2026/
- Fetched: 2026-08-17
- Source type: practitioner

## Where Nano Banana Pro WINS

### Photorealism / skin
> "skin texture, subsurface scattering in the lips, the way individual hair strands catch the
> shaft of window light"

Evaluator summary: Nano Banana Pro "looks like a frame from a real camera" versus GPT Image 2.0's
"strong digital painting."

### Phone-camera / UGC aesthetics
Nano Banana Pro captured "fisheye, the hard overhead LED reflection on the hoodie, the slight
sensor noise" that reads as authentic mobile photography. GPT 2.0's comparable output was
> "too clean, too evenly lit."

### Materials & lighting
Exploded-wristwatch test: Nano Banana Pro rendered "more convincing metal finishing on the case
middle and the rotor." Athletic ad: superior "photorealistic skin texture, shallow depth of field,
and tagline."

### Painterly texture
Storybook spread: "more convincing gouache texture and a more storybook-feeling palette."

## Where Nano Banana Pro LOSES

### In-image typography (the biggest surprise vs Google's marketing claims)
- Manga test: GPT 2.0's "dialogue bubbles and the page number 'p.01' are cleanly rendered with
  correct spelling," while Nano Banana Pro "mangle[d] the English text inside two of the four bubbles."
- Multilingual menu: GPT 2.0's "Japanese characters are legible and the English translations
  match," whereas Nano Banana Pro had "three of its six dish lines have garbled kanji or invented
  dish names."
- Dense silkscreen poster: GPT 2.0 got every element legible and correctly spelled; Nano Banana
  Pro's "four of its seven text blocks are gibberish."

### Policy refusals on named people
Nano Banana Pro refused an Elon Musk CV:
> "This prompt might violate our policies about generating prominent people. Please try a
> different prompt or send feedback."
GPT 2.0 produced it. Conclusion drawn: "if your pipeline touches press, political satire,
biographical content, recruiting flyers, editorial illustration, or podcast cover art featuring
known figures, you cannot rely on Nano Banana Pro."

## Recommended hybrid workflow (from the source)
> "Nano Banana Pro for the photographic base plate, GPT 2.0 for the typography pass, and a
> compositor (Photoshop, Affinity, or a React canvas layer) that layers the two."

## Caveat
Single-tester, 10-prompt sample; text-rendering results contradict Google's own marketing and
other comparisons (see the getimg.ai file), so treat text-rendering rank as prompt-dependent.
