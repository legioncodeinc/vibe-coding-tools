## Notes

Names five competitor types to sort into during identification, not just "direct competitor":
1. **Direct competitors**: same segment, same kind of solution
2. **Substitutes**: a different approach that solves the same customer need
3. **Potential entrants**: adjacent players capable of entering the market
4. **Complements**: products or services that enhance the value proposition rather than compete with it
5. **Gatekeepers**: platforms, resellers, distributors, or regulators that control access to the market

Common dimensions used to build a 2x2 or category tree: customer segment, price tier and value proposition, route to market, geography and regulatory context, capability strength (technology, brand, cost position, data).

Recommended output artifacts: a category tree (hierarchical, players placed into buckets), a 2x2 matrix (two dimensions, shows clustering and white space), an ecosystem map (partnerships and dependencies), a channel map, and a geographic heatmap. The framework's own framing: the goal is "decision-grade market maps" that let leadership align on positioning, pricing, and go-to-market, not just a longer list of names.
