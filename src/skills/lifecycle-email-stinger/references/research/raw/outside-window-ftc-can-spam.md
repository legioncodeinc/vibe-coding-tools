## Captured evidence

The FTC says CAN-SPAM applies to commercial email, including business-to-business email. Its compliance guide requires accurate header and routing information, a subject line that reflects the message, required advertising disclosure, a valid physical postal address, a clear opt-out method, and fulfillment of opt-out requests within 10 business days. The opt-out method must remain available for at least 30 days after sending.

The FTC distinguishes commercial content from narrowly defined transactional or relationship content. A prior relationship does not automatically make a promotional message transactional. Organizations remain responsible for vendors sending email on their behalf.
