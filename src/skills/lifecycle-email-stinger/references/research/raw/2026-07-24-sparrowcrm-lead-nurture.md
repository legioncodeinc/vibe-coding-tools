## Captured evidence

SparrowCRM describes nurture as a series of relevant touches over weeks or months, aligned to the lead's stage. It says the process should begin with segmentation and should map early-stage leads to education, mid-stage leads to proof or comparisons, and late-stage leads to decision support.

The guide warns against sending too many emails too quickly, treating every behavioral signal as equal, leaving a high-intent lead in automated nurture after it is appropriate to hand the lead to sales, and reusing one generic sequence at every stage.

It identifies opens and pricing-page visits as behavioral signals, but the source does not establish that either event proves human activity or buying intent. The tracking caution from the HighLevel community source remains applicable.
