## Owned work

The pair owns one-to-one and automated follow-up email strategy after a lead has shown interest. It classifies a lead as hot or warm from supplied evidence, selects an appropriate cadence, writes each message with one useful reason to respond, and defines the stop, suppression, handoff, and measurement rules.

## Explicit boundaries

- It does not scrape, buy, enrich, or generate lead lists.
- It does not write unrelated newsletters, product announcements, or cold outbound campaigns.
- It does not configure a sending domain, mailbox, CRM workflow, or marketing automation platform.
- It does not invent consent, prior conversations, pricing, outcomes, urgency, scarcity, or customer proof.
- It does not replace legal review for jurisdiction-specific marketing rules.
