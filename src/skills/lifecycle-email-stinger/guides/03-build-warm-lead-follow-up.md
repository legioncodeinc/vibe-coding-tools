# Guide 03: build warm-lead follow-up

Use this guide only after Guide 01 returns `WARM` and readiness is `READY` or `DRAFT_WITH_GAPS`.

## 1. Name the real warm signal

Write one internal sentence that proves valid permission or relationship and a real topic of interest. An opt-in tied to a topic, a requested educational asset, a relevant event registration, or a lead-supplied future revisit date can support a warm state. A list name, open, click, visit, or imported record alone cannot.

Current nurture guidance favors segmenting by stage and topic before writing. [../references/research/raw/2026-07-24-sparrowcrm-lead-nurture.md]

## 2. Match value to stage

| Known stage | Useful content type | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Early exploration | Clear explanation, checklist, or diagnostic question | Hard close or invented urgency |
| Comparing approaches | Approved comparison, requirements list, or answer to a known concern | Unverified competitor claim |
| Future timing | Short acknowledgement and one dated revisit | Repeated nudges before the stated date |
| Re-engaged nurture | New verified update or relevant decision aid | Claiming the lead is ready to buy |

SparrowCRM distinguishes education, proof or comparison, and decision support by stage. Proof can appear only when the agency supplies and approves it. [../references/research/raw/2026-07-24-sparrowcrm-lead-nurture.md]

## 3. Map the sequence

Before drafting, give each step a distinct job:

| Step | Job |
|---|---|
| W0 | Deliver or offer useful value tied to the real signal |
| W1 | Clarify the lead's priority |
| W2 | Provide an approved decision aid or answer |
| W3 | Ask for the right timing |
| W4 | Pause the active series |

Remove a step when it lacks new value. Current vendor and community guidance rejects generic checking-in language. [../references/research/raw/2026-06-12-hunter-three-follow-ups.md] [../references/research/raw/2026-09-02-reddit-sales-follow-ups.md]

## 4. Draft from the reusable sequence

Open `../references/templates/warm-lead-sequence.md` and fill only verified fields.

- Use a safe greeting fallback when the name can be blank.
- Describe only the action the lead actually took.
- Offer one useful next action.
- Make a timing change or no easy to communicate.
- End the active series at W4.

HighLevel documents that empty merge fields can create blank output, so implementation must test both populated and missing-value records. [../references/research/raw/2026-09-01-highlevel-merge-field-fallbacks.md]

## 5. Honor a stated review window

When a lead gives a date or says when they expect to revisit:

1. Acknowledge the timing without pressure.
2. Confirm the exact date only if it is explicit or ask permission to choose one.
3. Suppress intermediate default steps.
4. Send one useful follow-up on the recorded date.
5. Reclassify from the response.

A recent warm-lead thread centers on a prospect who supplied a real review window. The conservative synthesis is to follow the lead's stated timing unless a genuine service need requires clarification. [../references/research/raw/2026-08-05-reddit-warm-lead-timing.md]

## 6. Return the deliverable

Provide:

1. Plan card from Guide 01.
2. Sequence table with step, timing, value, CTA, and exit condition.
3. Plain-text drafts labeled W0 through W4.
4. Any lead-stated timing branch.
5. Unresolved evidence placeholders.
6. Handoff, suppression, QA, and measurement work still required.

Never call a warm lead hot to create urgency, and never turn an educational opt-in into a claim that the person requested a sales call.
