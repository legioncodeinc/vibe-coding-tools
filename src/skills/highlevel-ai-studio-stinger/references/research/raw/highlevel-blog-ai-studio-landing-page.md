# Turn your business ideas into live multi-page sites with a single AI prompt in HighLevel

Building a stunning digital presence used to require a team of developers, hours of design tweaks and a hefty budget. What if you could turn your brilliant business ideas into fully functional websites in seconds?

HighLevel has officially closed the gap between your imagination and reality. With HighLevel AI Studio, you can now launch a complete, multi-page web experience using just a single conversational text prompt.

## Transform your ideas into a HighLevel website

The days of staring at a blank canvas or dragging and dropping elements for hours are gone. HighLevel AI Studio acts as your generative design partner, allowing you to go from a simple concept to a live landing page in the blink of an eye.

Literally whatever idea you have, the conversational builder can spin it up. It generates professional layouts, balances typography and writes contextually relevant copy tailored to your business model. You can even build a complex, multi-page asset like a six-page website complete with a free lead magnet with just one single master prompt.

## Crafting the perfect prompt for your HighLevel landing page

To get the absolute best results from your build, providing clear context is key. When you are writing your master prompt, remember to include these essential elements:

- Business details: Describe your vision, target audience and business model.

- Key features: Mention any specific lead magnets, forms or appointment booking sections you need.

- Design language: Specify your stylistic preferences, such as light mode versus dark mode and your core brand colors.


If you are feeling stuck, you can even use other AI models to help brainstorm and write a detailed prompt for you. Once your prompt is ready, send it straight to HighLevel AI Studio and watch the workspace bring your layout to life.

## Fine-tune your layout with the HighLevel Code Editor

The magic does not stop after the first generation. HighLevel AI Studio keeps persistent context per project, meaning you can chat with the AI to tweak colors, rewrite headlines or add entirely new sections.

For operators who want complete control over their pages, you can easily switch over to the built-in HighLevel Code Editor. This environment gives you precise control over the TypeScript, JSX and CSS files powering your experience. You can execute global project searches, make granular edits and view instant updates in the live preview window without ever leaving the platform.

Once your design looks exactly how you want, you can seamlessly connect your HighLevel forms, integrate your booking calendars and publish the entire project to a custom domain.
