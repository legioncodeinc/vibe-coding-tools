Today, AI Studio is officially available to everyone inside HighLevel Labs which is free to access, ready to use and unlike anything we've shipped before. This isn't a minor update or a feature tucked inside a settings menu; AI Studio is a full generative builder experience that lets you go from an idea in your head to a fully functional product, just by describing what you want.

If you've ever felt like the gap between what you can imagine and what you can actually build was too wide, _AI Studio closes it._

And as Varun Vairavan, Co-Founder, HighLevel puts it, _"AI Studio is the beginning of something much bigger. We've always believed that the best technology should disappear into the work and that's exactly what we're building toward. A world where you come in with an idea and leave with something real, without the friction of learning tools, chasing approvals, or waiting on anyone. This is HighLevel's bet on what the future of building looks like."_

**What is AI Studio?**

AI Studio is a conversational, AI-powered builder built directly into HighLevel. You describe what you want to create and it builds it. You point it to a live website and it recreates the structure. You drop in an image for inspiration and it uses that as creative direction. You watch your product take shape in real time, make adjustments as you go, and when you're ready, you ship it on your own custom domain.

It's not a design tool. It's not just a code assistant. It's a complete build environment that combines conversational AI with a powerful generative design experience, purpose-built for the way agencies and operators actually work.

And it lives inside HighLevel Labs, which means you can start exploring it today at no additional cost. The future of AI Studio is massive. Anish George, Director of Engineering, HighLevel, affirms this and says, _"AI Studio was built in record time by a very small team and we have big plans that will keep rolling out every day until it's the best product in the market."_

Describing the product further, Madhav Agarwal, Product Manager, AI Studio, HighLevel, says, _"What excites me most is that AI Studio moves creation from a fragmented design workflow to an intelligent product workflow, helping agencies build complete digital experiences natively connected to the systems that run their business."_

**The numbers say it all:**

AI Studio has only been live in Labs. It is still in the free trial mode and here's what that's looked like so far:

22,000+ total projects created. 9,000+ distinct locations using it. 7,500+ distinct users are already building inside it. All with a 23% project publish rate, meaning nearly 1 in 4 projects created inside AI Studio have already been shipped.

When adoption moves this fast, the product is doing the talking, and when users can publish something so real, so quickly, it means the tool is working exactly the way it was designed to.

**What you can build with it:**

The best way to understand AI Studio is to understand what it removes from your process: the back-and-forth, the revisions, the waiting, the tool-switching.

Here's what you can do from day one:

- **Talk it into existence:** Describe what you want to build in plain language and watch AI Studio translate your intent into fully functional UI and logic. No prompts to engineer. No templates to wrestle with. Just say what you need.

- **Clone any website:** Drop in a live URL and AI Studio recreates its structure, layout and flows, giving you a head start on any build that would otherwise take hours to replicate from scratch.

- **Build from an image:** Have a screenshot or a design reference you love? Use it as a creative direction. AI Studio uses existing images to guide layout, styling and the overall look of your generated experience.

- **See it happen in real time:** A live preview environment means you're never guessing what something will look like. Your application evolves in front of you as you build forms, surveys, calendars, calculators and interactive widgets; things that actually make a page work and not just make it look good, all inside a sandboxed environment that keeps everything safe while you experiment.

- **Iterate without fear:** Time travel gives you full version history and the ability to roll back to any previous state. Try something bold. If it doesn't work, you're one click away from where you were.

- **Keep the momentum going:** Background generation lets AI Studio create designs and assets asynchronously while you keep working on other parts of your project. No waiting around. No interrupting your flow.

- **Ship when you're ready:** Move from build to deployment without leaving the platform. Publish on your own custom domain and go live with a production-ready presence.

- **Stay organized across every project:** Project home gives you a centralized workspace to manage all your builds in one place, so nothing gets lost and everything stays moving.


Beyond all of that, AI Studio is built for real use, with usage-aware credits, security safeguards and the infrastructure to scale with you as your projects grow.

**This is what building looks like now:**

The gap between imagination and execution has always been the hard part. Hiring developers, briefing designers, managing timelines, waiting on revisions; it's the part of building that slows everything down and drives costs up.

AI Studio is HighLevel's answer to all of it. It doesn't just speed up the process. It changes what's possible for the people using this platform every day.

It's available now. It's free inside HighLevel Labs. And the best way to understand what it can do is to open it and start building.
