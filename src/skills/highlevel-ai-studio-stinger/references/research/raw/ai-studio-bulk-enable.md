# Bulk Enable/Disable AI Studio Across Sub-Accounts

Modified on: Fri, 24 Jul, 2026 at 2:27 PM

This article explains how Agency owners and admins can disable AI Studio in bulk across multiple sub-accounts.

This guide is intended for Agency owners and admins who want to restrict access to AI Studio for their sub-accounts. Once disabled, AI Studio will only be available to sub-accounts when it is manually enabled by the Agency.

* * *

# **How to Bulk Enable/Disable AI Studio Across Sub-Accounts**

Follow the instructions in this article to configure this setting.

1. Navigate to your Agency dashboard



![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155076405330/original/axFRcgMj6VdFLAYz5Xs1Nv1Xg1Sy2MMX6w.png?1784557526)

2. Select **AI Suite** from the left-hand menu.



![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155076405693/original/2p9znunrha8pnf9Z97sevZCRBq7xsb5X2w.png?1784557688)

3. Select **AI setup** from the menu options in AI Suite.



![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155076406081/original/hhpkBk9Ut2SGPaoLQzMmFsY2xzkxs7ykpA.png?1784557862)

4. Use the multi select checkboxes in the table entries to select sub-accounts for which you wish to change the permissions in bulk. Once selected click on **Manage AI** access button which will open a drawer on the right side.



![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155076410130/original/gc2GS21nSbCoUNVKqRb5pzvAsLGrjM080A.png?1784559801)

5. In the drawer, select the AI Studio permission you want to apply to the selected sub-accounts. Then click **Apply Changes.**



**\- No change:** Keeps the existing AI Studio setting for each selected sub-account.



**-** **Enable for sub-account admins only:** Allows only sub-account admins to access AI Studio. Other users in those sub-accounts will not have access.



**\- Enable for all sub-account admins and users:** Allows all users in the selected sub-accounts to access AI Studio, regardless of their role.

**\- Disable:** Hides AI Studio and prevents access in the selected sub-accounts.



![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155076411555/original/aYiGzpCRptNQoPNTNN2rET6sdYYu5c5KcA.png?1784560312)
