## Captured facts

Immediate or background mobile call disconnections can result from disabled background refresh, restrictive battery settings, or denied microphone access. The article gives separate iOS and Android checks and notes that menu labels vary by device.

## Safety caveat

Ask the customer to confirm permissions rather than changing unrelated device privacy or security settings. If required permissions are already enabled, collect a controlled example and escalate instead of repeatedly placing calls.
