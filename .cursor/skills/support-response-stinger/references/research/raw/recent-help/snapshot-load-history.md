## Captured facts

Snapshot load history identifies which snapshot was loaded, when it was loaded, which version was used, who initiated it, which asset categories were included, and the status of those assets. It is distinct from agency-level snapshot push history.

## Safety caveat

Use history to establish cause before loading, refreshing, or pushing another snapshot. A second load can introduce duplicate or conflicting assets. Preserve the target account, source snapshot, version, and failed-asset list in any escalation.
