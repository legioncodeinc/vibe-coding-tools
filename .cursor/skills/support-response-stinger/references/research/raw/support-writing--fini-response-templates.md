## Captured findings

The source emphasizes that templates should preserve structural commitments while the agent personalizes the customer's issue and emotional context. It distinguishes acknowledgment, delays, escalations, refunds, outages, repeats, and reopening after closure.

It also argues for a named human sender instead of a faceless team signature. The component adopts a `{agent_name}` plus `{agency}` signature and requires a fallback owner when an individual name is unavailable.
