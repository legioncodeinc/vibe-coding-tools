# Email builder templates and campaign scheduling affected by GCS outage

- URL: https://status.gohighlevel.com/incident/919566
- Fetched: 2026-09-04
- Source type: official-status-incident
- Incident observed: 2026-06-10
- Trend window: 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive
- Window role: direct recent incident evidence

## Captured facts

Some agencies and sub-accounts could not save or load email templates or schedule campaigns because of an upstream cloud storage outage. The provider later reported normal service.

## Safety caveat

Preserve a local copy of unsaved content where possible. Before rescheduling, confirm whether the original campaign schedule was accepted so the same campaign is not sent twice.
