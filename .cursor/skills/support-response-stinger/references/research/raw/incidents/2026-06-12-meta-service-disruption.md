# Meta disruptions across advertising, lead sync, social publishing, and WhatsApp

- URL: https://status.gohighlevel.com/incident/922080
- Fetched: 2026-09-04
- Source type: official-status-incident
- Incident observed: 2026-06-12
- Trend window: 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive
- Window role: direct recent incident evidence

## Captured facts

An upstream Meta disruption affected connecting Facebook accounts and pages, Facebook lead form synchronization, Facebook lead delivery, Meta Social Planner posts, and WhatsApp functions. The provider later reported resolution.

## Safety caveat

Do not repeatedly disconnect and reconnect Meta assets during an upstream outage. After recovery, audit missing leads and failed posts, then use supported manual synchronization or controlled retries while preventing duplicates.
