## Captured evidence

The folder separates seven recurring tickets:

1. Inspecting delivery logs for a specific message
2. Tracked links that do not open
3. Replies that do not appear in the conversation inbox
4. A warning that the agency must change email settings
5. Missing open, click, delivered, or bounce statistics
6. Sending a test message from a conversation
7. A test-send error in a campaign email step

This collection supplies issue boundaries and routes the response toward the correct diagnostic rather than a generic deliverability reply.
