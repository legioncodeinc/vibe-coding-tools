## Captured evidence

The folder distinguishes these ticket types:

- Approved campaign without a linked sending number, including error 30034
- Brand registration and brand approval
- Campaign rejection and required corrections
- Vetting errors
- Campaign registration steps
- Brand and campaign approval best practices
- Campaign use-case selection
- Throughput questions
- Registration and carrier fees
- Canadian registration requirements
- Forbidden message categories in the United States and Canada

The separation matters in customer replies. A brand rejection, a campaign rejection, and an approved campaign with an unlinked number look similar to a customer but require different evidence and next actions.
