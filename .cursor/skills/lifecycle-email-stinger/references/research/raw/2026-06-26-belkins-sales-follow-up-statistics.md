## Captured evidence

Belkins says the study covers more than 7.5 million B2B emails. The initial email had the highest per-step reply rate, while follow-up steps 2 through 6 collectively produced 58.6 percent of replies. The article reports that step 3 produced 35.6 percent of email-sourced meetings and that steps 3 through 5 produced 53.5 percent of email-sourced meetings.

The source recommends treating step 3 as a new argument rather than a recycled reminder. It identifies 3 to 5 email steps as a practical range in its dataset and says returns diminish after step 5. It also recommends switching channels when email is exhausted rather than extending the sequence indefinitely.

## Transfer limits

- This is vendor-reported observational data, not an independent experiment.
- The measured campaigns were B2B outreach campaigns, not a controlled sample of opted-in hot and warm inbound leads.
- Use the source to justify multiple distinct touches, cumulative outcome measurement, and a bounded stop point. Do not import its rates as a promise or client benchmark.
