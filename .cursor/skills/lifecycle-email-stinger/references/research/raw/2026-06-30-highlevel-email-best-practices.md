## Captured evidence

HighLevel recommends a dedicated sending domain, email validation, DMARC, an aligned From address, unsubscribe support, opt-in list practices, consistent volume, and suppression of long-term unengaged contacts. The guide says a From address on a different organizational domain can create DMARC alignment and deliverability problems.

The guide is primarily for LC Email and LC Email dedicated-domain users. It tells custom SMTP users to consult their own provider for infrastructure-specific compliance and deliverability behavior.
