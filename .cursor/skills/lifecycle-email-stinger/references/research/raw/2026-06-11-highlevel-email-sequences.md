## Captured evidence

HighLevel documents an email sequence as multiple automated emails sent in order in one threaded conversation. The product supports per-step delays, open or delivery conditions, designed or plain-text messages, stop-on-reply, and sequence-level plus step-level statistics.

The page says stop-on-reply automatically ends the sequence for the replying contact when enabled. It also warns that contacts who become eligible for a step while a sequence is paused or in draft can be skipped.
