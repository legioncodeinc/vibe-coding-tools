## Captured evidence

HighLevel documents a User Replied trigger that fires after a team member's manual message from the conversation view is delivered. The trigger can support response-time measurement, stop an automation when a person takes over, update activity fields, and route by sender or channel.

The page says workflow-generated and Conversation AI messages do not fire this trigger. It also warns that several quick manual messages can each fire it unless re-enrollment is restricted, and that separate workflows react independently.
