## Captured evidence

Hunter argues that a follow-up should add a new reason to respond rather than repeat the initial message. Its proposed sequence keeps real follow-ups in the same thread, shortens later messages, reframes the value argument, introduces an appropriate second channel, and finishes with a smaller request.

The playbook recommends 2 to 3 email follow-ups for its outreach use case. Its worked setup spaces the first follow-up 3 to 5 days after the initial email, places a manual LinkedIn touch between later emails, and sends the final email 3 to 5 days after that touch. Hunter also instructs users to confirm stop-on-reply so an engaged recipient does not continue receiving automated messages.

Hunter links to its State of Email Outreach 2026 and reports a 3.3 percent average reply rate for the strongest single outreach email versus 6.8 percent after three follow-ups. This benchmark is cold-outreach evidence and must not be represented as a hot-lead result.
