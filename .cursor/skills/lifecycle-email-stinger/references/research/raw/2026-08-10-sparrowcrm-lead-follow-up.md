## Captured evidence

SparrowCRM defines lead follow-up as the contacts after an initial interaction until the lead converts or is disqualified. Its core recommendations are to respond while an inbound inquiry is fresh, use a planned cadence, match the message to the deal stage, use more than one channel when appropriate, and stop rather than chase forever.

The guide proposes 5 to 7 touchpoints over 2 to 3 weeks as a typical cadence, with closer spacing early and wider spacing later. It advises sending a proposal follow-up 2 to 3 days after the proposal and a trial follow-up 2 to 3 days before a verified trial end date. It recommends moving an unresponsive lead to nurture after a final message rather than leaving the lead in active pursuit.

The article's templates focus on an actual situation: a known question, an actual proposal, a real trial date, a stated objection, or a formally closed-lost deal. That situational approach supports evidence-gated personalization.
