# EAA enforcement context and WCAG version selection

Grounded entirely in distilled research sections 1, 3, and 6. Read this before scoring any criterion whose applicability depends on version (the WCAG 2.2 additions) or before writing anything that characterizes EAA enforcement risk in a report.

## 1. Why WCAG 2.1 AA, not 2.2 AA, is the scoring baseline

Neither Directive (EU) 2019/882 nor its Annex I names a WCAG version directly; Annex I states functional outcomes (perceivable through more than one sensory channel, understandable, operable), not a version-numbered checklist. [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] The presumption-of-conformity route runs through Article 15(1): conformity with a harmonised standard referenced in the Official Journal creates a presumption of conformity, only as far as that standard covers the requirement. [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] The currently-cited harmonised standard is EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (March 2021), which maps to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] [raw/www-disabilityworld-org-articles-eaa-first-year-enforcement-report.md] V4.1.1, which maps to WCAG 2.2, was in draft review as of November 2025 with an expected Official Journal citation around October 2026 per the vendor blog, or "within 18 months" of a May 2026 publish date per the advocacy-org source, independently. [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] [raw/www-disabilityworld-org-articles-eaa-first-year-enforcement-report.md] **Re-verify this against the live Official Journal listing before every engagement**; by the time this Stinger's forge stage runs again, V4.1.1 may already be cited.

## 2. Why claiming the newer version is not the "safer" choice

The vendor-blog source argues that claiming WCAG 2.2 AA conformance, while a broad engineering superset of 2.1 AA, is a worse legal-claim choice: it asserts something the EAA never required while not asserting conformity with the standard that actually grants the presumption, and it is objectively a larger promise (nine additional success criteria, each "another thing that can be wrong on your site, in a claim you published, in writing, about yourself"). [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] The source cites the FTC's action against accessiBe as turning "on claims of exactly this shape... not on the state of anyone's site, but on what was said about it." [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] This is single-sourced (only the vendor blog makes this argument) but directly shapes this Stinger's report-writing rule in `references/templates/a11y-score-rollup-and-rating-bands.md` section 3: dated, specific, gap-disclosing findings, never a blanket conformance verdict.

## 3. Enforcement is live, and penalty exposure varies roughly two orders of magnitude across the EU 27

The advocacy-org source, the higher-authority source of the two for enforcement data (cites European Commission DG JUST implementation notes and national market-surveillance bulletins, versus the vendor blog's self-described developer opinion), reports the EAA became enforceable across the Union on 28 June 2025. [raw/www-disabilityworld-org-articles-eaa-first-year-enforcement-report.md] As of its May 2026 publish date: transposition is formally complete in 27/27 Member States but operationally uneven (designated market-surveillance authority, complaint mechanism, and penalty schedule lag in seven Member States); penalty floors run as low as 5,000 euros (Estonia, Slovenia), ceilings around 75,000-100,000 euros in France/Germany/Netherlands, up to 1,000,000 euros in Spain, and Italy uses a turnover-percentage tier up to 5% instead of a fixed ceiling. [raw/www-disabilityworld-org-articles-eaa-first-year-enforcement-report.md] First sanctioning actions landed in Germany (BAFA), Spain (Ministerio de Asuntos Economicos), and France (ARCOM and DGCCRF) during the 2025-26 window, concentrated on e-commerce checkout flows and self-service kiosks. [raw/www-disabilityworld-org-articles-eaa-first-year-enforcement-report.md] Fewer than 50% of major EU e-commerce properties pass an automated WCAG 2.1 AA scan at mid-2026, up from 30-40% pre-deadline. [raw/www-disabilityworld-org-articles-eaa-first-year-enforcement-report.md]

**Report this jurisdiction/penalty context only when the audited party operates in or serves EU customers**; it is not universally applicable, and this Stinger's archive covers the EU regime exclusively (US ADA/Section 508 and other non-EU regimes are unresearched, see `guides/05-manual-vs-automated-confidence-and-non-goals.md`).

## 4. Honest limit on how settled section 1's version mapping actually is

The vendor-blog author states outright that they "could not establish from secondary sources, to my own satisfaction, exactly which references currently stand cited in the Official Journal under Directive (EU) 2019/882 as opposed to under Directive (EU) 2016/2102" (the older public-sector directive), and warns "sources disagree, and vendor pages state it with a confidence their citations do not support." [raw/groundedwp-com-blog-wcag-21-or-22-for-the-eaa.md] Carry this caveat into every report that cites the EN 301 549 version mapping; the live Official Journal listing is the actual check, not this guide.
