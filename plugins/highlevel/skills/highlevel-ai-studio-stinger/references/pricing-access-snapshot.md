# HighLevel AI Studio pricing and access snapshot

Snapshot date: 2026-09-03. Pricing, model availability, plan names, Labs state, and limits can change. Verify the live HighLevel UI before quoting a customer or changing billing. [research/raw/ai-product-pricing.md]

## AI Employee plan snapshot

- Pay-Per-Use has no monthly AI subscription fee and bills applicable usage.
- AI Employee Growth is listed at $50 per enabled location per month.
- AI Employee Unlimited is listed at $97 per enabled location per month.
- `Unlimited` remains subject to fair-use protections and can be throttled or limited.
- AI Employee rebilling requires the applicable agency plan and configuration. The archived pricing page lists a $497 per month agency prerequisite for AI Employee rebilling.

Source: [research/raw/ai-product-pricing.md], [research/raw/ai-employee-access.md].

## AI Studio cost drivers

- Reasoning and planning steps
- Number of pages
- Generated copy and visual assets
- URL, screenshot, or image analysis
- Form and calendar connection actions
- Iterative refinements and versions
- Publishing and custom-domain actions

The archived AI Studio examples cost $0.60, $1.30, and $1.10 for three different workloads. They are examples only. Image-heavy work can cost more than a larger text-and-structure project. [research/raw/ai-studio-pricing.md]

## Spending-limit controls

| Control | Archived behavior |
|---|---|
| No spending limit configured | AI can continue into billable usage. |
| Keep AI running, just notify | Soft threshold. AI continues and alerts are sent. This is the default behavior when a limit exists. |
| Block AI at the limit | New AI output stops until the limit is raised or the calendar-month reset occurs. |
| Threshold alerts | 70, 80, 90, and 100 percent of an active limit. |
| Maximum configured limit | $1,000 per month per sub-account and $500 per month per user in the archived page. |

Usage figures can lag by up to five minutes. A sub-account override can replace the agency default, and the limit with the least remaining allowance can stop a user first. [research/raw/ai-usage-limits.md]

## Access checks

1. Confirm Agency view versus the intended sub-account.
2. Confirm AI Employee and product access where required.
3. Confirm Labs visibility and enabled state.
4. Confirm the user has `View & Manage` when editing is required.
5. Confirm any agency bulk access rule is not hiding or disabling the product.
6. Confirm the plan, current included allowance, extra-usage setting, spending limit, and behavior at the limit.
