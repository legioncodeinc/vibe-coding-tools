## Captured facts

The workflow builder can flag integration failures, missing mandatory fields, and invalid If/Else or Wait configuration. When a workflow appears correct but does not run, the article recommends a fresh test contact, trigger-filter review, re-entry review, comparison of test and live behavior, and execution or error-log review.

## Safety caveat

AI-generated repair suggestions require human review before changing a live workflow. Escalate with the workflow name, affected contact, approximate execution time, screenshots, and relevant logs. Remove secrets and private customer content from evidence.
