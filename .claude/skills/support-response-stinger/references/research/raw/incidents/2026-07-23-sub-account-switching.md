# Issues with switching Between Sub-Accounts

- URL: https://status.gohighlevel.com/incident/975352
- Fetched: 2026-09-04
- Source type: official-status-incident
- Incident observed: 2026-07-23
- Trend window: 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive
- Window role: direct recent incident evidence

## Captured facts

Some users and account administrators could not switch between sub-accounts. The provider later reported resolution.

## Safety caveat

Do not remove and recreate the user or broaden permissions as a first response. Confirm the user's intended account access, role, browser session, and whether another authorized user can switch successfully.
