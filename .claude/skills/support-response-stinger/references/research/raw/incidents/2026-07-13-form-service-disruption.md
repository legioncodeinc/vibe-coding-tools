# Form Service Disruption

- URL: https://status.gohighlevel.com/incident/962741
- Fetched: 2026-09-04
- Source type: official-status-incident
- Incident observed: 2026-07-13
- Trend window: 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive

## Captured facts

A subset of users could not access certain forms. The provider later reported that forms were accessible and operating normally.

## Safety caveat

Do not replace or republish a production form before checking its existing submissions, embed, page assignment, and post-recovery behavior. Use a controlled test submission that cannot trigger a live charge or customer-facing automation.
