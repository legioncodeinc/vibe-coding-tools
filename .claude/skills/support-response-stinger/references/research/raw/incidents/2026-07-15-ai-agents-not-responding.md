## Captured facts

AI agents did not respond to prompts during the incident. The provider restored service and reported that agents were responding normally.

## Safety caveat

Preserve the agent configuration and prompt. Do not delete or recreate an agent until a fresh controlled prompt has been tested after recovery. Remove private customer data, credentials, and secrets from any diagnostic transcript.
