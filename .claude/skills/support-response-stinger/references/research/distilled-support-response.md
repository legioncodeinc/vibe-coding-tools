# Distilled research: white-label provider support responses

This article is the evidence base for professional provider-side ticket emails about a HighLevel-powered service. Trend evidence is fixed to 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 inclusive. Evergreen official documentation may fall outside that window and is used only for current remediation facts, not to imply recent frequency. [raw/incidents/2026-06-05-branded-sites-legacy-infrastructure.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--001--whatsapp-attachments-not-delivered.md] [raw/official--calendar-missing-slots.md]

The central rule is a two-rail model: public reports, incidents, interest signals, and search suggestions determine which issue families deserve coverage, while current official documentation and verified ticket evidence determine what `{agency}` may safely tell a customer to do. A frequency signal is not a diagnosis, and a documented remedy is not proof that its issue is common. [raw/official--status-incidents-2026-06-to-09.md] [raw/reddit--email-deliverability-cluster.md] [raw/official--knowledge-base-index.md]

## 1. Evidence contract

| Evidence tier | Source and role | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|

| F2 | Two or more independent recent community records | Repeated customer-observable symptoms and the language customers use to describe them | Root cause, product behavior for all accounts, compliant workarounds, or measured ticket prevalence [raw/reddit--email-deliverability-cluster.md] [raw/reddit--a2p-registration-cluster.md] |
| F3 | One recent community record | A real support intent worth considering when it has a distinct diagnostic or next action | A confirmed defect or a generally safe remedy [raw/reddit/reddit--052--workflow-step-delays.md] |
| F4 | Official community-board interest or current search autocomplete | Directional demand, discoverability pressure, and wording used in public searches | Ticket counts, query volume, incident status, or cause [raw/ideas--client-portal-customization.md] [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-email.md] |
| F5 | Popular-article placement, folder order, or collection breadth | A tie-breaker that prevents the catalog from omitting ordinary setup and how-to intents | Relative traffic, ticket volume, or issue severity [raw/official--support-home-popular.md] [raw/official--knowledge-base-index.md] |

| R2 | Evergreen official help article outside the trend window | A documented diagnostic model that remains useful when current account evidence matches it | Recent demand or a current incident [raw/official--calendar-missing-slots.md] [raw/official--funnel-and-website-faq-troubleshooting.md] |
| W1 | Current support-writing source | The communication job of acknowledgment, clarification, update, escalation, workaround, resolution, closure, and reopening | Product diagnosis, feature behavior, billing authority, or technical remediation [raw/support-writing--issuelinker-bug-replies.md] [raw/support-writing--kaltalk-ticket-responses.md] |

### Evidence-state labels

- `ACCOUNT-VERIFIED` means the ticket record, a controlled reproduction, or an internal diagnostic proves the observed behavior for this customer. Product facts in the reply may be applied to that evidence, but the response must still avoid claiming an unverified root cause. [raw/recent-help/call-quality.md] [raw/recent-help/workflow-error-resolution.md]
- `INCIDENT-CONFIRMED` means the affected surface and time overlap a verified incident record. Only the facts actually stated for that incident may be communicated, and restoration must be checked against the customer's account before closure. [raw/incidents/2026-09-02-appointment-data-visibility.md] [raw/incidents/2026-08-31-application-asset-loading.md]
- `COMMUNITY-REPORTED` means a public user described the symptom. It raises prioritization only and must never be presented to a customer as diagnosis or proof. [raw/reddit/reddit--030--form-builder-freeze.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--034--ai-studio-project-404.md]
- `DOC-COVERED` means an official article supplies a diagnostic path. The agent still has to match the ticket evidence to that path before recommending an action. [raw/official--email-delivery-troubleshooting-folder.md] [raw/official--calendar-troubleshooting-tool.md]
- `UNRESOLVED` means the archive has a demand signal but no safe, sufficiently specific remediation. The correct response is evidence collection plus specialist escalation, not a guessed workaround. [raw/reddit/reddit--001--whatsapp-attachments-not-delivered.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--076--undocumented-api-pagination-webhooks.md]

## 2. Research method and limits

The capture closed on 2026-09-04 and used the inclusive 2026-06-04 through 2026-09-04 window for trend evidence. The official incident roll-up contains 26 distinct incident URLs after lifecycle updates were collapsed, and the individual Reddit archive runs from record 001 through record 096. [raw/official--status-incidents-2026-06-to-09.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--001--whatsapp-attachments-not-delivered.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--096--multiple-websites-one-subaccount.md]

The sweep covers recent Reddit discussions, dated status incidents, current official community-board activity, Google autocomplete snapshots, current popular-help placement, current troubleshooting collections, and recently modified official help articles. These sources have different selection effects and are kept separate in the evidence contract. [raw/reddit--platform-annoyances.md] [raw/official--status-incidents-2026-06-to-09.md] [raw/ideas--platform-performance.md] [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-not.md] [raw/official--support-home-popular.md]

Important limits follow:

- Reddit records expose a visible relative age and a self-selected user report, not a stable population estimate. Every record explicitly warns that community workarounds are unverified or that the behavior remains a user observation. [raw/reddit/reddit--001--whatsapp-attachments-not-delivered.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--096--multiple-websites-one-subaccount.md]
- Incident records overrepresent outages and degradations. An incident can confirm a historical service event, but its presence does not prove the cause of a later look-alike symptom. [raw/official--status-incidents-2026-06-to-09.md] [raw/incidents/2026-09-01-multi-module-slowness.md]
- Autocomplete shows neither search volume nor the date a phrase became popular, and informational phrases must not be counted as failures. [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-email.md] [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-not.md]
- Community-board vote totals reflect public interest around a request or pain point, not support-ticket counts. A released capability can also turn an old limitation into a current configuration question, as happened with business-day booking windows. [raw/ideas--calendar-business-days.md] [raw/ideas--client-portal-customization.md]
- Official help collection sizes and popular-article order indicate breadth and current support intent, not measured frequency. [raw/official--knowledge-base-index.md] [raw/official--support-home-popular.md]
- No raw record in this archive provides `{agency}` private ticket counts, private help-search logs, or a representative global denominator. The resulting 100 issues must therefore be described as an evidence-ranked public shortlist, not as a statistical top 100. [raw/official--support-home-popular.md] [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-workflow.md]
- A future ranking refresh should use a redacted export of `{agency}` ticket tags and internal search terms as the primary frequency layer while retaining public evidence as a comparison set. The export must omit message bodies, credentials, payment data, authentication codes, and private customer content. [raw/incidents/2026-08-07-mcp-service-disruption.md] [raw/recent-help/stripe-payment-methods.md] [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-login.md]

## 4. White-label hard directive

Every customer-facing email must present `{agency}` as the provider. It must not name, cite, link to, or otherwise expose `help.gohighlevel.com`, `GoHighLevel`, `HighLevel`, `LeadConnector`, `LC Phone`, `LC Email`, the upstream status site, or another upstream platform identity unless the user explicitly supplies a contractual or legal reason for disclosure. Official source material is internal research only. [raw/recent-help/client-portal-setup.md] [raw/official--email-sending-warmup.md]

This is a content transformation, not an attribution exercise. The response should explain the verified steps directly in `{agency}` language, and may link only to an agency-owned help URL that the user has supplied. It must never substitute the upstream article URL as a convenience. [raw/recent-help/client-portal-setup.md] [raw/recent-help/api-v1-v2.md]

| Internal source concept | Customer-facing treatment |
|---|---|
| Upstream product, mail, phone, or status name | Use `your {agency} account`, `our platform`, `our email service`, `our messaging service`, or the exact neutral feature label visible in the customer's white-label interface. [raw/official--email-sending-warmup.md] [raw/recent-help/client-portal-setup.md] |
| Upstream help article | Paraphrase the applicable checks in the email. Do not cite or link the source. [raw/official--email-sending-warmup.md] |
| Historical upstream incident | Say that `{agency}` identified a service issue only when the current ticket overlaps verified incident evidence. Do not expose the upstream status page. [raw/incidents/2026-09-02-appointment-data-visibility.md] [raw/incidents/2026-08-31-application-asset-loading.md] |
| Escalation to an upstream team | Say `our technical team` or `our engineering team`, identify `{agency}` as the owner, and state the next update only when the commitment is real. [raw/support-writing--issuelinker-bug-replies.md] [raw/support-writing--intercom-email-auto-replies.md] |
| Upstream plan or internal service distinction | Explain the customer-visible entitlement or responsibility without exposing an internal service brand. For email, still distinguish the agency-managed sending path from customer-managed SMTP when that changes ownership. [raw/official--email-sending-warmup.md] |

Run the leak check across subject, preview text, greeting, body, signature, hyperlinks, visible URLs, image alt text, quoted history, forwarded text, filenames, and pasted screenshots. The portal and email records explicitly require upstream names, domains, and product identities to stay out of customer output. [raw/recent-help/client-portal-setup.md] [raw/official--email-sending-warmup.md]

## 5. Data-request prohibitions

The email must request only the minimum evidence needed to identify the account, affected object, time, and symptom. It must never use email to collect secrets or unrestricted private payloads. [raw/incidents/2026-08-07-mcp-service-disruption.md] [raw/recent-help/workflow-error-resolution.md]

| Never request in a ticket email | Request instead | Evidence basis |
|---|---|---|
| Password, one-time code, recovery code, or authentication challenge | Login email or user identifier, affected page or app, timestamp, exact redacted error, and whether a private-browser session reproduces the issue | Login searches cover password, email, app, page, dashboard, and mobile intents while explicitly prohibiting passwords and one-time codes. [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-login.md] |
| API key, private integration token, OAuth client secret, bearer token, authorization header, or webhook secret or signature | Endpoint and API version, authentication method name, operation, timestamp, correlation or request ID, and redacted error | Current API guidance and incident handling both prohibit secret collection and call for endpoint, version, operation, time, and redacted error evidence. [raw/recent-help/api-v1-v2.md] [raw/incidents/2026-08-07-mcp-service-disruption.md] |
| Full card number, security code, bank credential, or processor password | Subscription or invoice identifier, amount, currency, test or live mode, product surface, timestamp, and processor event or transaction identifier | Payment behavior varies by processor, country, currency, mode, recurring use, and surface, and official safety notes forbid full financial credentials. [raw/recent-help/stripe-payment-methods.md] [raw/incidents/2026-08-05-subscription-payment-failures.md] |
| Unredacted identity document in ordinary email | Registration, brand, campaign, or number identifier, rejection code, and a description of the requested evidence; use an agency-approved secure upload path if formal documents are truly required | A2P onboarding can involve sensitive business and identity material, and the phone-search record forbids requesting full identity documents. [raw/reddit/reddit--025--a2p-client-onboarding.md] [raw/autocomplete/gohighlevel-phone.md] |
| Another person's magic link or a magic link pasted into a shared ticket | Intended contact identity, destination, enrollment or access state, and whether the link is expired; regenerate only after identity and entitlement are verified | Magic links are recipient-specific, time-limited authentication and do not grant content permission. [raw/recent-help/course-magic-links.md] |
| Malicious attachment, active payload, or a complete private conversation transcript | Sender and message identifiers, timestamp, redacted screenshot, exact error, and the smallest safe excerpt needed to reproduce | Inbound spam evidence should not require forwarding malicious attachments, and diagnostic transcripts must remove private customer content and secrets. [raw/ideas--block-incoming-spam-email.md] [raw/incidents/2026-07-15-ai-agents-not-responding.md] |
| Full tool payload, customer dataset, contact export, or unredacted request body | Operation name, affected record IDs, timestamp, correlation ID, field names, counts, and a redacted minimal sample | MCP and API escalation guidance requires redacted identifiers and errors and prohibits full payloads containing private customer data. [raw/recent-help/call-quality.md] |

## 7. Support-writing structure is separate from troubleshooting truth

Support-writing research supplies the shape of a professional email: acknowledge the specific issue, state what is known, request only the missing evidence, identify the next owner and action, make only a supportable update commitment, and close after verification. It does not authorize a technical cause, workaround, refund, fix, or ETA. [raw/support-writing--fini-response-templates.md] [raw/support-writing--issuelinker-bug-replies.md] [raw/support-writing--kaltalk-ticket-responses.md]

Product troubleshooting research supplies the decision tree and safety boundaries. It does not determine tone, response-time promises, or the lifecycle state of the ticket. [raw/official--calendar-troubleshooting-tool.md] [raw/official--sms-delivery-troubleshooting.md] [raw/recent-help/workflow-error-resolution.md]

### Ticket lifecycle

| State | Email job | Required content and proof boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Acknowledged | Confirm receipt and ownership | Restate the customer-observable symptom, name `{agency}` as owner, provide the ticket reference, and distinguish receipt from resolution. State `{next_update_at}` only when the team can meet it; otherwise say the case is queued without inventing a time. [raw/support-writing--intercom-email-auto-replies.md] |
| 2. Triaged | Classify impact and risk | Identify affected account, surface, users or records, start time and timezone, whether the problem is ongoing, and whether it involves security, billing, legal, outage, data loss, or an irreversible action. [raw/recent-help/call-quality.md] [raw/incidents/2026-09-02-appointment-data-visibility.md] |
| 3. Needs information | Ask one consolidated, minimal evidence set | Explain why each requested item matters, prohibit secrets, and avoid asking the customer to repeat evidence already present. Use exact identifiers, timestamps, redacted errors, screenshots, and controlled examples. [raw/incidents/2026-08-07-mcp-service-disruption.md] [raw/recent-help/workflow-error-resolution.md] |
| 4. Investigating | Separate observations from hypotheses | State what has been reproduced, what has been ruled out, and what remains unknown. Do not call it a bug or outage, or claim an escalation, until the record proves that state. [raw/support-writing--issuelinker-bug-replies.md] [raw/reddit/reddit--052--workflow-step-delays.md] |
| 5. Incident confirmed | Communicate verified scope without exposing the upstream provider | State the affected `{agency}` service, observed impact, any safe temporary guidance, and the next update commitment. Do not reuse an old incident cause or restoration time for a new ticket. [raw/incidents/2026-09-01-multi-module-slowness.md] [raw/incidents/2026-08-25-isp-dns-data-loading.md] |
| 6. ### Professional email anatomy

1. Use a specific subject with `{ticket_id}` and the affected feature, not a vague `Following up`. [raw/support-writing--kaltalk-ticket-responses.md]
2. Open with the customer's actual symptom and business impact, not a generic apology. [raw/support-writing--fini-response-templates.md]
3. State the evidence status: observed, reproduced, incident-confirmed, documented configuration, suspected, or still unknown. [raw/support-writing--issuelinker-bug-replies.md]
4. Give one next action owner: customer, `{agent_name}`, `{agency}` technical team, billing authority, security authority, or an implementation specialist. [raw/support-writing--issuelinker-bug-replies.md]
5. Consolidate evidence requests into one short numbered list and explain any risky step before it is attempted. [raw/recent-help/workflow-error-resolution.md] [raw/recent-help/funnel-website-domain-dns.md]
6. State a next-update time only if supplied and achievable. An acknowledgment promise is not a resolution promise. [raw/support-writing--intercom-email-auto-replies.md]
