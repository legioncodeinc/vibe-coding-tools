# SR-042: Portal or course access fails

## Use when

Use when an invited or enrolled customer cannot sign in or access the expected course content.

## Required evidence

- Workspace, intended contact, portal page address, invitation state, course and offer, publication state, enrollment, access workflow, current permissions, browser comparison, and purchase history when applicable.

## Customer-facing email

**Subject:** {ticket_id}: Checking your portal or course access

Hi {customer_first_name},

I understand that access failed after the invitation or enrollment. We need to verify the intended identity, invitation, course publication, offer association, enrollment, and permissions before resending access or changing entitlement.

Please send the workspace, intended contact identifier, portal page address, invitation state, course and offer names, publication state, enrollment state, access workflow result, current permissions, and browser comparison. If access followed a purchase, include the invoice or purchase identifier and current payment state. Do not send a password, verification code, or another person's access link.

Please do not broaden permissions, recreate the purchase, or re-enroll the learner. I will check identity and entitlement history in order, then determine whether a verified resend or specialist review is appropriate.

{agent_name}
{agency} Support

## Agent notes

Evidence class `F2`. Remediation class `R1`. Apply `HR-11` and `HR-14`. Separate identity, invitation, publication, offer, enrollment, purchase, and permission state. Never share another contact's link or recreate entitlement blindly.
