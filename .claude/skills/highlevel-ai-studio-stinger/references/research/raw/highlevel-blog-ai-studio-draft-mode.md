# HighLevel has an AI Studio draft mode for safe editing

Updating a website or landing page often feels like a high-stakes gamble. For agencies, marketing teams and small businesses, the pressure to maintain an active online presence means that making creative changes usually comes with the underlying anxiety of accidentally breaking a live, high-converting asset. Fortunately, the HighLevel AI Studio draft mode completely eliminates this stress by allowing you to experiment with your designs safely and privately.

Instead of rushing to fix a broken live page or setting up complex external staging environments, you can leverage this built-in workspace to test bold ideas, refine layouts and update content with absolute peace of mind. Your visitors will continue to experience a seamless journey on your live site while you work behind the scenes to perfect your next big update.

## How to execute design updates with HighLevel AI Studio draft mode

Transforming your website layout or testing a fresh marketing angle is incredibly straightforward. By using the intuitive prompt system within the platform, you can guide visual changes without touching a single line of code. Here is how you can use the HighLevel AI Studio draft mode to revamp your assets smoothly:

1️⃣ **Access the platform workspace** – Log into your HighLevel sub account, look at the left hand navigation menu and select AI Studio to view your existing landing pages or websites.

2️⃣ **Input your creative prompts** – Locate the text field on the side panel and describe the specific transformations you want to see, such as changing theme colors from pink and purple to blue and purple.

3️⃣ **Evaluate your real time draft** – Watch as the system processes your commands and updates the design in a few seconds, generating a visual draft while leaving the original public page completely untouched.

4️⃣ **Publish your changes seamlessly** – Once you review the changes and ensure they look exactly right, click the publish button in the top right corner, confirm your choice and apply changes to take your new look live.

### Why the HighLevel AI Studio version history is essential for your team

Creative exploration requires room to fail and iterate. If an update goes live but fails to hit the mark, or if a client decides they prefer an older design variation, you need a quick way to pivot. This is where the robust tracking capabilities of the HighLevel AI Studio version history come into play to save your workflow.

✔️ **Total creative freedom** – Test radical new color schemes, messaging shifts or structured elements without worrying about accidental user friction or downtime.

✔️ **One click version restoration** – Open the history panel at any time to browse a visual timeline of your past outcomes, select the exact iteration you prefer and click restore to bring it back.

✔️ **Streamlined collaborative approvals** – Perfect your client landing pages in a controlled space, ensuring that your team only pushes updates live when the final product is completely polished and approved.

By separating your creative development workspace from your public customer experience, you unlock the ability to iterate faster, build higher-converting funnels and scale your business with absolute technical confidence.
